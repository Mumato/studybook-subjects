## Welcome to FIN401

This book follows the topics of **FIN401: Managerial Finance II** (corporate finance), in the order of the course schedule. Each chapter notes the matching chapter of *Fundamentals of Corporate Finance* (Berk, DeMarzo, Harford, Stangeland, Marosi), so you can use it next to your textbook.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking.

### Typing answers

- **Money:** type `1234.56` or `1,234.56` (a dollar sign in front is fine too). Answers are accepted if they're close: rounding to the cent or dollar is fine.
- **Rates:** type the percent, like `6.5` or `6.5%` for 6.5%.
- Keep a calculator (a financial one if you have it) next to you, just like in the exams.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test**, mostly multiple choice like the course exams. Score 80% to complete the chapter.

### The course

- Part 1: Review of FIN300 (time value of money, bonds, stocks)
- Part 2: Capital budgeting (NPV, IRR, free cash flow, CCA)
- Part 3: Cost of capital (WACC, CAPM)
- Part 4: Capital structure (Modigliani-Miller, taxes, distress, agency, pecking order)
- Part 5: Payout policy (dividends, buybacks, taxes, signals)
- Midterm practice: three practice exams covering Parts 1-5, multiple choice like the real midterm
- Part 6: Raising capital (venture capital, IPOs, rights offers, debt)
- Part 7: Options (calls, puts, put-call parity, convertibles)
- Part 8: Risk management (futures, marking to market, exchange rate hedging)
- Part 9: Mergers and acquisitions (synergies, premiums, exchange ratios, defences)
- Part 10: Corporate governance (boards, executive pay, control, regulation)
- Final practice: three cumulative practice exams, multiple choice like the real final
