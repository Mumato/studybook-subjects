---
id: gov-01
title: Owners, managers and the board
---
In large firms, shareholders own the company but managers run it. Corporate governance is the set of controls that keeps managers working for the owners. (Berk ch. 25)

::: rule
- **Separation of ownership and control** creates an **agency problem**: managers may pursue perks, empire building or a quiet life instead of shareholder value.
- Governance balances the **costs** and **benefits** of monitoring: perfect control is too expensive.
- The **board of directors** hires, pays and can fire the CEO.
- **Inside** directors are employees; **gray** directors aren't but have ties (lawyers, suppliers, the CEO's friends); **independent** directors have no ties to the firm.
- A board dominated by friends of the CEO is **captured**; independent boards monitor better.
:::

::: example
A CEO buys a corporate jet for personal trips. Shareholders bear most of the cost while the CEO enjoys the benefit: an agency cost.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
The agency problem in corporations comes from:
- [x] the separation of ownership and control
- [ ] the use of debt
- [ ] paying dividends
solution: Owners and managers are different people with different interests.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
A director who is the firm's outside lawyer is:
- [ ] an inside director
- [x] a gray director
- [ ] an independent director
solution: Not an employee, but with business ties to the firm.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
A "captured" board is one that:
- [x] looks after the CEO's interests rather than the shareholders'
- [ ] has been taken over by a raider
- [ ] has only independent directors
solution: Captured boards rubber-stamp management.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Which is an example of an agency cost?
- [ ] paying interest on debt
- [x] a CEO building a costly headquarters to boost personal prestige
- [ ] paying suppliers on time
solution: Spending shareholders' money for the manager's benefit.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
The main job of the board of directors is to:
- [ ] run daily operations
- [x] monitor management, including hiring and firing the CEO
- [ ] sell the firm's products
solution: The board represents shareholders and oversees management.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Why don't shareholders monitor managers perfectly?
- [x] monitoring is costly, and each small shareholder bears the cost while all share the benefit
- [ ] it's illegal
- [ ] managers always act in shareholders' interests
solution: Small shareholders free-ride on others' monitoring, so too little happens.
:::
