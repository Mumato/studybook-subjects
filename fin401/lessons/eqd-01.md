id: eqd-01
title: Private equity and the IPO
---
Young firms raise equity privately; later they may sell shares to the public in an **initial public offering** (IPO). (Berk ch. 14)

::: rule
- **Private sources:** angel investors, **venture capital** (VC) firms, private equity funds, institutional investors.
- In a VC round: **post-money valuation** = investment ÷ ownership bought; **pre-money** = post-money − investment.
- **IPO:** an **underwriter** (investment bank) helps set the price, often by **book building** (collecting orders from big investors), and is paid a **spread** (a % of the offer price).
- **Underpricing:** IPO shares tend to be priced below what they trade for on day one. First-day return = (first close − offer) ÷ offer. The gain to the first buyers is **money left on the table** for the firm.
:::

::: example
A VC invests \$2 million for 25% of a start-up: post-money value $2 / 0.25 = 8$ million, pre-money \$6 million.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="8" tolerance=0.01
A VC invests \$2 million for 25% of a firm. What is the post-money valuation, in millions?
solution: $2 / 0.25 = 8$ million.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="6" tolerance=0.01
What was that firm's pre-money valuation, in millions?
solution: $8 - 2 = 6$ million.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="20" tolerance=0.01
An IPO is priced at \$20 and closes its first day at \$24. What is the first-day return? Type the percent.
solution: $(24 - 20) / 20 = 20\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="40" tolerance=0.01
That IPO sold 10 million shares. How much money did the firm leave on the table, in millions?
solution: $10 \times (24 - 20) = 40$ million.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="18.6" tolerance=0.01
The underwriter's spread is 7% of the \$20 offer price. How much does the firm receive per share?
solution: $20 \times (1 - 0.07) = 18.60$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
In book building, the underwriter:
- [x] collects orders from large investors to set the offer price
- [ ] sets the price by auction on the stock exchange
- [ ] buys all the shares at the first-day closing price
solution: The order book shows how much demand there is at each price.
:::
