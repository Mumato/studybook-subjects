::: problem id=t1 type=choice
A \$50,000 car loan at 6% APR (compounded monthly) is repaid monthly over 5 years. The monthly payment is closest to:
- [x] \$966.64
- [ ] \$833.33
- [ ] \$1,186.98
- [ ] \$250.00
solution: Monthly rate 0.5%, 60 payments: $\frac{50000 \times 0.005}{1 - 1.005^{-60}} = 966.64$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
Which bond's price falls the most if yields rise by 1%?
- [ ] a 2-year bond with a 9% coupon
- [ ] a 10-year bond with a 9% coupon
- [x] a 10-year zero-coupon bond
- [ ] a 1-year zero-coupon bond
solution: Longest maturity and no coupons: the most interest rate risk.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
A firm retains 50% of its earnings and earns 12% on new investment. Its next dividend is \$2 and $r_E = 10\%$. Its share price is:
- [ ] \$20.00
- [ ] \$33.33
- [x] \$50.00
- [ ] \$100.00
solution: $g = 0.5 \times 12\% = 6\%$; $P = 2 / (0.10 - 0.06) = 50$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
An \$80,000 asset has a 25% CCA rate; the tax rate is 26% and the cost of capital 9% (half-year rule). The PV of its CCA tax shield is closest to:
- [ ] \$20,800
- [x] \$14,663
- [ ] \$15,294
- [ ] \$5,200
solution: $\frac{80000 \times 0.25 \times 0.26}{0.34} \times \frac{1.045}{1.09} = 14662.71$. (\$15,294 forgets the half-year factor.)
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
Comparable firms are 60% equity (beta 1.6) and 40% debt (beta 0.1). The risk-free rate is 3% and the market risk premium 6%. The project's cost of capital is:
- [x] 9.0%
- [ ] 12.6%
- [ ] 8.4%
- [ ] 3.6%
solution: $\beta_U = 0.6 \times 1.6 + 0.4 \times 0.1 = 1.0$; $3 + 1.0 \times 6 = 9\%$. (12.6% uses the equity beta.)
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
A firm close to bankruptcy turns down a positive-NPV project because most of the gain would go to its lenders. This is:
- [ ] asset substitution
- [x] debt overhang
- [ ] the pecking order
- [ ] a tax shield
solution: Debt overhang: under-investment caused by existing debt.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A firm announces a large new share issue. Asymmetric information predicts its share price will:
- [ ] rise
- [x] fall
- [ ] be unaffected
solution: Investors suspect managers sell shares when they believe the shares are overvalued.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Dividends are taxed at 35% and capital gains at 15%. The effective dividend tax rate is closest to:
- [ ] 20.00%
- [x] 23.53%
- [ ] 35.00%
- [ ] 15.00%
solution: $(0.35 - 0.15) / 0.85 = 23.53\%$.
:::
