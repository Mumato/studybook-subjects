::: problem id=t1 type=choice
Which director is most likely to monitor the CEO independently?
- [ ] the CFO
- [ ] the firm's main banker
- [x] a retired executive with no ties to the firm
- [ ] the CEO's college roommate
solution: Independent directors have no ties to the firm or its managers.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="20" tolerance=0.01
A family owns 40% of firm A, which owns 50% of firm B. What percent of B's cash flows does the family own?
solution: $0.4 \times 0.5 = 20\%$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
Stock-based pay aims mainly to:
- [x] align managers' interests with shareholders'
- [ ] lower the firm's taxes
- [ ] reduce the share price
solution: Managers who own shares gain when shareholders gain.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
Which makes managers **more** entrenched?
- [ ] an independent board
- [x] a staggered board and a poison pill
- [ ] an active takeover market
- [ ] say on pay
solution: Takeover defences protect managers from being replaced.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
A large shareholder campaigns to replace directors at the annual meeting. This is a:
- [x] proxy fight
- [ ] tender offer
- [ ] poison pill
solution: A proxy fight is a contest for shareholders' votes.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
The agency problem between managers and shareholders is **smallest** when:
- [x] the CEO owns a large part of the firm
- [ ] ownership is spread among millions of small shareholders
- [ ] the board is captured
solution: An owner-manager bears much of the cost of any waste.
:::
