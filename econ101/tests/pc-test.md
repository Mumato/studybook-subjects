::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="4"
The price is \$25. The marginal cost of the 1st to 5th units is \$10, \$15, \$20, \$25, \$32. How many units should the firm make?
solution: Units 1 to 4 cost no more than \$25; the 5th would lose \$7.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="800"
The price is \$30. The firm makes 200 units at an ATC of \$26. What is its profit?
solution: $(30 - 26) \times 200 = \$800$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
The price is \$9 and the firm's AVC is \$10. In the short run it should:
- [ ] keep producing
- [x] shut down
- [ ] raise its price
solution: $P < AVC$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="10"
A firm's marginal cost is $MC = 4Q$ and the price is \$40. What output maximizes profit?
solution: $40 = 4Q$, so $Q = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
In long-run equilibrium, a perfectly competitive firm earns:
- [ ] large economic profits
- [x] zero economic profit
- [ ] losses
- [ ] zero accounting profit
solution: Entry and exit drive the price to min ATC.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="6"
A firm's AVC is lowest at \$6 and its ATC lowest at \$9. Below what price does it shut down?
solution: The minimum AVC, \$6.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
The demand curve one perfectly competitive firm faces is:
- [x] horizontal at the market price
- [ ] the market demand curve
- [ ] vertical
- [ ] downward sloping
solution: It can sell any amount at the market price and nothing above it.
:::
