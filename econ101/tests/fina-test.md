::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="20"
$Q_d = 100 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 3P$. What is the equilibrium price?
solution: $100 - 2P = 3P$, so $P = 20$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="1.2" tolerance=0.01
The price rises from \$10 to \$14 and the quantity demanded falls from 30 to 20. Using the midpoint method, what is $E_d$?
solution: $\%\Delta Q = 10/25 = 40\%$, $\%\Delta P = 4/12 = 33.3\%$. $E_d = 40/33.3 = 1.2$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="4"
A competitive firm faces a price of \$18. The marginal cost of the 1st to 5th units is \$6, \$10, \$14, \$18, \$24. How many units should it make?
solution: Units 1 to 4 cost no more than \$18; the 5th would lose \$6.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
In the short run a competitive firm should shut down when the price is below:
- [ ] average total cost
- [x] average variable cost
- [ ] marginal revenue
- [ ] average fixed cost
solution: Below AVC, producing loses more than the fixed costs.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="40"
A monopoly faces demand $P = 60 - Q$ and $MC = 20$. What price does it charge?
solution: $MR = 60 - 2Q = 20$ gives $Q = 20$. $P = 60 - 20 = 40$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="3750"
Three firms have shares of 50%, 25% and 25%. What is the HHI?
solution: $2500 + 625 + 625 = 3750$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A factory's pollution harms people downstream. The best per-unit tax to fix this equals:
- [x] the external cost per unit
- [ ] the factory's profit per unit
- [ ] the price of the good
- [ ] the factory's marginal cost
solution: A Pigouvian tax makes the factory count the damage it causes.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="2"
Output sells for \$20. The 1st to 4th workers' marginal products are 9, 7, 5 and 3. The wage is \$120. How many workers are hired?
solution: MRPs: 180, 140, 100, 60. Two are worth at least \$120.
:::
