::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="2"
In a day, Rae picks 20 apples or 10 pears. How many apples does one pear cost her?
solution: $20 / 10 = 2$ apples.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="800"
Demand is $P = 60 - Q$ and the market price is \$20. What is the consumer surplus?
solution: $Q = 40$. Consumer surplus $= \frac{1}{2} \times 40 \times (60 - 20) = 800$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
Coffee gets more expensive and people buy more tea. The cross-price elasticity of tea with respect to coffee's price is:
- [x] positive: they're substitutes
- [ ] negative: they're complements
- [ ] zero
solution: Higher coffee prices raise tea purchases.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="30000"
Revenue is \$500,000, explicit costs \$320,000 and implicit costs \$150,000. What is the economic profit?
solution: $500000 - 320000 - 150000 = \$30{,}000$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="12"
In a perfectly competitive industry, every firm's ATC is lowest at \$12. What is the long-run price?
solution: Entry and exit push the price to min ATC: \$12.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="2"
Payoffs (A, B): both cut prices (2, 2); only A cuts (7, 0); only B cuts (0, 7); neither cuts (5, 5). What does each firm earn in the Nash equilibrium?
solution: Cutting is dominant for each (7 > 5 and 2 > 0), so both cut and earn 2.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Free-to-air radio broadcasts are a:
- [ ] private good
- [ ] club good
- [ ] common resource
- [x] public good
solution: Anyone can tune in (not excludable), and one listener doesn't use it up (not rival).
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="100"
Labour demand is $L_d = 500 - 10W$ and supply is $L_s = 15W$. A minimum wage of \$24 is set. How many people want work but can't find it?
solution: Equilibrium is \$20. At \$24: $L_d = 260$ and $L_s = 360$. Unemployment $= 100$.
:::
