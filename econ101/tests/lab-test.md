::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="150"
A worker's marginal product is 6 units and output sells for \$25. What is their MRP, in dollars?
solution: $6 \times 25 = \$150$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="3"
Output sells for \$10. The 1st to 4th workers' marginal products are 10, 8, 6 and 4. The wage is \$55. How many workers are hired?
solution: MRPs: 100, 80, 60, 40. Three are worth at least \$55.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="20"
Labour demand is $L_d = 900 - 30W$ and supply is $L_s = 15W$. What is the equilibrium wage?
solution: $900 - 30W = 15W$, so $W = 20$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="180"
Same market. A minimum wage of \$24 is set. How many people want work but can't find it?
solution: $L_d = 900 - 720 = 180$ and $L_s = 360$. Unemployment $= 180$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
A monopsony is a market with:
- [ ] one seller
- [x] one buyer, such as the only employer in town
- [ ] a few sellers
- [ ] many buyers and sellers
solution: As the only buyer of labour, it can pay less than a competitive wage.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="30"
Quintile shares are 5%, 10%, 15%, 25% and 45%. What share goes to the poorest 60%? Type the percent.
solution: $5 + 10 + 15 = 30\%$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A Gini coefficient closer to 1 means:
- [ ] more equal incomes
- [x] more unequal incomes
- [ ] higher average income
- [ ] lower taxes
solution: 0 is perfect equality; 1 is one household earning everything.
:::
