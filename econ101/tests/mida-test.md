::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="82"
You skip a 4-hour shift paying \$18 an hour to go to a free event, and the bus there and back costs \$10. What is the opportunity cost, in dollars?
solution: $4 \times 18 + 10 = 72 + 10 = \$82$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="20"
$Q_d = 120 - 4P$ and $Q_s = 2P$. What is the equilibrium price?
solution: $120 - 4P = 2P$, so $P = 20$ (and $Q = 40$).
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
A price ceiling set below the equilibrium price causes:
- [x] a shortage
- [ ] a surplus
- [ ] no change
- [ ] a higher market price
solution: At the lower price buyers want more and sellers offer less.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="1.25" tolerance=0.01
The price rises from \$4 to \$6 and the quantity demanded falls from 50 to 30. Using the midpoint method, what is $E_d$?
solution: $\%\Delta Q = 20/40 = 50\%$, $\%\Delta P = 2/5 = 40\%$. $E_d = 50/40 = 1.25$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
When the price of good Y rises, people buy less of good X. X and Y are:
- [ ] substitutes
- [x] complements
- [ ] inferior goods
- [ ] unrelated
solution: A negative cross-price elasticity: they're used together.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="15"
At your best mix, $MU_X = 20$, $P_X = 4$ and $P_Y = 3$. What is $MU_Y$?
solution: $20/4 = 5$ per dollar, so $MU_Y = 5 \times 3 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="-5000"
A shop's revenue is \$90,000 and its explicit costs are \$50,000. The owner gave up a \$45,000 job. What is the economic profit?
solution: $90000 - 50000 - 45000 = -\$5{,}000$.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="20"
Fixed costs are \$300 and variable costs are \$900 at 60 units. What is the ATC?
solution: $(300 + 900) / 60 = 20$.
:::
