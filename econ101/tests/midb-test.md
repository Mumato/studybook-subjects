::: problem id=t1 type=choice
In a day, Al makes 6 loaves or 3 bottles of juice; Bea makes 4 loaves or 4 bottles. Who has the comparative advantage in juice?
- [ ] Al
- [x] Bea
- [ ] Neither
solution: A bottle costs Al $6/3 = 2$ loaves and Bea $4/4 = 1$ loaf.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="12"
$Q_s = 5P - 10$. Demand rises from $Q_d = 50 - P$ to $Q_d = 62 - P$. What is the new equilibrium price?
solution: $62 - P = 5P - 10$, so $72 = 6P$ and $P = 12$ (up from 10).
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="450"
Demand is $P = 40 - Q$ and the market price is \$10. What is the consumer surplus?
solution: $Q = 30$. Consumer surplus $= \frac{1}{2} \times 30 \times (40 - 10) = 450$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
Demand is inelastic. A price rise makes total revenue:
- [x] rise
- [ ] fall
- [ ] stay the same
solution: The quantity falls by a smaller percent than the price rises.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="-0.5" tolerance=0.01
Incomes rise 8% and purchases of instant noodles fall 4%. What is the income elasticity?
solution: $-4/8 = -0.5$: an inferior good.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
Chicken gets cheaper, and you eat more chicken and less beef. The switch away from beef is the:
- [x] substitution effect
- [ ] income effect
- [ ] law of supply
- [ ] diminishing marginal utility
solution: Chicken is now cheaper compared with beef.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="7"
3 workers produce 45 units and 4 workers produce 52. What is the marginal product of the 4th worker?
solution: $52 - 45 = 7$.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="25"
Total cost is \$320 at 8 units and \$345 at 9 units. What is the marginal cost of the 9th unit?
solution: $345 - 320 = 25$.
:::
