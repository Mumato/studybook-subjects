::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="9"
Total utility: 1 unit 15, 2 units 27, 3 units 36, 4 units 42. What is the marginal utility of the 3rd unit?
solution: $36 - 27 = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
The marginal utilities above (15, 12, 9, 6) show:
- [x] diminishing marginal utility
- [ ] falling total utility
- [ ] increasing marginal utility
- [ ] negative marginal utility
solution: Each unit adds less than the one before, while the total still rises.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="5"
A good costs \$8 and its marginal utility is 40. What is its marginal utility per dollar?
solution: $40/8 = 5$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
$MU_X / P_X = 5$ and $MU_Y / P_Y = 7$. You should:
- [ ] buy more X and less Y
- [x] buy more Y and less X
- [ ] keep your current mix
solution: A dollar moved to Y gains 7 and loses 5.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="20"
At your best mix, $P_X = 4$, $P_Y = 2$ and $MU_Y = 10$. What is $MU_X$?
solution: $10/2 = 5$ per dollar, so $MU_X = 5 \times 4 = 20$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
An inferior good's price falls. The income effect alone makes you buy:
- [ ] more of it
- [x] less of it
- [ ] the same amount
solution: Feeling richer, you buy less of an inferior good.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="10"
You take 25 bus rides a month at \$2.00. The fare drops to \$1.60. How many dollars a month do you save on the same rides?
solution: $25 \times 0.40 = \$10$.
:::
