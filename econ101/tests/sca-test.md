::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="55"
You take a 2-hour lunch instead of working at \$20 an hour. The lunch itself costs \$15. What is its opportunity cost, in dollars?
solution: $15 + 2 \times 20 = \$55$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
Opportunity cost is:
- [ ] the money price of a good
- [x] the value of the next best alternative given up
- [ ] the total value of all the alternatives given up
- [ ] the cost already paid that can't be recovered
solution: Only the next best alternative counts. The last option describes a sunk cost.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="15"
You have \$90. Good A costs \$9 and good B costs \$6. What's the most of good B you can buy?
solution: $90 / 6 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="1.5"
Same prices: how many units of B does one unit of A cost you?
solution: $9 / 6 = 1.5$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
An economy with many unemployed workers produces at a point:
- [x] inside its PPF
- [ ] on its PPF
- [ ] outside its PPF
- [ ] that shifts the PPF outward
solution: Idle resources mean it could make more of both goods: a point inside the frontier.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="1.5"
An economy moves along its PPF from 10 cars and 50 computers to 20 cars and 35 computers. How many computers does each extra car cost?
solution: $(50 - 35) / (20 - 10) = 15 / 10 = 1.5$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Which statement is **normative**?
- [ ] Higher gas taxes reduce driving.
- [x] The city should ban cars downtown.
- [ ] Rents rose 5% last year.
- [ ] Unemployment is higher among young workers.
solution: "Should" is an opinion about what ought to be; the others can be checked with data.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A firm spent \$2 million on research for a product. Finishing it would cost \$1 million more and bring in \$1.5 million. The firm should:
- [x] finish it: the \$2 million is sunk, and \$1.5 million beats \$1 million
- [ ] stop: total cost \$3 million is more than \$1.5 million
- [ ] stop: it can never earn back the \$2 million
- [ ] spend more on research first
solution: Only future costs and benefits matter. Finishing adds $1.5 - 1 = \$0.5$ million.
:::
