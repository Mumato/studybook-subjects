id: lab-03
title: Poverty and inequality
---
Markets reward people by what they bring to the market, which can leave large gaps in income. How do we measure those gaps, and what can be done? (OpenStax ch. 15)

::: rule
- **Income quintiles:** split households into five equal groups from poorest to richest and compare each group's share of total income.
- The **Lorenz curve** plots the share of income the poorest x% receive. The further it bows from the straight line of perfect equality, the more unequal.
- The **Gini coefficient** runs from 0 (everyone earns the same) to 1 (one household earns everything).
- **Progressive taxes** take a higher share from higher incomes. Benefits that shrink as you earn more can trap people: losing \$0.50 of benefits per \$1 earned works like a 50% tax.
:::

::: example
Quintile shares of 5%, 10%, 15%, 25% and 45%: the richest fifth receives 45% of all income, nine times the poorest fifth's 5%.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="13"
Quintile shares are 4%, 9%, 15%, 23% and 49%. What share of income goes to the poorest 40% of households? Type the percent.
solution: The two bottom quintiles: $4 + 9 = 13\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="12.25" tolerance=0.01
Same shares (4%, 9%, 15%, 23%, 49%). How many times the poorest fifth's share does the richest fifth receive?
solution: $49 / 4 = 12.25$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="0"
What is the Gini coefficient of a society where every household earns the same?
solution: 0: perfect equality.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Country A's Lorenz curve bows much further from the equality line than country B's. So:
- [x] A's income is more unequal
- [ ] B's income is more unequal
- [ ] A is richer
- [ ] B is richer
solution: The Lorenz curve shows how income is shared, not how much there is.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="60"
A worker on benefits earns \$1,000 more and loses \$600 of benefits. What is the effective tax rate on those earnings? Type the percent.
solution: $600 / 1000 = 60\%$: they keep only \$400 of the \$1,000.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="9000"
A tax takes 10% of income up to \$40,000 and 25% of every dollar above that. How much tax is paid on \$60,000?
solution: $0.10 \times 40000 + 0.25 \times 20000 = 4000 + 5000 = \$9{,}000$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
A **progressive** tax is one where:
- [x] higher incomes pay a larger share of their income
- [ ] everyone pays the same amount
- [ ] everyone pays the same share
- [ ] lower incomes pay a larger share
solution: In the last problem, \$60,000 pays 15% overall, while \$40,000 pays 10%.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="20000" creative=true
A benefit pays \$10,000 a year, reduced by \$0.50 for every dollar the person earns. At what yearly earnings does the benefit reach zero?
solution: $10000 / 0.50 = \$20{,}000$. Up to then, each dollar earned adds only 50 cents to their income.
:::
