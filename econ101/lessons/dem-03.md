id: dem-03
title: Market equilibrium
---
Put buyers and sellers together and the price moves until the amount wanted matches the amount offered.

::: rule
At the **equilibrium** price, $Q_d = Q_s$. Set the two equal, solve for $P$, then put $P$ back in to find $Q$.
- Above equilibrium there's a **surplus** ($Q_s > Q_d$): sellers cut prices.
- Below equilibrium there's a **shortage** ($Q_d > Q_s$): buyers bid prices up.
:::

::: example
$Q_d = 100 - 5P$ and $Q_s = 10P - 20$.
$$100 - 5P = 10P - 20 \Rightarrow 120 = 15P \Rightarrow P = 8$$
Then $Q = 100 - 40 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="15"
$Q_d = 60 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 3P - 15$. What is the equilibrium price?
solution: $60 - 2P = 3P - 15$, so $75 = 5P$ and $P = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="30"
$Q_d = 60 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 3P - 15$. What is the equilibrium quantity?
solution: $P = 15$, so $Q = 60 - 30 = 30$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="25"
$Q_d = 60 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 3P - 15$. At $P = 20$, how big is the surplus?
solution: $Q_d = 20$, $Q_s = 45$. Surplus $= 45 - 20 = 25$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="15"
$Q_d = 60 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 3P - 15$. At $P = 12$, how big is the shortage?
solution: $Q_d = 36$, $Q_s = 21$. Shortage $= 36 - 21 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
At a price below equilibrium:
- [x] there's a shortage, and the price tends to rise
- [ ] there's a surplus, and the price tends to fall
- [ ] there's a shortage, and the price tends to fall
- [ ] the market is in equilibrium
solution: Buyers want more than sellers offer, so they bid the price up.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="20"
$Q_d = 200 - 4P$ and $Q_s = 6P$. What is the equilibrium price?
solution: $200 - 4P = 6P$, so $P = 20$ (and $Q = 120$).
:::
