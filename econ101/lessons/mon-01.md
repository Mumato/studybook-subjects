title: What makes a monopoly
---
A monopoly is the only seller of a good with no close substitutes. It can stay that way only if something keeps rivals out. (OpenStax ch. 9)

::: rule
A **monopoly** is a single seller protected by **barriers to entry**:
- **Natural monopoly:** economies of scale so large that one firm can supply the whole market most cheaply (water pipes, power lines).
- Control of a key **resource**.
- **Legal barriers:** patents, copyrights, government licences.
- **Network effects:** a product is worth more the more people use it.
A monopoly is a **price maker**: it faces the whole downward-sloping market demand curve, so selling more means charging less.
:::

::: example
A water company has fixed costs of \$1,000,000 and each unit costs \$2. Serving 100,000 homes costs \$12 each; serving 500,000 costs \$4 each. One big network is far cheaper than two.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
A drug company holds a 20-year patent on a new medicine. This is a:
- [x] legal barrier to entry
- [ ] natural monopoly
- [ ] network effect
- [ ] sign of perfect competition
solution: The law stops others from making the drug.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
A natural monopoly is a market where:
- [x] one firm can supply the whole market at a lower cost than several firms
- [ ] the government owns the only firm
- [ ] a firm controls a natural resource
- [ ] the product occurs in nature
solution: Huge fixed costs (laying pipes) make one network cheapest.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
The demand curve a monopoly faces is:
- [ ] horizontal at the market price
- [x] the downward-sloping market demand curve
- [ ] vertical
- [ ] upward sloping
solution: It's the only seller, so the market's buyers are its buyers.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Which is the clearest example of **network effects**?
- [x] A social media app that's more useful the more friends use it
- [ ] A farm that owns the best land
- [ ] A company with a patent
- [ ] A city bus licence
solution: Each new user makes it more valuable to every other user, which keeps rivals out.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="12"
A water company has fixed costs of \$1,000,000 and a cost of \$2 per unit. What is its ATC at 100,000 units?
solution: $\frac{1000000}{100000} + 2 = 10 + 2 = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="4"
Same company (FC \$1,000,000, \$2 per unit). What is its ATC at 500,000 units?
solution: $\frac{1000000}{500000} + 2 = 2 + 2 = 4$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Which is **not** a barrier to entry?
- [ ] A patent
- [ ] Owning the only supply of a mineral
- [x] Many firms selling identical goods
- [ ] A government licence
solution: Many sellers of identical goods is perfect competition: there's nothing keeping new firms out.
:::
