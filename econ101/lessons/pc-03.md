id: pc-03
title: Entry, exit and the long run
---
Profits don't last in a competitive market. New firms come in and push the price down until the extra profit is gone.

::: rule
- **Economic profit** attracts new firms: supply shifts right and the price falls.
- **Losses** make firms leave: supply shifts left and the price rises.
- In the **long run**: $P = MC = \text{min } ATC$ and economic profit is **zero**.
- This is **productively efficient** (made at the lowest cost per unit) and **allocatively efficient** ($P = MC$: the value to buyers equals the cost of the last unit).
:::

::: example
The price is \$20 and firms' lowest ATC is \$15. They're making profits, so firms enter until the price falls to \$15.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="15"
Every firm's ATC is lowest at \$15. What is the long-run price?
solution: Entry and exit push the price to min ATC: \$15.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Firms in a competitive market are earning economic profits. Over time:
- [x] firms enter and the price falls
- [ ] firms leave and the price rises
- [ ] nothing changes
- [ ] the price rises as demand grows
solution: Profits attract entry; more supply lowers the price.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Firms in a competitive market are losing money. Over time:
- [ ] more firms enter
- [x] firms leave and the price rises
- [ ] the price falls further
- [ ] all firms shut down for good
solution: Exit shifts supply left until the remaining firms break even.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="0"
What is a perfectly competitive firm's economic profit in long-run equilibrium?
solution: Zero. It still earns a normal accounting profit.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
**Allocative efficiency** means:
- [x] price equals marginal cost
- [ ] output is made at the lowest ATC
- [ ] every firm earns a profit
- [ ] the government sets the price
solution: The last unit costs exactly what buyers value it at: no trade that would help both sides is missed.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
**Productive efficiency** means:
- [ ] price equals marginal cost
- [x] goods are made at the lowest possible ATC
- [ ] firms make the most profit
- [ ] the market has many firms
solution: No resources are wasted making each unit.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="14"
Market demand is $Q = 1000 - 20P$. In the long run each firm makes 50 units at its lowest ATC of \$15. How many firms are in the market?
solution: Price \$15, so $Q = 1000 - 300 = 700$. Firms: $700 / 50 = 14$.
:::
