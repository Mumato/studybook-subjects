---
id: pc-02
title: Losses and shutting down
---
When the price drops below average cost, the firm loses money. Should it keep producing or close for now?

::: rule
In the short run, fixed costs must be paid even at zero output.
- If $P \ge AVC$: **keep producing**. Revenue covers variable costs and some of the fixed costs, so the loss is smaller than FC.
- If $P < AVC$: **shut down**. The loss is then just FC.
- Below min ATC the firm loses money (**break-even price** = min ATC); below min AVC it shuts down (**shutdown price** = min AVC).
- The firm's short-run supply curve is its MC curve above min AVC.
:::

::: example
$P = 8$, $Q = 100$, $ATC = 10$, $AVC = 6$. Producing: loss $= (10 - 8) \times 100 = 200$. Shutting down: loss $= FC = (10 - 6) \times 100 = 400$. Keep producing.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
The price is \$8, AVC is \$6 and ATC is \$10. The firm should:
- [x] keep producing in the short run
- [ ] shut down
- [ ] raise its price
solution: $P > AVC$: each unit covers its variable cost and helps pay the fixed costs.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="200"
$P = 8$, $ATC = 10$, $Q = 100$. How big is the loss if the firm keeps producing?
solution: $(10 - 8) \times 100 = 200$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="400"
$ATC = 10$ and $AVC = 6$ at $Q = 100$. What are the fixed costs (the loss if it shuts down)?
solution: $AFC = 10 - 6 = 4$, so $FC = 4 \times 100 = 400$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
The price is \$5, AVC is \$6 and ATC is \$9. The firm should:
- [ ] keep producing
- [x] shut down
- [ ] produce more to spread its fixed costs
solution: $P < AVC$: each unit loses money even before fixed costs.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="150"
At $Q = 50$, ATC is \$9 and AVC is \$6. If the firm shuts down, how big is its loss?
solution: $FC = (9 - 6) \times 50 = 150$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="7"
A firm's AVC is lowest at \$7 and its ATC is lowest at \$11. Below what price does it shut down?
solution: The shutdown price is the minimum AVC: \$7.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="11"
Same firm (min AVC \$7, min ATC \$11). Below what price does it make a loss?
solution: The break-even price is the minimum ATC: \$11. Between \$7 and \$11 it loses money but keeps producing.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="80" creative=true
The price is \$12. At its best output of 40 units, a firm's ATC is \$14 and AVC is \$10. By how many dollars is it better off producing than shutting down?
solution: Producing: loss $(14 - 12) \times 40 = 80$. Shutting down: loss $FC = (14 - 10) \times 40 = 160$. Producing is better by $160 - 80 = \$80$.
:::
