## Welcome to ECON101

This book covers a first university course in **Principles of Microeconomics**: how people, firms and markets make choices when there isn't enough of everything. You don't need any background: if you can add, multiply and divide, you can start. Each chapter notes the matching chapter of the free OpenStax textbook *Principles of Microeconomics*, if you want to read more.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**, or click an option. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking.
- Some problems show a graph: click the point the question asks for, then press **Check**.

### Typing answers

- **Money:** type `12.50` or `$12.50`. **Percents:** type the number, like `25` or `25%` for 25%.
- **Elasticities and ratios:** type a decimal like `1.5` or a fraction like `3/2`.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test**. Score 80% to complete the chapter.

### The course

- Part 1: Thinking like an economist (scarcity, opportunity cost, trade)
- Part 2: Supply and demand (markets, equilibrium, price controls, surplus)
- Part 3: Elasticity
- Part 4: Consumer choice (utility)
- Part 5: Production and costs
- Midterm practice: two practice exams on Parts 1-5
- Part 6: Perfect competition
- Part 7: Monopoly
- Part 8: Monopolistic competition and oligopoly (game theory)
- Part 9: Market failure (externalities, public goods, information)
- Part 10: Labour markets and inequality
- Final practice: two cumulative practice exams
