title: Radians
---
::: rule
**Radians** measure angles by arc length on a circle of radius 1. A full turn is $2\pi$ radians, so

$$180° = \pi \text{ radians}$$

- degrees to radians: multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$
- radians to degrees: multiply by $\frac{180}{\pi}$

Arc length on a circle of radius r: $s = r\theta$ (θ in radians).
:::

::: example
$60° = 60 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{\pi}{3}$.
:::

::: example
$\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{180}{4} = 45°$.
:::

Type π as pi, like pi/3.

::: problem id=x9 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra
An arc of length 12 lies on a circle of radius 4. What is the angle in radians?
solution: $\theta = \frac{s}{r} = \frac{12}{4} = 3$.

::: problem id=x10 type=numeric answer="4pi" set=extra
A circle has radius 10. How long is the arc for an angle of 72°?
hint: Change 72° to radians first.
solution: $72° = \frac{2\pi}{5}$. $s = 10 \cdot \frac{2\pi}{5} = 4\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
An arc of length $6\pi$ has angle $\frac{3\pi}{4}$. What is the radius?
solution: $r = \frac{s}{\theta} = 6\pi \cdot \frac{4}{3\pi} = 8$.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=choice set=extra

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="2pi/3" set=extra
Through what angle (in radians) does a clock's minute hand turn in 20 minutes?
solution: 20 minutes is $\frac{1}{3}$ of a turn: $\frac{2\pi}{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="pi/3" set=extra
The Earth turns once in 24 hours. Through what angle (radians) does it turn in 4 hours?
solution: $\frac{4}{24} \cdot 2\pi = \frac{\pi}{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="9pi/4" set=extra

:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="6pi" set=extra
A bike wheel has radius 0.3 m. How far does the bike roll in 10 full turns of the wheel?
hint: One turn is $2\pi$ radians.
solution: $s = r\theta = 0.3 \cdot 10 \cdot 2\pi = 6\pi \approx 18.85$ m.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="200" set=extra
A wheel of radius 0.5 m rolls 100 m. Through how many radians does it turn?
hint: Use $\theta = \frac{s}{r}$.
solution: $\theta = \frac{100}{0.5} = 200$ radians.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="6pi" set=extra
A clock's minute hand is 12 cm long. How far does its tip travel in 15 minutes?
hint: Find the angle in radians first.
solution: 15 minutes is a quarter turn: $\frac{\pi}{2}$. $s = 12 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = 6\pi$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="3200pi/3" tolerance="0.01" set=extra creative=true
Two towns lie on the same north-south line, at latitudes 10° N and 40° N. Take the Earth as a sphere of radius 6400 km. How far apart are they along the surface? (exact or to hundredths)
hint: The angle between them at the Earth's centre is the difference in latitude.
solution: $30° = \frac{\pi}{6}$. $s = 6400 \cdot \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{3200\pi}{3} \approx 3351.03$ km.
:::
