---
id: mul-02
title: Multiplying bigger numbers
---
::: rule
To multiply by a one-digit number, multiply each digit starting with the **ones**, and **carry** just like in adding.

To multiply by a two-digit number:
1. multiply by the ones digit
2. multiply by the tens digit (that's really tens, so put a 0 at the end)
3. add the two results
:::

::: example
$47 \times 6$

Ones: $7 \times 6 = 42$. Write 2, carry 4.
Tens: $4 \times 6 = 24$, plus the 4 carried is 28.

The answer is **282**.
:::

::: example
$34 \times 25$

$34 \times 5 = 170$
$34 \times 20 = 680$
$170 + 680 = 850$

The answer is **850**.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="69"
$23 \times 3 = ?$
solution: $3 \times 3 = 9$, $2 \times 3 = 6$: **69**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="232"
$58 \times 4 = ?$
hint: $8 \times 4 = 32$: write 2, carry 3.
solution: Ones: $8 \times 4 = 32$, write 2 carry 3. Tens: $5 \times 4 + 3 = 23$. Answer: **232**.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="936"
$312 \times 3 = ?$
solution: $2 \times 3 = 6$, $1 \times 3 = 3$, $3 \times 3 = 9$: **936**.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="364"
$26 \times 14 = ?$
hint: Work out $26 \times 4$ and $26 \times 10$, then add.
solution: $26 \times 4 = 104$, $26 \times 10 = 260$, $104 + 260 = 364$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="1440"
$45 \times 32 = ?$
solution: $45 \times 2 = 90$, $45 \times 30 = 1350$, $90 + 1350 = 1440$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="288"
There are 24 boxes with 12 pencils in each. How many pencils?
hint: $24 \times 12 = 24 \times 10 + 24 \times 2$.
solution: $24 \times 10 = 240$, $24 \times 2 = 48$, $240 + 48 = 288$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="735"
$105 \times 7 = ?$
solution: $5 \times 7 = 35$: write 5 carry 3. $0 \times 7 + 3 = 3$. $1 \times 7 = 7$. Answer: **735**.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="6300"
$63 \times 100 = ?$
hint: Multiplying by 100 puts two zeros on the end.
solution: $63 \times 100 = 6300$.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=multi answers="4;9" creative=true
Find two whole numbers whose product is 36 and whose sum is 13. Type both, like 2, 7.
hint: List the pairs that multiply to 36: 1 and 36, 2 and 18, ...
solution: Pairs for 36: 1 and 36, 2 and 18, 3 and 12, **4 and 9**, 6 and 6. Only $4 + 9 = 13$.
:::
