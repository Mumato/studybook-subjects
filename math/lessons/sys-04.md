---
id: sys-04
title: Word problems with two unknowns
---
::: rule
1. Use two letters for the two unknowns.
2. Write **two** equations, one for each fact in the story.
3. Solve the system by substitution or elimination.
:::

::: example
Adult tickets cost 8 dollars and child tickets 5. Ten tickets cost 65 dollars. How many adult tickets?

$a + c = 10$ and $8a + 5c = 65$. Substitute $c = 10 - a$: $8a + 50 - 5a = 65$, so $3a = 15$ and $a = 5$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="13"
Two numbers add up to 20 and their difference is 6. What is the larger number?
solution: $x + y = 20$, $x - y = 6$. Add: $2x = 26$, $x = 13$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="6"
You have 12 coins, all 5-cent and 10-cent coins, worth 90 cents in total. How many 5-cent coins?
hint: $n + d = 12$ and $5n + 10d = 90$.
solution: $d = 12 - n$. $5n + 120 - 10n = 90$, so $-5n = -30$ and $n = 6$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="5"
A rectangle's perimeter is 26 and its length is 3 more than its width. What is the width?
solution: $2l + 2w = 26$ and $l = w + 3$. $2(w + 3) + 2w = 26$, $4w = 20$, $w = 5$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="1"
3 apples and 2 pears cost 7 dollars. 1 apple and 2 pears cost 5 dollars. How much is one apple?
hint: Subtract the second equation from the first.
solution: $3a + 2p - (a + 2p) = 7 - 5$, so $2a = 2$ and $a = 1$ dollar.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="2"
In the same shop (3 apples + 2 pears = 7, 1 apple + 2 pears = 5), how much is one pear?
solution: $a = 1$, so $1 + 2p = 5$ and $p = 2$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="5"
You mix a 10% juice and a 30% juice to get 20 L of 25% juice. How many litres of the 10% juice?
hint: $x + y = 20$ and $0.1x + 0.3y = 0.25 \times 20$.
solution: $y = 20 - x$: $0.1x + 6 - 0.3x = 5$, $-0.2x = -1$, $x = 5$ L.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="10"
Sam is twice as old as Jo. In 5 years their ages will add up to 40. How old is Jo now?
solution: $s = 2j$ and $(s + 5) + (j + 5) = 40$. $3j + 10 = 40$, $j = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="10"
A farm has 30 animals, all cows and chickens, with 80 legs in total. How many cows?
solution: $c + h = 30$, $4c + 2h = 80$. Subtract twice the first: $2c = 20$, $c = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=numeric answer="-20" creative=true
Two numbers add up to 1 and their difference is 9. What is their product?
hint: Find the two numbers first.
solution: $x + y = 1$, $x - y = 9$: $x = 5$, $y = -4$. Product: $5 \times (-4) = -20$.
:::

::: problem id=x1 type=numeric answer="7" set=extra
Two numbers add up to 35. The larger is 4 times the smaller. What is the smaller?
solution: $a = 4b$ and $a + b = 35$. $5b = 35$, $b = 7$.
:::

::: problem id=x2 type=numeric answer="9" set=extra
Two numbers add up to 27 and differ by 9. What is the smaller?
solution: $x + y = 27$, $x - y = 9$. Add: $x = 18$. So $y = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=x3 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
You have 15 coins, all 10-cent and 25-cent coins, worth 270 cents. How many 25-cent coins?
hint: $d + q = 15$ and $10d + 25q = 270$.
solution: $d = 15 - q$: $150 - 10q + 25q = 270$, so $15q = 120$ and $q = 8$.
:::

::: problem id=x4 type=numeric answer="30" set=extra
Student tickets cost 4 dollars and adult tickets 9 dollars. 100 tickets bring in 550 dollars. How many adult tickets were sold?
solution: $s + a = 100$, $4s + 9a = 550$. $400 - 4a + 9a = 550$, $5a = 150$, $a = 30$.
:::

::: problem id=x5 type=numeric answer="12" set=extra
A rectangle has perimeter 40. Its length is 4 more than its width. What is the length?
solution: $2l + 2w = 40$ and $l = w + 4$. $4w + 8 = 40$, $w = 8$, $l = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=x6 type=numeric answer="4" set=extra
2 burgers and 1 fries cost 11 dollars. 1 burger and 2 fries cost 10 dollars. What does a burger cost?
solution: Double the first: $4b + 2f = 22$. Subtract the second: $3b = 12$, $b = 4$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x7 type=numeric answer="17" set=extra
Ann is 6 years older than Ben. Their ages add up to 40. How old is Ben?
solution: $a = b + 6$, $a + b = 40$. $2b + 6 = 40$, $b = 17$.
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=numeric answer="10" set=extra
You mix a 20% acid and a 50% acid to make 30 L of 40% acid. How many litres of the 20% acid?
hint: $x + y = 30$ and $0.2x + 0.5y = 0.4 \times 30$.
solution: $0.2x + 0.5(30 - x) = 12$, so $15 - 0.3x = 12$ and $x = 10$ L.
:::

::: problem id=x9 type=numeric answer="2500" set=extra
5000 dollars is split between an account paying 3% and one paying 5%. The total interest in a year is 200 dollars. How much is in the 5% account?
solution: $x + y = 5000$, $0.03x + 0.05y = 200$. $150 - 0.03y + 0.05y = 200$, $0.02y = 50$, $y = 2500$.
:::

::: problem id=x10 type=numeric answer="50" set=extra
A plane flies at 600 km/h with the wind and 500 km/h against it. What is the wind speed?
solution: $p + w = 600$, $p - w = 500$. Subtract: $2w = 100$, $w = 50$ km/h.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=choice set=extra
A pen and a pencil cost 3 dollars together. The pen costs 2 dollars more than the pencil. Which system fits? (p = pen, q = pencil)
- [x] $p + q = 3$, $p = q + 2$
- [ ] $p + q = 3$, $q = p + 2$
- [ ] $p - q = 3$, $p = 2q$
solution: Together: $p + q = 3$. The pen is 2 more: $p = q + 2$.
:::

::: problem id=x12 type=text answers="no" set=extra
A farm has 30 animals (cows and chickens) with 80 legs. Lia says there are 8 cows and 22 chickens. Is she right? Type yes or no.
solution: $8 + 22 = 30$ is fine, but the legs are $4(8) + 2(22) = 76$, not 80: **no**. (It's 10 cows.)
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=numeric answer="20" set=extra
Making x mugs costs $200 + 5x$ dollars, and selling them brings in $15x$ dollars. How many mugs must be sold to break even?
solution: Break even: $15x = 200 + 5x$, so $10x = 200$ and $x = 20$.
:::

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="36" set=extra
A two-digit number has digits adding up to 9. Swapping the digits makes it 27 bigger. What is the number?
hint: Write the number as $10t + u$.
solution: $t + u = 9$ and $(10u + t) - (10t + u) = 27$, so $u - t = 3$. Then $u = 6$, $t = 3$: **36**.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="10" set=extra
Gym A charges 50 dollars plus 5 dollars a visit. Gym B charges 20 dollars plus 8 dollars a visit. For how many visits do they cost the same?
solution: $50 + 5v = 20 + 8v$, so $30 = 3v$ and $v = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="6" set=extra
Coffee beans at 8 dollars per kg and 12 dollars per kg are mixed to make 10 kg worth 9.60 dollars per kg. How many kg of the 8-dollar beans?
hint: The whole mix is worth $10 \times 9.6 = 96$ dollars.
solution: $x + y = 10$, $8x + 12y = 96$. $80 - 8y + 12y = 96$, $y = 4$, so $x = 6$ kg.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="50" set=extra
A rectangle's length is twice its width. If both are made 3 longer, the perimeter becomes 42. What was the original area?
hint: $l = 2w$ and $2(l + 3) + 2(w + 3) = 42$.
solution: $2l + 2w + 12 = 42$, so $l + w = 15$. With $l = 2w$: $w = 5$, $l = 10$. Area 50.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="300" set=extra
A concert sold 500 tickets: floor seats at 40 dollars and balcony seats at 25 dollars, for 15500 dollars in total. How many balcony tickets were sold?
hint: $f + b = 500$ and $40f + 25b = 15500$.
solution: $40(500 - b) + 25b = 15500$, so $20000 - 15b = 15500$ and $b = 300$.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="2" set=extra
Jo covered 10 km in 1.5 hours, running at 8 km/h and walking at 4 km/h. How many km did Jo walk?
hint: Let r and w be the hours running and walking.
solution: $r + w = 1.5$, $8r + 4w = 10$. $8r + 6 - 4r = 10$, $r = 1$, $w = 0.5$. Walked $4 \times 0.5 = 2$ km.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="7" set=extra creative=true
3 sandwiches and 2 drinks cost 19 dollars. 5 sandwiches and 4 drinks cost 33 dollars. What do 1 sandwich and 1 drink cost together?
hint: You don't need each price: subtract the first order from the second.
solution: The difference is 2 sandwiches and 2 drinks for $33 - 19 = 14$, so one of each is 7 dollars. (Check: sandwich 5, drink 2.)
:::
