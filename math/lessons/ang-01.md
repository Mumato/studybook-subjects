---
id: ang-01
title: Angle relationships
---
::: rule
- **Complementary** angles add up to 90°. **Supplementary** angles add up to 180°.
- Angles on a straight line add up to 180°; angles around a point add up to 360°.
- **Vertical angles** (opposite each other where two lines cross) are equal.
- When a line crosses two **parallel** lines, **corresponding** angles and **alternate** angles are equal.
:::

::: example
Two lines cross. One angle is 110°. The angle next to it on the line is $180 - 110 = 70°$, and the angle opposite is also 110°.
:::

Type angles as just the number of degrees, like 70.

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="55"
What is the complement of a 35° angle?
solution: $90 - 35 = 55°$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="70"
What is the supplement of a 110° angle?
solution: $180 - 110 = 70°$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="48"
Two lines cross, making a 48° angle. What is the angle vertically opposite it?
solution: Vertical angles are equal: **48°**.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="60"
Two angles on a straight line are x and 2x. What is x?
hint: They add up to 180°.
solution: $3x = 180$, $x = 60°$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="75"
A line crosses two parallel lines. One angle is 75°. What is the corresponding angle at the other parallel line?
solution: Corresponding angles are equal: **75°**.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="140"
Three angles around a point are 100°, 120° and x. What is x?
solution: $360 - 100 - 120 = 140°$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=text answers="complementary"
What do we call two angles that add up to 90°?
solution: **Complementary**.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=text answers="yes"
Are vertical angles always equal? Type yes or no.
solution: **Yes**.
:::
