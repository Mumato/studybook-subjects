title: Multiplying whole numbers
---
Multiplication is repeated addition.

::: rule
$a \times b$ means $b$ added $a$ times.
:::

::: example
$3 \times 4 = 4 + 4 + 4 = 12$
:::

![3 rows of 4 dots make 12](assets/arrays.png)

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="42"
What is $6 \times 7$?
hint: Think of 6 groups of 7.
solution: $6 \times 7 = 42$
because six sevens make forty-two.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Which is bigger?
- [ ] $2 \times 3$
- [x] $3 \times 3$
- [ ] $1 \times 5$
solution: $9 > 6 > 5$
:::
