id: geo-01
title: Square roots
---
::: rule
The **square root** $\sqrt{a}$ is the number that, multiplied by itself, gives a: $\sqrt{25} = 5$ because $5^2 = 25$.

Numbers like 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100 are **perfect squares**. For other numbers, the root lies between two whole numbers: $\sqrt{50}$ is between 7 and 8.

Under a root sign, work out the inside first: $\sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
:::

::: example
$\sqrt{49} = 7$ because $7 \times 7 = 49$.
:::

::: example
$\sqrt{50}$: $7^2 = 49$ and $8^2 = 64$, so $\sqrt{50}$ is a little more than 7.
:::

You can type square roots as sqrt(…).

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Between which two whole numbers is $\sqrt{30}$?
- [x] 5 and 6
- [ ] 4 and 5
- [ ] 6 and 7
solution: $5^2 = 25$ and $6^2 = 36$, and 30 is in between.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="10" set=extra
A big square has 4 times the area of a square with side 5. How long is the side of the big square?
hint: Find the small area first.
solution: Small area 25, big area $4 \times 25 = 100$, side $\sqrt{100} = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="7.4" tolerance="0.05" set=extra
Estimate $\sqrt{55}$ to the nearest tenth.
hint: $7^2 = 49$ and $8^2 = 64$. Try $7.4^2$ and $7.5^2$.
solution: $7.4^2 = 54.76$ and $7.5^2 = 56.25$. 55 is much closer to 54.76, so $\sqrt{55} \approx 7.4$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra creative=true
What is the smallest whole number n so that $12n$ is a perfect square?
hint: Write 12 as $4 \times 3$.
solution: $12 = 4 \times 3$. Multiplying by 3 gives $36 = 6^2$. So $n = 3$.
:::
