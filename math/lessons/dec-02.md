id: dec-02
title: Adding and subtracting decimals
---
::: rule
Line up the **decimal points**, fill empty places with zeros, then add or subtract as with whole numbers. The decimal point in the answer goes straight below.
:::

::: example
$4.75 + 12.3$

Write 12.3 as 12.30. $4.75 + 12.30 = 17.05$.
:::

::: example
$10 - 3.46$

Write 10 as 10.00. $10.00 - 3.46 = 6.54$.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="44.26" set=extra
Ana has 50 dollars. She spends 12.49 and 8.75 dollars, then earns 15.50 dollars. How much does she have now?
hint: Work step by step, left to right.
solution: $50 - 12.49 = 37.51$, $37.51 - 8.75 = 28.76$, $28.76 + 15.50 = 44.26$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="3.5" set=extra
A fence of 20.6 m in total goes round a garden with four sides. Three sides are 6.8 m, 3.5 m and 6.8 m. How long is the fourth side?
hint: Add the three known sides first.
solution: $6.8 + 3.5 + 6.8 = 17.1$, and $20.6 - 17.1 = 3.5$ m.
:::

:::

solution: $2.35 + 3.65 = 6.00$. (Check others: $1.8 + 4.15 = 5.95$.) The larger is **3.65**.
