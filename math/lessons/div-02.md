---
id: div-02
title: Long division
---
::: rule
For bigger numbers, repeat four steps from left to right:
1. **Divide** the part you're looking at
2. **Multiply** back
3. **Subtract**
4. **Bring down** the next digit

Check by multiplying: answer $\times$ divisor should give the starting number.
:::

::: example
$864 \div 4$

8 ÷ 4 = 2. $2 \times 4 = 8$, $8 - 8 = 0$. Bring down 6.
6 ÷ 4 = 1. $1 \times 4 = 4$, $6 - 4 = 2$. Bring down 4: 24.
24 ÷ 4 = 6. $6 \times 4 = 24$, $24 - 24 = 0$.

Answer: **216**. Check: $216 \times 4 = 864$.
:::

::: example
$936 \div 12$

93 ÷ 12 = 7. $7 \times 12 = 84$, $93 - 84 = 9$. Bring down 6: 96.
96 ÷ 12 = 8. $8 \times 12 = 96$, remainder 0.

Answer: **78**. Check: $78 \times 12 = 936$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="21"
$84 \div 4 = ?$
solution: 8 ÷ 4 = 2, 4 ÷ 4 = 1: **21**. Check: $21 \times 4 = 84$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="26"
$156 \div 6 = ?$
hint: 15 ÷ 6 = 2 with 3 left over. Bring down the 6.
solution: 15 ÷ 6 = 2, $15 - 12 = 3$. Bring down 6: 36 ÷ 6 = 6. Answer: **26**.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="147"
$735 \div 5 = ?$
solution: 7 ÷ 5 = 1 r 2. 23 ÷ 5 = 4 r 3. 35 ÷ 5 = 7. Answer: **147**. Check: $147 \times 5 = 735$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="156"
$1248 \div 8 = ?$
hint: Start with 12 ÷ 8.
solution: 12 ÷ 8 = 1 r 4. 44 ÷ 8 = 5 r 4. 48 ÷ 8 = 6. Answer: **156**. Check: $156 \times 8 = 1248$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="68"
$952 \div 14 = ?$
hint: 95 ÷ 14 is 6, because $6 \times 14 = 84$.
solution: 95 ÷ 14 = 6, $95 - 84 = 11$. Bring down 2: 112 ÷ 14 = 8. Answer: **68**.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="24"
360 students ride 15 buses, with the same number on each bus. How many students per bus?
solution: $360 \div 15 = 24$. Check: $24 \times 15 = 360$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="300"
$2100 \div 7 = ?$
hint: 21 ÷ 7 = 3, then the zeros come along.
solution: $21 \div 7 = 3$, so $2100 \div 7 = 300$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="338"
$4056 \div 12 = ?$
solution: 40 ÷ 12 = 3 r 4. 45 ÷ 12 = 3 r 9. 96 ÷ 12 = 8. Answer: **338**. Check: $338 \times 12 = 4056$.
:::
