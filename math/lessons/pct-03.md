id: pct-03
title: Finding the percent or the whole
---
::: rule
Every percent question uses: **part = percent × whole**.

- missing **percent**: part ÷ whole, then × 100
- missing **whole**: part ÷ percent (as a decimal)
:::

::: example
12 out of 48 is what percent? $12 \div 48 = 0.25 = 25\%$.
:::

::: example
18 is 30% of what number? $18 \div 0.3 = 60$. Check: 30% of 60 is 18.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="160" set=extra
A dough has 240 g of flour, which is 60% of its weight. How many grams of the dough are not flour?
hint: Find the whole weight first.
solution: Whole: $240 \div 0.6 = 400$ g. Not flour: $400 - 240 = 160$ g.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="63" set=extra
30% of a number is 27. What is 70% of the same number?
hint: Find the number, or use 10% first.
solution: 10% is $27 \div 3 = 9$, so 70% is $7 \times 9 = 63$. (The number is 90.)
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="60" set=extra
A team won 18 games, lost 6 and drew 6. What percent of its games did it win?
hint: Add up all the games first.
solution: $18 + 6 + 6 = 30$ games. $18 \div 30 = 0.6 = 60\%$.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="400" set=extra
A town has 1200 adults, who are 75% of the people. The rest are children. How many children are there?
hint: Find the total population first.
solution: Total: $1200 \div 0.75 = 1600$. Children: $1600 - 1200 = 400$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="10" set=extra creative=true
$A$ is 25% of $B$, and $B$ is 40% of $C$. $A$ is what percent of $C$?
hint: Try $C = 100$.
solution: $C = 100$ gives $B = 40$ and $A = 0.25 \times 40 = 10$. So $A$ is **10%** of $C$.
:::
