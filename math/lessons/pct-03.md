id: pct-03
title: Finding the percent or the whole
---
::: rule
Every percent question uses: **part = percent × whole**.

- missing **percent**: part ÷ whole, then × 100
- missing **whole**: part ÷ percent (as a decimal)
:::

::: example
12 out of 48 is what percent? $12 \div 48 = 0.25 = 25\%$.
:::

::: example
18 is 30% of what number? $18 \div 0.3 = 60$. Check: 30% of 60 is 18.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="25"
9 is what percent of 36?
solution: $9 \div 36 = 0.25 = 25\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="90"
You got 18 out of 20 questions right. What percent is that?
solution: $18 \div 20 = 0.9 = 90\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="70"
14 is 20% of what number?
hint: Divide 14 by 0.2.
solution: $14 \div 0.2 = 70$. Check: 20% of 70 is 14.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="60"
45 is 75% of what number?
solution: $45 \div 0.75 = 60$. Check: 75% of 60 is 45.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="20"
7 is what percent of 35?
solution: $7 \div 35 = 0.2 = 20\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="15"
6 of the 40 apples in a box are bad. What percent are bad?
solution: $6 \div 40 = 0.15 = 15\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="46"
23 is 50% of what number?
hint: 50% is half.
solution: 23 is half the number, so the number is **46**.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="400"
You have saved 60 dollars, which is 15% of your goal. What is your goal?
solution: $60 \div 0.15 = 400$ dollars.
:::
