---
id: rat-01
title: Ratios
---
::: rule
A **ratio** compares two amounts: 12 boys to 18 girls is $12 : 18$, which can also be written $\frac{12}{18}$.

Simplify a ratio like a fraction: $12 : 18 = 2 : 3$.

To **share** an amount in a ratio $a : b$, split it into $a + b$ equal parts.
:::

::: example
A class has 12 boys and 18 girls. Boys to girls is $12 : 18 = 2 : 3$. Boys to the whole class is $12 : 30 = 2 : 5$.
:::

::: example
Share 30 sweets in the ratio $2 : 3$. There are $2 + 3 = 5$ parts, each part is $30 \div 5 = 6$. The shares are $2 \times 6 = 12$ and $3 \times 6 = 18$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="3"
Simplify the ratio $8 : 12$. What is the second number?
solution: Divide both by 4: $2 : 3$. The second number is **3**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="2/5"
A class has 10 boys and 15 girls. What fraction of the class are boys?
hint: Boys out of everyone.
solution: $\frac{10}{25} = \frac{2}{5}$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="9"
A recipe uses flour and milk in the ratio $2 : 3$. If you use 6 cups of flour, how many cups of milk?
solution: 6 is $2 \times 3$, so use $3 \times 3 = 9$ cups of milk.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="15"
Red and blue marbles are in the ratio $3 : 5$. There are 40 marbles. How many are red?
hint: How many parts are there altogether?
solution: $3 + 5 = 8$ parts, each $40 \div 8 = 5$. Red: $3 \times 5 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which ratio is the same as $4 : 6$?
- [x] $2 : 3$
- [ ] $3 : 4$
- [ ] $6 : 4$
solution: Divide both by 2: $2 : 3$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="25"
35 dollars are shared in the ratio $2 : 5$. How big is the larger share?
solution: 7 parts of 5 dollars each. The larger share is $5 \times 5 = 25$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="3/4"
Write the ratio of 45 minutes to 1 hour as a fraction in simplest form.
hint: Use the same unit for both.
solution: 1 hour is 60 minutes: $\frac{45}{60} = \frac{3}{4}$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="16"
Cats and dogs at a shelter are in the ratio $3 : 4$. There are 12 cats. How many dogs?
solution: 12 is $3 \times 4$, so dogs: $4 \times 4 = 16$.
:::
