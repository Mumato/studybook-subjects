---
id: pct-04
title: Discounts, tax and percent change
---
::: rule
- **Discount** of d%: new price = price × (1 − d as a decimal)
- **Tax** or **increase** of t%: new price = price × (1 + t as a decimal)
- **Percent change** = change ÷ original × 100
:::

::: example
An 80-dollar game is 25% off. $80 \times 0.75 = 60$ dollars.
:::

::: example
A price goes from 50 to 60 dollars. The change is 10, and $10 \div 50 = 0.2$, so it rose by **20%**.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="48"
A 60-dollar jacket is 20% off. What is the sale price in dollars?
solution: $60 \times 0.8 = 48$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="42"
A meal costs 40 dollars plus 5% tax. What is the total?
solution: $40 \times 1.05 = 42$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="20"
A price goes from 25 to 30 dollars. By what percent did it increase?
hint: The change is 5. Divide by the original price.
solution: $5 \div 25 = 0.2 = 20\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="25"
A price drops from 80 to 60 dollars. By what percent did it decrease?
solution: The change is 20. $20 \div 80 = 0.25 = 25\%$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="35"
A 50-dollar game is 30% off. What is the sale price?
solution: $50 \times 0.7 = 35$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="230"
A town of 200 people grows by 15%. How many people live there now?
solution: $200 \times 1.15 = 230$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="50"
After a 10% discount you pay 45 dollars. What was the original price?
hint: You paid 90% of the original.
solution: $45 \div 0.9 = 50$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
A price of 100 goes up 10%, then the new price goes down 10%. What is the final price?
- [x] 99
- [ ] 100
- [ ] 101
hint: The second 10% is taken from a bigger number.
solution: $100 \times 1.1 = 110$, then $110 \times 0.9 = 99$.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=numeric answer="60" creative=true
A shirt is 50% off, and then there is a further 20% off the sale price. Altogether, what percent off the original price is that?
hint: Start with a price of 100.
solution: $100 \to 50 \to 50 \times 0.8 = 40$. You pay 40 out of 100, so it is **60%** off (not 70%).
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=choice set=extra
Which percent is bigger?
- [ ] The decrease from 50 to 40
- [x] The increase from 40 to 50
- [ ] They are the same
solution: Both change by 10. Decrease: $10 \div 50 = 20\%$. Increase: $10 \div 40 = 25\%$. Always divide by the **original**.
:::

::: problem id=x9 type=choice set=extra

- [x] 25%, because you divide by the original price 80
- [ ] Eli is right
- [ ] 80%
solution: Percent change = change ÷ original: $20 \div 80 = 0.25 = 25\%$.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=numeric answer="0.65" set=extra
To find a price after 35% off, you multiply the price by what decimal?
solution: You pay $100\% - 35\% = 65\%$, so multiply by **0.65**.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="33" set=extra
A 50-dollar item is 40% off, and then 10% tax is added to the sale price. What do you pay?
hint: Discount first, then tax on the new price.
solution: $50 \times 0.6 = 30$, then $30 \times 1.1 = 33$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="80" set=extra
An 80-dollar price goes up 25%, then the new price goes down 20%. What is the final price?
hint: Each percent is of the price at that moment.
solution: $80 \times 1.25 = 100$, then $100 \times 0.8 = 80$ dollars: back where it started.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="73.8" set=extra
A meal costs 60 dollars. You add 8% tax and a 15% tip, both worked out on the 60 dollars. What is the total?
hint: Together that is 23% of 60.
solution: $60 \times 1.23 = 73.80$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
A shop buys a lamp for 40 dollars and marks the price up 50%. Later it sells the lamp at 20% off that price. How many dollars of profit does it make?
hint: Find the marked price, then the sale price.
solution: Marked: $40 \times 1.5 = 60$. Sale: $60 \times 0.8 = 48$. Profit: $48 - 40 = 8$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="50" set=extra
A plant grows from 0.8 m to 1.2 m. By what percent did its height increase?
solution: The change is 0.4. $0.4 \div 0.8 = 0.5 = 50\%$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="21" set=extra creative=true
A price goes up 10%, and then the new price goes up another 10%. By what percent did it go up in total?
hint: Start with a price of 100.
solution: $100 \to 110 \to 110 \times 1.1 = 121$. Total increase **21%**, not 20%.
:::
