title: Place value and rounding
---
Every digit in a number has a **place**, and the place tells you what the digit is worth.

::: rule
In 4,827 the 4 is in the **thousands** place (worth 4000), the 8 in the **hundreds** (800), the 2 in the **tens** (20) and the 7 in the **ones** (7).

To **round**, look at the digit just to the right of the place you round to:
- 5 or more: round **up**
- 4 or less: keep it the same

Then replace every digit to the right with 0.
:::

::: example
What is the 6 in 3,642 worth?

The 6 is in the hundreds place, so it is worth $6 \times 100 = 600$.
:::

::: example
Round 3,648 to the nearest hundred.

The hundreds digit is 6. The digit to its right is 4, which is less than 5, so the 6 stays. Everything after becomes 0: **3,600**.
:::

Type big numbers without commas, like 4827.

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="50"
What is the digit 5 worth in 2,153?
hint: Which place is the 5 in?
solution: The 5 is in the tens place, so it is worth $5 \times 10 = 50$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="70000"
What is the digit 7 worth in 70,412?
hint: Count the places from the right: ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten thousands.
solution: The 7 is in the ten thousands place: $7 \times 10000 = 70000$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="3046"
Write "three thousand forty-six" as a number.
hint: There are no hundreds, so the hundreds digit is 0.
solution: 3 thousands, 0 hundreds, 4 tens, 6 ones: **3046**.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="90"
Round 87 to the nearest ten.
solution: The ones digit is 7, which is 5 or more, so round up: **90**.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="4400"
Round 4,432 to the nearest hundred.
hint: Look at the tens digit.
solution: The hundreds digit is 4 and the tens digit is 3. 3 is less than 5, so keep the 4: **4400**.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="16000"
Round 15,500 to the nearest thousand.
hint: Look at the hundreds digit.
solution: The thousands digit is 5 and the hundreds digit is 5. 5 means round up: **16000**.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="2350"
Round 2,349 to the nearest ten.
solution: The tens digit is 4 and the ones digit is 9, so round up to 5 tens: **2350**.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
Which number is greater?
- [x] 30,298
- [ ] 30,289
solution: Compare from the left. Both start 30,2. In the tens place, 9 is more than 8, so **30,298** is greater.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=numeric answer="487" creative=true
I am a 3-digit number. My hundreds digit is 4. My tens digit is twice my hundreds digit. My ones digit is one less than my tens digit. What number am I?
hint: Find the digits one at a time, starting with the tens.
solution: Hundreds: 4. Tens: $2 \times 4 = 8$. Ones: $8 - 1 = 7$. The number is **487**.
:::
