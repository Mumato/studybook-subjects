---
id: frop-01
title: Multiplying fractions
---
::: rule
To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms:
$\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d}$

Then simplify. The word **of** means multiply: $\frac{3}{4}$ of 20 is $\frac{3}{4} \times 20$.
:::

::: example
$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$
:::

::: example
$\frac{3}{4}$ of 20: $\frac{3}{4} \times 20 = \frac{60}{4} = 15$. (Or: a quarter of 20 is 5, so three quarters is 15.)
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="3/10"
$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{3}{5} = ?$
solution: $\frac{1 \times 3}{2 \times 5} = \frac{3}{10}$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="3/5"
$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{10} = ?$
hint: Multiply, then simplify.
solution: $\frac{18}{30} = \frac{3}{5}$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="18"
What is $\frac{3}{4}$ of 24?
hint: Find a quarter of 24 first.
solution: A quarter of 24 is 6, so three quarters is 18.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="10/3"
$5 \times \frac{2}{3} = ?$
solution: $\frac{5 \times 2}{3} = \frac{10}{3}$ (that is $3\frac{1}{3}$).
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="1"
$\frac{4}{7} \times \frac{7}{4} = ?$
solution: $\frac{28}{28} = 1$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="12"
$\frac{2}{5}$ of the 30 students in a class walk to school. How many walk?
solution: $\frac{2}{5} \times 30 = \frac{60}{5} = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="4/9"
$\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = ?$
hint: Squaring means multiplying the fraction by itself.
solution: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{9}$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="1/6"
$\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{4}{9} = ?$
solution: $\frac{12}{72} = \frac{1}{6}$.
:::

::: problem id=x1 type=numeric answer="6/35" set=extra
$\frac{3}{7} \times \frac{2}{5} = ?$
solution: $\frac{3 \times 2}{7 \times 5} = \frac{6}{35}$.
:::

::: problem id=x2 type=numeric answer="1/4" set=extra
$\frac{5}{6} \times \frac{3}{10} = ?$
solution: $\frac{15}{60} = \frac{1}{4}$.
:::

::: problem id=x3 type=numeric answer="1/6" set=extra
$\frac{4}{9} \times \frac{3}{8} = ?$
hint: Cancel before multiplying: 4 with 8, 3 with 9.
solution: 4 goes into 8 twice; 3 goes into 9 three times. Left: $\frac{1 \times 1}{3 \times 2} = \frac{1}{6}$.
:::

::: problem id=x4 type=numeric answer="18" set=extra
What is $\frac{2}{5}$ of 45?
solution: A fifth of 45 is 9, so two fifths is 18.
:::

::: problem id=x5 type=numeric answer="20" set=extra
$\frac{4}{5} \times 25 = ?$
solution: $25 \div 5 = 5$, and $4 \times 5 = 20$.
:::

::: problem id=x6 type=numeric answer="9/25" set=extra
$\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2 = ?$
solution: $\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{9}{25}$.
:::

::: problem id=x7 type=numeric answer="2/5" set=extra
$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = ?$
hint: Cancel the 3s and the 4s first.
solution: The 3s and 4s cancel, leaving $\frac{2}{5}$.
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=numeric answer="21/2" set=extra
$\frac{7}{12} \times 18 = ?$
solution: $\frac{7 \times 18}{12} = \frac{126}{12} = \frac{21}{2}$ (that is $10\frac{1}{2}$).
:::

::: problem id=x9 type=numeric answer="7/2" set=extra
$2\frac{1}{3} \times 1\frac{1}{2} = ?$
hint: Change both to improper fractions first.
solution: $\frac{7}{3} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{21}{6} = \frac{7}{2}$, or $3\frac{1}{2}$.
:::

::: problem id=x10 type=choice set=extra
Pat says $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{15}$ is wrong and should be $\frac{6}{8}$. What did Pat do?
- [x] Added tops and bottoms instead of multiplying
- [ ] Multiplied correctly
- [ ] Flipped the second fraction
solution: $2 + 4 = 6$ and $3 + 5 = 8$: Pat added. The right answer is $\frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 5} = \frac{8}{15}$.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=choice set=extra
When you multiply 20 by $\frac{3}{4}$, the answer is...
- [x] smaller than 20
- [ ] bigger than 20
- [ ] equal to 20
solution: Multiplying by a fraction less than 1 takes only part of the number: $\frac{3}{4} \times 20 = 15 < 20$.
:::

::: problem id=x12 type=numeric answer="20" set=extra
A tank holds 32 litres and is $\frac{5}{8}$ full. How many litres are in it?
solution: $\frac{5}{8} \times 32 = 5 \times 4 = 20$ litres.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=numeric answer="1/6" set=extra
Half of a cake is left, and you eat $\frac{1}{3}$ of what is left. What fraction of the whole cake did you eat?
solution: $\frac{1}{3}$ of $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}$.
:::

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="40" set=extra
How many minutes are in $\frac{2}{3}$ of an hour?
solution: $\frac{2}{3} \times 60 = 40$ minutes.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="3/4" set=extra
A rectangle is $\frac{3}{2}$ m long and $\frac{1}{2}$ m wide. What is its area in square metres?
solution: Area is length times width: $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4}$ m².
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="5" set=extra
$\frac{?}{6} \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{1}{2}$. What is the missing number?
hint: The product's top is $? \times 3$ and its bottom is 30.
solution: $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{15}{30}$, so $? \times 3 = 15$ and $? = 5$.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="60" set=extra
Of 120 students, $\frac{3}{4}$ play a sport. Of those, $\frac{2}{3}$ play football. How many play football?
hint: Find $\frac{3}{4}$ of 120 first.
solution: $\frac{3}{4} \times 120 = 90$, then $\frac{2}{3} \times 90 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="42/5" set=extra
$3\frac{1}{2} \times 2\frac{2}{5} = ?$
hint: $3\frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{2}$ and $2\frac{2}{5} = \frac{12}{5}$.
solution: $\frac{7}{2} \times \frac{12}{5} = \frac{84}{10} = \frac{42}{5}$, or $8\frac{2}{5}$.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="12" set=extra
Sara spends $\frac{1}{4}$ of her money on a book and $\frac{1}{3}$ of what remains on lunch. She has \$24 left. How many dollars did the book cost?
hint: After the book, $\frac{3}{4}$ is left. Lunch takes $\frac{1}{3}$ of that.
solution: Left at the end: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$ of her money, which is \$24. So she had \$48, and the book cost $\frac{1}{4} \times 48 = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="1/10" set=extra creative=true
$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} \times \cdots \times \frac{9}{10} = ?$
hint: Write a few factors out. What cancels?
solution: Each top cancels the bottom before it: only the first top 1 and the last bottom 10 remain. $\frac{1}{10}$.
:::
