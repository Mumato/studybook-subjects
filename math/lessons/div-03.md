---
id: div-03
title: Remainders
---
::: rule
When a number doesn't split evenly, what's left over is the **remainder**. It is always smaller than the number you divide by.

$47 \div 5 = 9$ **remainder 2**.

**Check:** answer $\times$ divisor + remainder = starting number.
:::

::: example
$47 \div 5$

The biggest multiple of 5 not over 47 is $5 \times 9 = 45$.
$47 - 45 = 2$ is left over.

So $47 \div 5 = 9$ remainder 2. Check: $9 \times 5 + 2 = 47$.
:::

::: example
In a story, read what is being asked. 30 people need cars that hold 4 each. $30 \div 4 = 7$ remainder 2. Seven cars leave 2 people behind, so you need **8** cars.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="3"
What is the remainder of $23 \div 4$?
hint: $4 \times 5 = 20$.
solution: $4 \times 5 = 20$, $23 - 20 = 3$. Remainder **3**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="7"
$50 \div 7 = ?$ remainder something. What is the answer before the remainder?
solution: $7 \times 7 = 49$ is the biggest that fits, so the answer is **7** remainder 1.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="1"
What is the remainder of $100 \div 9$?
hint: $9 \times 11 = 99$.
solution: $100 - 99 = 1$. Remainder **1**.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="1"
What is the remainder of $365 \div 7$?
solution: $7 \times 52 = 364$, $365 - 364 = 1$. Remainder **1** (a year is 52 weeks and 1 day).
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="4"
50 eggs are packed into boxes of 12. How many boxes are completely full?
solution: $50 \div 12 = 4$ remainder 2, so **4** full boxes.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="2"
In the same packing (50 eggs, boxes of 12), how many eggs are left over?
solution: $4 \times 12 = 48$, $50 - 48 = 2$ eggs left over.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="7"
27 people need cars that hold 4 people each. How many cars are needed?
hint: Nobody can be left behind.
solution: $27 \div 4 = 6$ remainder 3. Six cars leave 3 people, so you need **7** cars.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="63"
A division by 5 gave 12 remainder 3. What number was divided?
hint: Use the check: answer × divisor + remainder.
solution: $12 \times 5 + 3 = 63$.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=numeric answer="21" creative=true
I'm a number between 20 and 30. Divided by 4 I leave remainder 1. Divided by 5 I also leave remainder 1. What number am I?
hint: If both leave 1 over, then one less than me divides evenly by 4 and by 5.
solution: One less than the number is a multiple of both 4 and 5, so of 20. $20 + 1 = 21$. Check: $21 = 4 \times 5 + 1$ and $21 = 5 \times 4 + 1$.
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=choice set=extra
Sue writes $29 \div 4 = 6$ remainder 5. What is wrong?

- [ ] Nothing, it is right
- [ ] It should be 5 remainder 9
- [ ] It should be 8 remainder 3

::: problem id=x12 type=text answers="wednesday" set=extra
Today is Monday. What day of the week will it be in 30 days?
hint: Every 7 days it is Monday again. Find the remainder of $30 \div 7$.
solution: $30 \div 7 = 4$ remainder 2. After 4 full weeks it's Monday again, then 2 more days: **Wednesday**.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=choice set=extra
Which number can **not** be a remainder when you divide by 6?
- [ ] 0
- [ ] 3
- [ ] 5
- [x] 6
solution: A remainder is always smaller than the divisor, so it can be 0 to 5. **6** would make another group.
:::

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="60" set=extra
A baker has 250 cookies and packs them in bags of 12. She sells each full bag for 3 dollars and eats the leftovers. How many dollars does she get?
hint: Find the number of full bags first.
solution: $250 \div 12 = 20$ remainder 10, so 20 full bags. $20 \times 3 = 60$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="1" set=extra
47 people sit at tables for 6. Just enough tables are used for everyone. How many seats are empty?
hint: How many tables are needed? How many seats do they have?
solution: $47 \div 6 = 7$ remainder 5, so 8 tables are needed. $8 \times 6 = 48$ seats, $48 - 47 = 1$ empty.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="51" set=extra
What is the smallest number greater than 50 that leaves remainder 2 when divided by 7?
hint: Find a multiple of 7 close to 50, then add 2.
solution: $7 \times 7 = 49$, and $49 + 2 = 51$. It is greater than 50, so **51**.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="16" set=extra
1000 hours is how many full days and how many hours more? Type the extra hours.
hint: A day has 24 hours.
solution: $1000 \div 24 = 41$ remainder 16, because $41 \times 24 = 984$. So 41 days and **16** hours.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
What is the remainder of $2024 \div 9$?
hint: Use long division: 20 ÷ 9 first.
solution: $9 \times 224 = 2016$, $2024 - 2016 = 8$. Remainder **8**.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="9" set=extra
Each of 28 children needs 3 pencils. Pencils come in packs of 10. How many packs must the teacher buy?
hint: Find the total number of pencils first.
solution: $28 \times 3 = 84$ pencils. $84 \div 10 = 8$ remainder 4, so 8 packs aren't enough: **9** packs.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="49" set=extra creative=true
I'm a number between 30 and 60. Divided by 2, by 3 or by 4 I leave remainder 1, but 7 divides me exactly. What number am I?
hint: One less than me divides by 2, 3 and 4, so by 12.
solution: One less than the number is a multiple of 12: 36 or 48. So the number is 37 or 49. Only $49 = 7 \times 7$ divides by 7: **49**.
:::
