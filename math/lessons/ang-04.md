---
id: ang-04
title: Area and volume
---
::: rule
Areas: parallelogram $bh$, trapezoid $\frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$, circle $\pi r^2$.

Volumes:
- box (prism): base area × height, $V = lwh$
- cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$
- cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
- sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

Surface area: add the areas of all the faces.
:::

::: example
A cylinder with radius 2 and height 5: $V = \pi \times 4 \times 5 = 20\pi$.
:::

::: example
A trapezoid with parallel sides 6 and 10 and height 4: $A = \frac{1}{2}(6 + 10) \times 4 = 32$.
:::

You can give answers in terms of π, like 20pi.

What is the volume of a box 3 by 4 by 5?
solution: $3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="20pi"
What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 2 and height 5?
solution: $\pi \times 2^2 \times 5 = 20\pi$.
:::

:::

:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="24"
What is the surface area of a cube with side 2?
solution: 6 faces of $2 \times 2$: $6 \times 4 = 24$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="32"
What is the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 6 and 10 and height 4?
solution: $\frac{1}{2}(16)(4) = 32$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="21"
What is the area of a parallelogram with base 7 and height 3?
solution: $7 \times 3 = 21$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="40+2pi"
A 10 by 4 rectangle has a half-circle on one of its 4-long sides (so the radius is 2). What is the total area?
hint: Half of $\pi r^2$.
solution: $40 + \frac{1}{2}\pi \times 2^2 = 40 + 2\pi$.
:::

solution: $(2r)^3 = 8r^3$: **8** times.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=numeric answer="12000pi" set=extra
A water tank is a cylinder with radius 2 m and height 3 m. One cubic metre holds 1000 litres. How many litres does it hold? (Answer in terms of π.)
solution: $V = \pi \times 4 \times 3 = 12\pi$ m³. Times 1000: $12000\pi$ litres (about 37700).
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="40" set=extra
An open box (no lid) has a 4 by 3 base and height 2. What is its outside surface area?
solution: Base 12, two sides $4 \times 2$, two sides $3 \times 2$: $12 + 16 + 12 = 40$.
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="9" set=extra
A cylinder's radius is tripled and its height stays the same. How many times bigger is the volume?
solution: $r^2$ becomes $(3r)^2 = 9r^2$: **9** times.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="28pi" set=extra
What is the total surface area of a cylinder with radius 2 and height 5?
hint: Two circles, plus the curved side, which unrolls into a rectangle $2\pi r$ wide and h tall.
solution: Circles $2 \times 4\pi = 8\pi$. Side $2\pi(2)(5) = 20\pi$. Total $28\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="48pi" set=extra
An ice cream cone has radius 3 and height 10, with a half sphere of radius 3 on top. What is the total volume?
hint: Add the cone and the hemisphere.
solution: Cone $\frac{1}{3}\pi(9)(10) = 30\pi$. Hemisphere $18\pi$. Total $48\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="64-16pi" set=extra
A circle of radius 4 is cut out of an 8 by 8 square. What area is left?
hint: The circle just fits in the square.
solution: $64 - \pi \times 16 = 64 - 16\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="2/3" set=extra creative=true
A sphere fits exactly inside a cylinder (same radius r, cylinder height $2r$). What fraction of the cylinder does the sphere fill?
hint: Write both volumes in terms of r.
solution: Sphere $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, cylinder $\pi r^2 \cdot 2r = 2\pi r^3$. Ratio $\frac{4/3}{2} = \frac{2}{3}$.
:::
