---
id: ang-04
title: Area and volume
---
::: rule
Areas: parallelogram $bh$, trapezoid $\frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$, circle $\pi r^2$.

Volumes:
- box (prism): base area × height, $V = lwh$
- cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$
- cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
- sphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

Surface area: add the areas of all the faces.
:::

::: example
A cylinder with radius 2 and height 5: $V = \pi \times 4 \times 5 = 20\pi$.
:::

::: example
A trapezoid with parallel sides 6 and 10 and height 4: $A = \frac{1}{2}(6 + 10) \times 4 = 32$.
:::

You can give answers in terms of π, like 20pi.

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="60"
What is the volume of a box 3 by 4 by 5?
solution: $3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="20pi"
What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 2 and height 5?
solution: $\pi \times 2^2 \times 5 = 20\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="12pi"
What is the volume of a cone with radius 3 and height 4?
solution: $\frac{1}{3}\pi \times 9 \times 4 = 12\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="36pi"
What is the volume of a sphere with radius 3?
solution: $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 27 = 36\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="24"
What is the surface area of a cube with side 2?
solution: 6 faces of $2 \times 2$: $6 \times 4 = 24$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="32"
What is the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 6 and 10 and height 4?
solution: $\frac{1}{2}(16)(4) = 32$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="21"
What is the area of a parallelogram with base 7 and height 3?
solution: $7 \times 3 = 21$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="40+2pi"
A 10 by 4 rectangle has a half-circle on one of its 4-long sides (so the radius is 2). What is the total area?
hint: Half of $\pi r^2$.
solution: $40 + \frac{1}{2}\pi \times 2^2 = 40 + 2\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=c1 type=numeric answer="8" creative=true
If you double the radius of a sphere, how many times bigger does its volume get?
hint: The radius is cubed.
solution: $(2r)^3 = 8r^3$: **8** times.
:::
