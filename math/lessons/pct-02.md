---
id: pct-02
title: Percent of a number
---
::: rule
To find a percent **of** a number, change the percent to a decimal and multiply:
$30\% \text{ of } 80 = 0.3 \times 80 = 24$

Mental shortcuts: 10% is divide by 10, 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 1% is divide by 100.
:::

::: example
$30\%$ of 80: $0.3 \times 80 = 24$.
:::

::: example
A 15% tip on a 40-dollar meal: 10% is 4, 5% is half of that, 2. Together: **6** dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="32"
What is 50% of 64?
solution: Half of 64 is **32**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="25"
What is 10% of 250?
solution: $250 \div 10 = 25$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="12"
What is 25% of 48?
hint: 25% is a quarter.
solution: $48 \div 4 = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="7"
What is 20% of 35?
solution: $0.2 \times 35 = 7$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="9"
What is 15% of 60?
hint: 10% plus 5%.
solution: 10% of 60 is 6, 5% is 3. $6 + 3 = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="12"
40% of the 30 students in a class play an instrument. How many is that?
solution: $0.4 \times 30 = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="60"
What is 120% of 50?
hint: 120% is more than the whole: $1.2 \times 50$.
solution: $1.2 \times 50 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="7"
What is 1% of 700?
solution: $700 \div 100 = 7$.
:::
