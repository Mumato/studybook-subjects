id: pct-02
title: Percent of a number
---
::: rule
To find a percent **of** a number, change the percent to a decimal and multiply:
$30\% \text{ of } 80 = 0.3 \times 80 = 24$

Mental shortcuts: 10% is divide by 10, 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 1% is divide by 100.
:::

::: example
$30\%$ of 80: $0.3 \times 80 = 24$.
:::

::: example
A 15% tip on a 40-dollar meal: 10% is 4, 5% is half of that, 2. Together: **6** dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=choice set=extra
Which is bigger: 10% of 50, or 50% of 10?
- [ ] 10% of 50
- [ ] 50% of 10
- [x] They are equal
solution: $0.1 \times 50 = 5$ and $0.5 \times 10 = 5$. Equal: $a\%$ of $b$ is always $b\%$ of $a$.
:::

solution: $80 \div 5$ is 20%, not 5%. 10% is 8, so 5% is **4**.

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="160" set=extra
A school has 800 students. 35% walk and 45% take the bus. The rest are driven. How many are driven?
hint: Find the percent for "the rest" first.
solution: $100\% - 35\% - 45\% = 20\%$. 20% of 800 is **160**.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="24" set=extra
$15\%$ of 80 plus $80\%$ of 15 = ?
hint: Are the two parts equal?
solution: $0.15 \times 80 = 12$ and $0.8 \times 15 = 12$. Total **24**.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="810" set=extra
Jay has 1200 dollars. He spends 25% of it, then 10% of what is left. How much does he have now?
hint: Each percent is of a different amount.
solution: 25% of 1200 is 300, leaving 900. 10% of 900 is 90, leaving **810** dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="9" set=extra creative=true

hint: $a\%$ of $b$ equals $b\%$ of $a$. Is 25% of 36 easier?
solution: 36% of 25 = 25% of 36 = a quarter of 36 = **9**.
:::
