---
id: rat-02
title: Rates and unit rates
---
::: rule
A **rate** compares amounts in different units, like kilometres per hour or dollars per kilogram.

A **unit rate** is the amount for **one** unit: divide.

To find the better buy, compare the price for one item.
:::

::: example
A car drives 150 km in 3 hours. $150 \div 3 = 50$ km per hour.
:::

::: example
6 rolls for 4.50 dollars or 10 rolls for 7 dollars? $4.50 \div 6 = 0.75$ and $7 \div 10 = 0.70$ dollars per roll. The 10-pack is the better buy.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="60"
A train travels 240 km in 4 hours. What is its speed in km per hour?
solution: $240 \div 4 = 60$ km per hour.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="0.6"
5 apples cost 3 dollars. How much does one apple cost?
solution: $3 \div 5 = 0.6$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="60"

solution: $300 \div 5 = 60$ words per minute.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="12"
You earn 84 dollars for 7 hours of work. How much per hour?
solution: $84 \div 7 = 12$ dollars per hour.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which is the better buy?
- [ ] 3 pens for 1.50 dollars
- [x] 5 pens for 2.25 dollars
hint: Find the price of one pen in each.
solution: $1.50 \div 3 = 0.50$ and $2.25 \div 5 = 0.45$ dollars per pen, so 5 for 2.25.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="21"
A car uses 6 litres of fuel per 100 km. How many litres for 350 km?
hint: How many hundreds of km are in 350?
solution: 350 km is 3.5 hundreds: $6 \times 3.5 = 21$ litres.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="360"

:::

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="1.5" set=extra

:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="75" set=extra

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="60" set=extra
Two cars leave the same place in the same direction. One drives at 60 km per hour, the other at 45 km per hour. How far apart are they after 4 hours?
hint: Each hour the gap grows by the difference in speed.
solution: The gap grows $60 - 45 = 15$ km each hour. After 4 hours: $4 \times 15 = 60$ km.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
Ana paints 3 m² of wall per hour and Bo paints 2 m² per hour. Working together, how many hours do they need for 40 m²?
hint: Add their rates.
solution: Together $3 + 2 = 5$ m² per hour. $40 \div 5 = 8$ hours.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="0.36" set=extra
Juice comes in 750 mL for 2.85 dollars or 1.2 L for 4.32 dollars. What is the price per 100 mL of the better buy?
hint: 1.2 L is 1200 mL.
solution: $2.85 \div 7.5 = 0.38$ and $4.32 \div 12 = 0.36$ dollars per 100 mL. The better buy costs **0.36**.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="52.5" set=extra
A trip is 210 km. The first 120 km are driven at 60 km per hour and the rest at 45 km per hour. What is the average speed for the whole trip, in km per hour?
hint: Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
solution: Times: $120 \div 60 = 2$ h and $90 \div 45 = 2$ h. $210 \div 4 = 52.5$ km per hour.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="1.5" set=extra creative=true
Walking to school at 4 km per hour takes 15 minutes longer than cycling at 12 km per hour. How many km is it to school?
hint: Try distances: how long does each way take for 1 km? For 2 km?
solution: Each km takes 15 min walking and 5 min cycling: 10 min more per km. 15 min more means $15 \div 10 = 1.5$ km.
:::
