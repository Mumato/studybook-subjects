id: fr-02
title: Simplifying fractions
---
::: rule
To **simplify** a fraction, divide the top and the bottom by their **greatest common factor**. A fraction is in **simplest form** when the only number that divides both is 1.
:::

::: example
Simplify $\frac{18}{24}$.

The GCF of 18 and 24 is 6. $18 \div 6 = 3$ and $24 \div 6 = 4$, so $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4}$.

You can also divide in smaller steps: $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{9}{12} = \frac{3}{4}$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="4"
Simplify $\frac{6}{8}$. What is the denominator in simplest form?
solution: Divide both by 2: $\frac{3}{4}$. The denominator is **4**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="2"
Simplify $\frac{10}{15}$. What is the numerator in simplest form?
hint: Both divide by 5.
solution: $\frac{10 \div 5}{15 \div 5} = \frac{2}{3}$. The numerator is **2**.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
What is $\frac{12}{16}$ in simplest form?
- [x] $\frac{3}{4}$
- [ ] $\frac{6}{8}$
- [ ] $\frac{4}{3}$
solution: GCF is 4: $\frac{12 \div 4}{16 \div 4} = \frac{3}{4}$. ($\frac{6}{8}$ is equal but can still be simplified.)
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="3"
Simplify $\frac{24}{36}$. What is the denominator in simplest form?
hint: The GCF of 24 and 36 is 12.
solution: $\frac{24 \div 12}{36 \div 12} = \frac{2}{3}$. The denominator is **3**.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which fraction is already in simplest form?
- [ ] $\frac{9}{12}$
- [x] $\frac{7}{15}$
- [ ] $\frac{10}{25}$
solution: 9 and 12 share 3; 10 and 25 share 5. 7 and 15 share nothing but 1, so $\frac{7}{15}$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="3"
Simplify $\frac{45}{60}$. What is the numerator in simplest form?
hint: Both divide by 15.
solution: $\frac{45 \div 15}{60 \div 15} = \frac{3}{4}$. The numerator is **3**.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="3"
You answered 20 of 30 questions correctly. Write this as a fraction in simplest form. What is the denominator?
solution: $\frac{20}{30} = \frac{2}{3}$. The denominator is **3**.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
What is $\frac{14}{21}$ in simplest form?
- [x] $\frac{2}{3}$
- [ ] $\frac{7}{10}$
- [ ] $\frac{1}{3}$
solution: Both divide by 7: $\frac{2}{3}$.
:::
