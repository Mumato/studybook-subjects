---
id: fr-02
title: Simplifying fractions
---
::: rule
To **simplify** a fraction, divide the top and the bottom by their **greatest common factor**. A fraction is in **simplest form** when the only number that divides both is 1.
:::

::: example
Simplify $\frac{18}{24}$.

The GCF of 18 and 24 is 6. $18 \div 6 = 3$ and $24 \div 6 = 4$, so $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4}$.

You can also divide in smaller steps: $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{9}{12} = \frac{3}{4}$.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="4"
Simplify $\frac{6}{8}$. What is the denominator in simplest form?
solution: Divide both by 2: $\frac{3}{4}$. The denominator is **4**.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="2"
Simplify $\frac{10}{15}$. What is the numerator in simplest form?
hint: Both divide by 5.
solution: $\frac{10 \div 5}{15 \div 5} = \frac{2}{3}$. The numerator is **2**.
:::

::: problem id=x6 type=numeric answer="14" set=extra
What number should you divide by to simplify $\frac{42}{56}$ in one step?
hint: Find the GCF of 42 and 56.
solution: $42 = 2 \times 3 \times 7$ and $56 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 7$. GCF $= 2 \times 7 = 14$, giving $\frac{3}{4}$.
:::

::: problem id=x10 type=choice set=extra
Mia simplified $\frac{16}{64}$ by crossing out both 6s and wrote $\frac{1}{4}$. What is true?
- [x] Her answer is right, but crossing out digits is not a valid method
- [ ] Her answer and her method are right
- [ ] Her answer is wrong
solution: $\frac{16}{64} = \frac{16 \div 16}{64 \div 16} = \frac{1}{4}$, so the answer happens to be right. Crossing out digits fails in general: $\frac{12}{24}$ would give $\frac{1}{4}$, but it is $\frac{1}{2}$.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra
A recipe uses 250 g of sugar from a 1000 g bag. What fraction of the bag is left, in simplest form? Type the numerator.

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="5" set=extra
Simplify $\frac{60}{84}$. What is the numerator in simplest form?
hint: Divide by 2, then 2, then 3, or find the GCF 12.
solution: $\frac{60 \div 12}{84 \div 12} = \frac{5}{7}$. The numerator is **5**.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
Simplify $\frac{96}{180}$. What is the numerator in simplest form?
hint: $96 = 2^5 \times 3$ and $180 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5$.
solution: GCF $= 2^2 \times 3 = 12$. $\frac{96 \div 12}{180 \div 12} = \frac{8}{15}$. The numerator is **8**.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="5" set=extra
Of 120 students, 45 walk to school and the rest take the bus. What fraction take the bus, in simplest form? Type the numerator.
hint: First find how many take the bus.
solution: $120 - 45 = 75$. $\frac{75}{120} = \frac{5}{8}$ (GCF 15). The numerator is **5**.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="18" set=extra
A fraction simplifies to $\frac{2}{3}$, and its denominator is 27. What is its numerator?
hint: How was 3 made into 27?
solution: $3 \times 9 = 27$, so the numerator is $2 \times 9 = 18$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="4" set=extra creative=true
How many of the fractions $\frac{n}{12}$, with $n$ from 1 to 11, are already in simplest form?
hint: $n$ and 12 must share no factor.
solution: $n$ must share no factor with 12, so not divisible by 2 or 3: $n = 1, 5, 7, 11$. That is **4** fractions.
:::
