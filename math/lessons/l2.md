::: problem id=f1 type=expression answer="x^2-1" vars="x" form=factored
Factor $x^2 - 1$.
solution: $(x-1)(x+1)$
:::

::: problem id=m1 type=multi answers="2;-3"
Solve $x^2 + x - 6 = 0$.
solution: $x = 2$ or $x = -3$
:::
