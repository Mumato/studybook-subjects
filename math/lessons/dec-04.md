id: dec-04
title: Dividing decimals
---
::: rule
- **Dividing by a whole number:** divide as usual and put the decimal point in the answer straight above the one in the number.
- **Dividing by a decimal:** move the decimal point in **both** numbers to the right until the divisor is a whole number, then divide.

Dividing by 10, 100, 1000 moves the decimal point 1, 2, 3 places to the **left**.
:::

::: example
$7.2 \div 4 = 1.8$ (because $72 \div 4 = 18$, and the point stays in place).
:::

::: example
$1.5 \div 0.03$

Move both points two places: $150 \div 3 = 50$.
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=choice set=extra
Jack wrote $4.8 \div 0.6 = 0.8$. What is the right answer?
- [ ] 0.8
- [x] 8
- [ ] 80
- [ ] 0.08
solution: Move both points one place: $48 \div 6 = 8$. Check: $8 \times 0.6 = 4.8$.
:::

::: problem id=x9 type=choice set=extra
Without working it out: $6 \div 0.5$ is...
- [ ] less than 6
- [x] more than 6
- [ ] equal to 6
solution: Dividing by a number less than 1 gives more. ($6 \div 0.5 = 12$: twelve halves in 6.)
:::

::: problem id=x10 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra
A 3.6 m ribbon is cut into pieces 0.45 m long. How many pieces?
solution: $3.6 \div 0.45 = 360 \div 45 = 8$.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=numeric answer="2.45" set=extra

::: problem id=x12 type=numeric answer="8" set=extra

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="13.15" set=extra

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="1.7" set=extra
Juice comes in 1.5 L for 2.70 dollars or 2 L for 3.40 dollars. What is the price per litre of the better buy?
hint: Find the price per litre of each.
solution: $2.70 \div 1.5 = 1.80$ and $3.40 \div 2 = 1.70$. The better buy costs **1.70** dollars per litre.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="40" set=extra
$(4.2 + 1.8) \div 0.15 = ?$
hint: Brackets first.
solution: $4.2 + 1.8 = 6$, and $6 \div 0.15 = 600 \div 15 = 40$.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra creative=true
A number is divided by 0.5, and the result is divided by 0.2. The final answer is 30. What was the number?
hint: Dividing by 0.5 doubles a number. What does dividing by 0.2 do?
solution: Dividing by 0.5 multiplies by 2, dividing by 0.2 multiplies by 5: in all, times 10. So the number is $30 \div 10 = 3$.
:::
