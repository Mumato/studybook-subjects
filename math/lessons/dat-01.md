---
id: dat-01
title: Units and conversions
---
Measurements need units. To change units, multiply by a fraction that equals 1, so the old unit cancels.

::: rule
**Metric:** 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 cm = 10 mm, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 L = 1000 mL.

**US:** 1 ft = 12 in, 1 yd = 3 ft, 1 mi = 5280 ft, 1 lb = 16 oz, 1 gal = 4 qt. Also 1 in = 2.54 cm.

**Time:** 1 h = 60 min, 1 min = 60 s.

A **unit fraction** like $\frac{12 \text{ in}}{1 \text{ ft}}$ equals 1. Multiply by the one that puts the old unit on the bottom, so it cancels. Big unit to small unit: the number gets bigger. Small to big: smaller.

For a rate (like km per hour), convert the top and the bottom separately.
:::

::: example
Change 3.5 km to m.

$3.5 \text{ km} \times \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ km}} = 3500$ m.

Change 30 in to ft.

$30 \text{ in} \times \frac{1 \text{ ft}}{12 \text{ in}} = 2.5$ ft.
:::

::: example
Change 60 miles per hour to feet per second.

Top: 60 mi $= 60 \times 5280 = 316800$ ft.

Bottom: 1 h $= 3600$ s.

$316800 \div 3600 = 88$ ft per second.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="420"
How many cm are in 4.2 m?
solution: $4.2 \times 100 = 420$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="2.5"
How many kg are in 2500 g?
hint: Small unit to big unit: divide.
solution: $2500 \div 1000 = 2.5$ kg.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="60"
How many inches are in 5 ft?
solution: $5 \times 12 = 60$ in.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="12"
How many quarts are in 3 gallons?
solution: $3 \times 4 = 12$ qt.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="1.5"
How many hours are in 90 minutes?
solution: $90 \div 60 = 1.5$ h.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
To change yards to feet, which fraction do you multiply by?
- [x] $\frac{3 \text{ ft}}{1 \text{ yd}}$
- [ ] $\frac{1 \text{ yd}}{3 \text{ ft}}$
- [ ] $\frac{12 \text{ in}}{1 \text{ ft}}$
- [ ] $\frac{1 \text{ ft}}{3 \text{ yd}}$
solution: Yards must be on the bottom so they cancel, and $3 \text{ ft} = 1 \text{ yd}$.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="20"
A car goes 72 km per hour. How many metres per second is that?
hint: 72 km is 72000 m, and 1 h is 3600 s.
solution: $72000 \div 3600 = 20$ m per second.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=numeric answer="25.4"
How many cm are in 10 inches?
solution: $10 \times 2.54 = 25.4$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=x7 type=choice set=extra
Which is the longest? (1 mi is about 1609 m, 1 yd is about 0.91 m.)
- [x] 1 mile
- [ ] 1 km
- [ ] 1000 yards
- [ ] 1000 feet
solution: 1 mi ≈ 1609 m, 1 km = 1000 m, 1000 yd ≈ 914 m, 1000 ft ≈ 305 m. The mile is longest.
:::

::: problem id=x8 type=numeric answer="150" set=extra
How many minutes are in 2.5 hours?
solution: $2.5 \times 60 = 150$ min.
:::

::: problem id=x9 type=numeric answer="6" set=extra
Pia changes 600 cm to metres by multiplying by 100 and gets 60000 m. What is the right answer in m?
solution: cm are smaller than m, so the number must get smaller: $600 \div 100 = 6$ m.
:::

::: problem id=x10 type=numeric answer="3" set=extra
A recipe needs 0.75 L of milk. How many 250 mL cups is that?
solution: $0.75$ L $= 750$ mL, and $750 \div 250 = 3$ cups.
:::

::: problem id=x11 type=numeric answer="6" set=extra
A board 8 ft long is cut into pieces 16 inches long. How many pieces?
solution: $8 \times 12 = 96$ in, and $96 \div 16 = 6$ pieces.
:::

::: problem id=x12 type=numeric answer="5" set=extra
A car goes 30 miles on 1 gallon of fuel. How many gallons does a 150-mile trip use?
solution: $150 \times \frac{1 \text{ gal}}{30 \text{ mi}} = 5$ gallons.
:::

::: problem id=x13 type=numeric answer="0.36" set=extra
Cheese costs 3.60 dollars per kg. What does it cost per 100 g?
solution: 1 kg is 10 lots of 100 g: $3.60 \div 10 = 0.36$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=x14 type=numeric answer="25" set=extra
A tap runs at 1.5 L per minute. How many mL per second is that?
solution: $1.5$ L $= 1500$ mL, and 1 min $= 60$ s: $1500 \div 60 = 25$ mL per second.
:::

::: problem id=x15 type=numeric answer="20000" set=extra
How many cm² are in 2 m²?
hint: A 1 m by 1 m square is 100 cm by 100 cm.
solution: $1 \text{ m}^2 = 100 \times 100 = 10000$ cm². So $2 \text{ m}^2 = 20000$ cm².
:::

::: problem id=x16 type=numeric answer="63360" set=extra
How many inches are in 1 mile?
hint: Miles to feet, then feet to inches.
solution: $5280 \times 12 = 63360$ in.
:::

::: problem id=x17 type=numeric answer="108" set=extra
A train goes 30 m per second. How many km per hour is that?
hint: In 1 hour (3600 s) it goes $30 \times 3600$ m.
solution: $30 \times 3600 = 108000$ m per hour $= 108$ km per hour.
:::

::: problem id=x18 type=numeric answer="18" set=extra
A runner runs 400 m in 80 seconds. What is her speed in km per hour?
hint: Find metres per second first.
solution: $400 \div 80 = 5$ m/s. Then $5 \times 3600 = 18000$ m per hour $= 18$ km/h.
:::

::: problem id=x19 type=numeric answer="177.8" set=extra
Leo is 5 ft 10 in tall. How tall is he in cm?
hint: Change everything to inches first.
solution: $5 \times 12 + 10 = 70$ in. Then $70 \times 2.54 = 177.8$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=x20 type=numeric answer="5" set=extra creative=true
A snail crawls 3 mm every second. How many hours does it take to cross a 54 m path?
hint: Change 54 m to mm first.
solution: $54$ m $= 54000$ mm. Time $54000 \div 3 = 18000$ s. $18000 \div 3600 = 5$ hours.
:::
