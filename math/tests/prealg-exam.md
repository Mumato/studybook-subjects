::: problem id=e6 type=numeric answer="3"
At midnight it is $-9$ degrees. It warms 4 degrees an hour for 5 hours, then cools 8 degrees. What is the temperature now?
solution: $-9 + 5 \times 4 - 8$
$= -9 + 20 - 8 = 3$ degrees.
:::

::: problem id=e22 type=numeric answer="6"
What is the smallest integer that makes $3x - 5 \ge 11$ true?
solution: $3x \ge 16$, so $x \ge 5.33\ldots$ The smallest integer is 6.

::: problem id=e23 type=numeric answer="7"
A rectangle's length is 4 cm more than twice its width. Its perimeter is 50 cm. What is the width?
solution: $2w + 2(2w + 4) = 50$
$6w + 8 = 50$, so $w = 7$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=e24 type=numeric answer="19"
Ana has 120 dollars and saves 15 dollars a week. She needs at least 400 dollars. What is the least number of weeks she must save?
solution: $120 + 15w \ge 400$, so $15w \ge 280$ and $w \ge 18.7$. At least **19** weeks.
:::

::: problem id=e29 type=numeric answer="62"
A rectangle has a diagonal of 25 cm and one side of 7 cm. What is its perimeter?
solution: Other side: $\sqrt{625 - 49} = \sqrt{576} = 24$. Perimeter $2(7 + 24) = 62$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=e30 type=numeric answer="9pi" tolerance="0.06"
A round pool has a diameter of 8 m. A path 1 m wide goes all around it. What is the area of the path, to the nearest tenth of a m²?
solution: Pool radius 4, outer radius 5. $25\pi - 16\pi = 9\pi \approx 28.3$ m².
:::

::: problem id=e33 type=numeric answer="6"

::: problem id=e34 type=numeric answer="84.5"

::: problem id=e35 type=numeric answer="4/25"
A bag has 3 red and 2 blue balls. You draw one, put it back, and draw again. What is the probability both are blue?
solution: $\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{4}{25}$.
