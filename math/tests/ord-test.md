:::

::: problem id=t18 type=numeric answer="52"
A pack has $2^3$ stickers. You buy 5 packs and get 12 loose stickers. Work out $5 \times 2^3 + 12$. How many stickers?
solution: $2^3 = 8$. $5 \times 8 = 40$. $40 + 12 = 52$ stickers.
:::
