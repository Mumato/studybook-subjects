::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="5"
Simplify the ratio $15 : 25$. What is the second number?
solution: Divide both by 5: $3 : 5$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="36"
48 is shared in the ratio $1 : 3$. How big is the larger share?
solution: 4 parts of 12. The larger share is $3 \times 12 = 36$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="60"
A bus travels 180 km in 3 hours. What is its speed in km per hour?
solution: $180 \div 3 = 60$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="1.5"
4 pens cost 6 dollars. How much does one pen cost?
solution: $6 \div 4 = 1.5$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="3"
$\frac{x}{9} = \frac{4}{12}$. What is x?
solution: $12x = 36$, so $x = 3$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="350"
A drawing uses the scale 1 : 50. A wall is 7 cm long in the drawing. How long is the real wall in cm?
solution: $7 \times 50 = 350$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="20"
Juice and water are mixed in the ratio $1 : 4$. How many cups of water for 5 cups of juice?
solution: $5 \times 4 = 20$ cups.
:::
