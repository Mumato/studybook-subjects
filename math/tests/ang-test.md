::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="125"
What is the supplement of 55°?
solution: $180 - 55 = 125°$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="45"
Two angles on a line are x and 3x. What is x?
solution: $4x = 180$, $x = 45°$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="80"
A triangle has angles 40° and 60°. What is the third?
solution: $180 - 100 = 80°$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="50"
An isosceles triangle has a top angle of 80°. What is each base angle?
solution: $(180 - 80) \div 2 = 50°$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="12"
Similar triangles: sides 4 and 6 match sides 8 and x. What is x?
solution: Scale factor 2: $x = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="45pi"
What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 3 and height 5?
solution: $\pi \times 9 \times 5 = 45\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="72"
What is the volume of a box 2 by 4 by 9?
solution: $72$.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="63"
What is the complement of 27°?
solution: $90 - 27 = 63°$.
:::

::: problem id=t9 type=numeric answer="70"
Two vertical angles are $4x - 10$ and $2x + 30$ degrees. How big is each angle?
solution: $4x - 10 = 2x + 30$, $x = 20$. Angle $4(20) - 10 = 70°$.
:::

::: problem id=t10 type=numeric answer="122"
A line crosses two parallel lines. Between the parallel lines and on the same side of the crossing line, one angle is 58°. What is the other?
solution: These angles add to 180°: $180 - 58 = 122°$.
:::

::: problem id=t11 type=numeric answer="120"
What is each exterior angle of an equilateral triangle?
solution: $180 - 60 = 120°$.
:::

::: problem id=t12 type=numeric answer="65"
An exterior angle of a triangle is 110°. One interior angle not next to it is 45°. What is the other one?
solution: $110 - 45 = 65°$.
:::

::: problem id=t13 type=numeric answer="90"
A triangle's angles are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. What is the largest angle?
solution: 6 parts make 180°: $30°$ each. Largest $3 \times 30 = 90°$.
:::

::: problem id=t14 type=numeric answer="16"
A 2 m pole casts a 3 m shadow. At the same time a tower casts a 24 m shadow. How tall is the tower?
solution: $\frac{h}{24} = \frac{2}{3}$, $h = 16$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t15 type=numeric answer="48"
A triangle has area 3. A similar triangle has sides 4 times as long. What is its area?
solution: $3 \times 4^2 = 48$.
:::

::: problem id=t16 type=numeric answer="21pi"
What is the volume of a cone with radius 3 and height 7?
solution: $\frac{1}{3}\pi \cdot 9 \cdot 7 = 21\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=t17 type=numeric answer="32pi/3"
What is the volume of a sphere with radius 2?
solution: $\frac{4}{3}\pi \cdot 8 = \frac{32\pi}{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=t18 type=numeric answer="24"
A cone has radius 3 and the same volume as a cylinder with radius 3 and height 8. How tall is the cone?
solution: Cylinder $72\pi$. Cone $\frac{1}{3}\pi \cdot 9 \cdot h = 3\pi h = 72\pi$, so $h = 24$.
:::
