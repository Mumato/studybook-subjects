::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="125"
What is the supplement of 55°?
solution: $180 - 55 = 125°$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="45"
Two angles on a line are x and 3x. What is x?
solution: $4x = 180$, $x = 45°$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="80"
A triangle has angles 40° and 60°. What is the third?
solution: $180 - 100 = 80°$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="50"
An isosceles triangle has a top angle of 80°. What is each base angle?
solution: $(180 - 80) \div 2 = 50°$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="12"
Similar triangles: sides 4 and 6 match sides 8 and x. What is x?
solution: Scale factor 2: $x = 12$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="45pi"
What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 3 and height 5?
solution: $\pi \times 9 \times 5 = 45\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="72"
What is the volume of a box 2 by 4 by 9?
solution: $72$.
:::
