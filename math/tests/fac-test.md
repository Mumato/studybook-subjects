::: problem id=t1 type=choice
Which number divides by 9?
- [x] 7281
- [ ] 4562
- [ ] 5003
solution: Digit sums: 18, 17, 8. Only 18 divides by 9, so **7281**.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="11"
What is the smallest prime factor of 143?
solution: 143 isn't divisible by 2, 3, 5 or 7. $143 = 11 \times 13$, so **11**.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="2"
$72 = 2^3 \times 3^b$. What is b?
solution: $72 \div 8 = 9 = 3^2$, so $b = 2$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="15"
What is the GCF of 30 and 45?
solution: $30 = 2 \times 3 \times 5$, $45 = 3^2 \times 5$. Shared: $3 \times 5 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="30"
What is the LCM of 6 and 10?
solution: Multiples of 10: 10, 20, 30. 6 divides 30, so **30**.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="9"
How many factors does 36 have?
solution: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36: **9** factors.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="12"
Two lights blink together. One blinks every 4 seconds, the other every 6 seconds. After how many seconds do they next blink together?
solution: LCM of 4 and 6 is **12** seconds.
:::
