::: problem id=e19 type=numeric answer="3.2"
A tunnel entrance is half an ellipse, 10 m wide and 4 m high. How high is it 3 m from the centre?
solution: $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{16} = 1$ with $x = 3$: $\frac{y^2}{16} = \frac{16}{25}$.
$y = \frac{16}{5} = 3.2$ m.
:::

::: problem id=e20 type=numeric answer="2"
Solve $x + y + z = 3$, $2x - y + z = 0$, $x + 2y - z = 5$. What is y?
solution: $R_2 - 2R_1$: $-3y - z = -6$. $R_3 - R_1$: $y - 2z = 2$.
From the second, $y = 2 + 2z$: $-6 - 6z - z = -6$, so $z = 0$, $y = 2$, $x = 1$.
:::

::: problem id=e24 type=numeric answer="8"
2 adult and 3 child tickets cost 31 dollars. 1 adult and 2 child tickets cost 18 dollars. What does an adult ticket cost?
solution: $2a + 3k = 31$, $a + 2k = 18$.
$a = 18 - 2k$: $36 - k = 31$, $k = 5$, $a = 8$.
:::

::: problem id=e30 type=numeric answer="336000"

Total: $\frac{8}{2}(35000 + 49000) = 336000$.
