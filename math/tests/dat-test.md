::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="4.5"
How many metres are in 450 cm?
solution: $450 \div 100 = 4.5$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="48"
How many inches are in 4 ft?
solution: $4 \times 12 = 48$ in.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="3200"
How many grams are in 3.2 kg?
solution: $3.2 \times 1000 = 3200$ g.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="21"
How many feet are in 7 yards?
solution: $7 \times 3 = 21$ ft.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="15"
A cyclist rides at 54 km per hour. How many metres per second is that?
solution: $54000 \div 3600 = 15$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="30000"
How many cm² are in 3 m²?
solution: $1 \text{ m}^2 = 10000$ cm², so $3 \text{ m}^2 = 30000$ cm².
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="9"
Find the mean of 8, 12, 5, 11, 9.
solution: $45 \div 5 = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="10"
Find the median of 14, 3, 9, 20, 6, 11.
solution: Sorted: 3, 6, 9, 11, 14, 20. $\frac{9 + 11}{2} = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=t9 type=numeric answer="8"
Find the mode of 5, 8, 3, 8, 5, 8.
solution: 8 appears three times: **8**.
:::

::: problem id=t10 type=numeric answer="19"
Find the range of 17, $-2$, 9, 4.
solution: $17 - (-2) = 19$.
:::

::: problem id=t11 type=numeric answer="84"
Quizzes count 40% (score 75) and the exam counts 60% (score 90). What is the weighted mean?
solution: $0.4(75) + 0.6(90) = 30 + 54 = 84$.
:::

::: problem id=t12 type=numeric answer="8"
Five numbers have a mean of 7. Four of them are 6, 9, 4 and 8. What is the fifth?
solution: Total $5 \times 7 = 35$. $35 - 27 = 8$.
:::

::: problem id=t13 type=choice
Data: 3, 4, 4, 5, 40. Which measure gives the best typical value?
- [x] The median
- [ ] The mean
- [ ] The range
solution: 40 is an outlier. The mean is $56 \div 5 = 11.2$, above almost every value. The median, 4, is typical.
:::

::: problem id=t14 type=numeric answer="1/3"
You roll a die. What is the probability of a multiple of 3?
solution: 3 and 6: $\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=t15 type=numeric answer="3/10"
A bag has 7 red and 3 white balls. What is $P(\text{not red})$?
solution: $1 - \frac{7}{10} = \frac{3}{10}$.
:::

::: problem id=t16 type=numeric answer="24"
You flip two coins and roll a die. How many outcomes are there?
solution: $2 \times 2 \times 6 = 24$.
:::

::: problem id=t17 type=numeric answer="5/36"
You roll two dice. What is the probability the sum is 8?
solution: (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2): $\frac{5}{36}$.
:::

::: problem id=t18 type=numeric answer="0.64"
A player makes 80% of her free throws, each shot independent of the others. What is the probability she makes two in a row?
solution: $0.8 \times 0.8 = 0.64$.
:::
