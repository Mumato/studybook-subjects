::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="9"
What is the radius of $x^2 + y^2 = 81$?
solution: $\sqrt{81} = 9$.

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="5"
What is the radius of $x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 6y - 15 = 0$?
solution: $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 15 + 1 + 9 = 25$: radius 5.
:::

::: problem id=t18 type=numeric answer="3"
