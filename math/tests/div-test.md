::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="214"
$642 \div 3 = ?$
solution: 6 ÷ 3 = 2, 4 ÷ 3 = 1 r 1, 12 ÷ 3 = 4. Answer: **214**. Check: $214 \times 3 = 642$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="69"
$1035 \div 15 = ?$
solution: 103 ÷ 15 = 6 ($90$), $103 - 90 = 13$. 135 ÷ 15 = 9. Answer: **69**. Check: $69 \times 15 = 1035$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="3"
What is the remainder of $59 \div 8$?
solution: $8 \times 7 = 56$, $59 - 56 = 3$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="16"
$100 \div 6 = ?$ remainder something. What is the answer before the remainder?
solution: $6 \times 16 = 96$, $100 - 96 = 4$. So **16** remainder 4.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="9"
83 books go on shelves that hold 10 books each. How many shelves are needed?
solution: $83 \div 10 = 8$ remainder 3. The last 3 books need one more shelf: **9** shelves.
:::
