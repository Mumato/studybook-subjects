::: problem id=e33 type=numeric answer="42"
What is the LCM of 14 and 21?
solution: $14 = 2 \times 7$, $21 = 3 \times 7$. LCM $= 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.
:::

::: problem id=e34 type=numeric answer="5"
84 red roses and 126 white roses are made into identical bunches, as many as possible, with none left over. How many roses are in each bunch?
solution: Bunches: GCF of 84 and 126 = 42. Each bunch: $84 \div 42 = 2$ red and $126 \div 42 = 3$ white, so **5** roses.
:::

::: problem id=e35 type=numeric answer="8"
Two lighthouses flash together now. One flashes every 18 seconds and the other every 24 seconds. How many more times do they flash together in the next 10 minutes?
solution: LCM of 18 and 24 is 72 seconds. 10 minutes = 600 s. $600 \div 72 = 8$ remainder 24, so **8** times.
:::
