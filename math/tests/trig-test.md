::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="8/17"
A right triangle has sides 8, 15, 17. What is the sine of the angle opposite 8?
solution: $\frac{8}{17}$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="15/8"
Same triangle: what is the tangent of the angle opposite 15?
solution: Opposite 15, adjacent 8: $\frac{15}{8}$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="1/2"
$\cos 60° = ?$
solution: $\frac{1}{2}$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="7"
The hypotenuse is 14 and one angle is 30°. How long is the side opposite the 30°?
solution: $14 \times \frac{1}{2} = 7$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="60"
$\cos A = \frac{1}{2}$. What is A in degrees?
solution: $60°$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="3sqrt(2)"
A 45-45-90 triangle has legs of 3. How long is the hypotenuse? (exact)
solution: $3\sqrt{2}$.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="sqrt(3)"
$\tan 60° = ?$ (exact)
solution: $\sqrt{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="sqrt(2)/2"
$\sin 45° = ?$ (exact)
solution: $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
:::

::: problem id=t9 type=numeric answer="5sqrt(3)"
In a 30-60-90 triangle the short side is 5. How long is the longer leg? (exact)
solution: $5\sqrt{3}$.
:::

::: problem id=t10 type=numeric answer="30"
A tree is 15 m tall. You stand $15\sqrt{3}$ m from its foot. What is the angle of elevation to the top, in degrees?
solution: $\tan A = \frac{15}{15\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, so $A = 30°$.
:::

::: problem id=t11 type=numeric answer="8.45" tolerance="0.01"
Use a calculator in degree mode. A right triangle has hypotenuse 20 and an angle of 25°. How long is the side opposite the 25°? (to hundredths)
solution: $20 \sin 25° \approx 8.45$.
:::

::: problem id=t12 type=numeric answer="6sqrt(2)" tolerance="0.01"
In triangle ABC, $A = 30°$, $B = 45°$ and $a = 6$. Find b. (exact or to hundredths)
solution: $b = \frac{6 \sin 45°}{\sin 30°} = \frac{6 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\frac{1}{2}} = 6\sqrt{2} \approx 8.49$.
:::

::: problem id=t13 type=numeric answer="30"
A triangle has sides 10 and 12 with a 30° angle between them. What is its area?
solution: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 12 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 30$.
:::

::: problem id=t14 type=numeric answer="2"
In triangle ABC, $A = 30°$, $a = 4$ and $b = 6$. How many different triangles fit these measurements?
solution: $\sin B = \frac{6 \cdot \frac{1}{2}}{4} = 0.75$: $B \approx 48.59°$ or $131.41°$. $30 + 131.41 < 180$, so **2**.
:::

::: problem id=t15 type=numeric answer="sqrt(19)" tolerance="0.01"
In triangle ABC, $a = 3$, $b = 5$ and $C = 60°$. Find c. (exact or to hundredths)
solution: $c^2 = 9 + 25 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 19$, $c = \sqrt{19} \approx 4.36$.
:::

::: problem id=t16 type=numeric answer="120"
A triangle has sides 7, 8 and 13. What is its largest angle in degrees?
solution: $\cos = \frac{49 + 64 - 169}{2 \cdot 7 \cdot 8} = -\frac{1}{2}$: $120°$.
:::

::: problem id=t17 type=numeric answer="36"
What is the area of a triangle with sides 9, 10 and 17?
solution: Heron: $s = 18$. $\sqrt{18 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 1} = \sqrt{1296} = 36$.
:::

::: problem id=t18 type=numeric answer="14"
Two hikers leave the same point. One walks 6 km, the other 10 km, along paths 120° apart. How far apart are they?
solution: $d^2 = 36 + 100 - 120\cos 120°$ $= 136 + 60 = 196$, $d = 14$ km.
:::
