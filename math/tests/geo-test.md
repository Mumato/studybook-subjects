::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="12"
$\sqrt{144} = ?$
solution: $12 \times 12 = 144$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="34"
What is the perimeter of a rectangle 6 cm by 11 cm?
solution: $6 + 11 + 6 + 11 = 34$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="36"
What is the area of a triangle with base 9 and height 8?
solution: $\frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 8 = 36$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="36pi"
What is the area of a circle with radius 6? Give it in terms of π.
solution: $\pi \times 6^2 = 36\pi$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="20pi" tolerance="0.06"
A circle has a diameter of 20 cm. What is its circumference, to the nearest tenth?
solution: $\pi \times 20 \approx 62.8$ cm.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="15"
A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. How long is the hypotenuse?
solution: $81 + 144 = 225$, so 15.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="15"
A right triangle has hypotenuse 17 and one leg 8. How long is the other leg?
solution: $289 - 64 = 225$, so 15.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Between which two whole numbers is $\sqrt{40}$?
- [x] 6 and 7

solution: $6^2 = 36$ and $7^2 = 49$.
