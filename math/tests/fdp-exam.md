::: problem id=e1 type=numeric answer="15"
$\frac{3}{7} = \frac{?}{35}$. What is the missing number?
solution: $7 \times 5 = 35$, so $3 \times 5 = 15$.
:::

::: problem id=e2 type=numeric answer="5"
Simplify $\frac{36}{60}$. What is the denominator in simplest form?
solution: GCF 12: $\frac{36 \div 12}{60 \div 12} = \frac{3}{5}$. The denominator is **5**.
:::

::: problem id=e3 type=choice
Which is bigger?
- [ ] $\frac{5}{9}$
- [x] $\frac{4}{7}$
- [ ] They are equal
solution: Cross-multiply: $5 \times 7 = 35$ and $9 \times 4 = 36$. So $\frac{4}{7}$ is bigger.
:::

:::

::: problem id=e13 type=numeric answer="6"
You have $4\frac{1}{2}$ cups of flour. Each batch of muffins uses $\frac{3}{4}$ cup. How many batches can you make?
solution: $\frac{9}{2} \div \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} = 6$.
:::

::: problem id=e14 type=numeric answer="1440"
Leo spends $\frac{1}{3}$ of his pay on rent and $\frac{1}{4}$ on food. He has 600 dollars left. How much is his pay?
solution: Spent: $\frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$, so $\frac{5}{12}$ is left. $600 \div \frac{5}{12} = 1440$ dollars.
:::

::: problem id=e29 type=numeric answer="72"

::: problem id=e34 type=numeric answer="10"
You walk 6 km in 1.5 hours. At the same speed, how far do you walk in 2.5 hours?
solution: Speed: $6 \div 1.5 = 4$ km per hour. In 2.5 hours: $4 \times 2.5 = 10$ km.
:::

::: problem id=e35 type=numeric answer="700"
A mix uses flour and sugar in the ratio $5 : 2$. You have 600 g of flour and 200 g of sugar. What is the largest amount of mix, in grams, you can make?
solution: Sugar runs out first: 200 g is 2 parts, so a part is 100 g. Flour needed: 500 g (you have enough). Mix: $500 + 200 = 700$ g.
:::
