::: problem id=e9 type=numeric answer="7"
A rock is thrown from a 35 m cliff. Its height is $h = -5t^2 + 30t + 35$ metres. After how many seconds does it hit the ground?
solution: $-5t^2 + 30t + 35 = 0$, so $t^2 - 6t - 7 = 0$, $(t - 7)(t + 1) = 0$. Time is positive: **7**.
:::
