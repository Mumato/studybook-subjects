## Welcome to Math

This book teaches you math one small step at a time.

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**. Type your answer and press Enter: the box turns green when you're right and red when you're not. Stuck? Ask for a hint, or open the worked solution.

Chapters end with a **test**. Pass it with 80% and the chapter is complete. Already know a topic? Take its test straight away.

You can type answers like `3/4`, `0.75`, `2x+1` or `sqrt(2)`.
