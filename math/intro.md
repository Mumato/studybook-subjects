## Welcome to Math

This book takes you from multiplication and division all the way to calculus, one small step at a time.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking. They don't block your progress.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test**. Score 80% or more and the chapter is complete. Already know a topic? Take its test straight away and skip ahead.

### Typing answers

You can type `3/4`, `0.75`, `2 1/3` (a mixed number), `25%`, `$4.50`, `2x+1`, `x^2` and `sqrt(2)`. Equivalent answers count: `1/2`, `0.5` and `2/4` are all right.

### The course

- Part 1: Arithmetic
- Part 2: Fractions, decimals and percents
- Part 3: Pre-algebra
- Parts 4 to 9 (Algebra, Geometry and trigonometry, Precalculus, Calculus I and II) arrive as they are written. Your book downloads them by itself.
