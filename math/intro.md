## Welcome to Math

This book takes you from multiplication and division all the way to calculus, one small step at a time. It covers what university courses from pre-algebra to Calculus II cover, with the same amount of practice.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking. They don't block your progress.
- After the practice comes **Extra practice**: about 20 more problems, from quick drills to a few hard ones. It's the homework set. You don't need it to finish the lesson, but it's how the skill sticks.

### Endless practice

Chapters with skills that need repetition (times tables, fractions, equations, derivatives, ...) have an **Endless practice** page on the contents page: new problems every time, and turning past the last page makes another set. It doesn't count towards your progress, so practise as much as you like.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test** of about 18 problems. Score 80% or more and the chapter is complete. Already know a topic? Take its test straight away and skip ahead.

Each part ends with an **exam** of about 35 problems covering the whole part, like a university final.

### Typing answers

You can type `3/4`, `0.75`, `2 1/3` (a mixed number), `25%`, `2x+1`, `x^2`, `sqrt(2)`, `pi`, `ln(5)`, `e^x` and `xcos(x)`. Equivalent answers count: `1/2`, `0.5` and `2/4` are all right. Word answers like `yes` or `or` ignore capital letters. A degree sign is fine: `55°` and `55` both work.

Some problems show a **graph**: click on it to place a point, or click the parts of a shape to shade them, then press **Check**.

### The course

- Part 1: Arithmetic
- Part 2: Fractions, decimals and percents
- Part 3: Pre-algebra (with data, measurement and probability)
- Part 4: Algebra I
- Part 5: Algebra II (with complex numbers)
- Part 6: Geometry and trigonometry (with the laws of sines and cosines, inverse trig and trig formulas)
- Part 7: Precalculus (with conic sections, matrices, the binomial theorem, vectors, parametric and polar)
- Part 8: Calculus I (limits, derivatives, related rates, L'Hôpital's rule, curve sketching, integrals)
- Part 9: Calculus II (integration techniques, applications, parametric and polar calculus, series)

New lessons and fixes download into your book by themselves.
