## Welcome to STAT101

This book covers a first university course in **Introductory Statistics**: how to describe data, how chance works, and how to draw honest conclusions about a whole population from a sample. You don't need any background beyond basic algebra. Each chapter notes the matching chapter of the free OpenStax textbook *Introductory Statistics*, if you want to read more.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**, or click an option. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking.

### Typing answers

- **Probabilities:** type a decimal like `0.25` or a fraction like `1/4`. Round to the places the question asks for.
- **Percents:** type the number, like `25` or `25%` for 25%.
- No tables needed: any z, t or chi-square value a problem needs is given in the problem. A calculator helps.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test**. Score 80% to complete the chapter.

### The course

- Part 1: Data and sampling
- Part 2: Describing data (center, spread, z-scores)
- Part 3: Probability
- Part 4: Discrete random variables (binomial)
- Part 5: The normal distribution
- Midterm practice: two practice exams on Parts 1-5
- Part 6: Sampling distributions and the central limit theorem
- Part 7: Confidence intervals
- Part 8: Hypothesis tests
- Part 9: Comparing two groups
- Part 10: Chi-square tests
- Part 11: Correlation and regression
- Part 12: One-way ANOVA
- Final practice: two cumulative practice exams
