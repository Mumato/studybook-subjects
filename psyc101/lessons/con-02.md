id: con-02
title: Drugs and altered states
---
Psychoactive drugs change consciousness by acting at synapses: copying, boosting or blocking neurotransmitters. Hypnosis and meditation change it without drugs. (OpenStax Psychology 2e ch. 4)

::: rule
- **Depressants** slow the nervous system: alcohol, sedatives, and **opioids** (heroin, morphine), which also relieve pain.
- **Stimulants** speed it up: caffeine, nicotine, cocaine, amphetamines.
- **Hallucinogens** distort perception: LSD, psilocybin ("magic mushrooms").
- **Tolerance:** with repeated use, more of the drug is needed for the same effect. **Withdrawal:** unpleasant symptoms when stopping. **Dependence** (addiction): continued use despite harm.
- **Hypnosis** is focused attention with heightened suggestibility; it can reduce pain but can't make people act against their values or reliably recover memories. **Meditation** trains attention and can lower stress.
:::

::: example
A regular coffee drinker finds one cup no longer keeps them alert (**tolerance**). When they skip coffee for a day, they get a headache and feel tired (**withdrawal**). Caffeine is a mild stimulant, but the same pattern, much stronger, drives addiction to more powerful drugs.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
Alcohol is classified as a:
- [x] depressant
- [ ] stimulant
- [ ] hallucinogen
solution: It slows nervous system activity, even though small amounts can make people feel less inhibited.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Which of these is a stimulant?
- [ ] heroin
- [x] nicotine
- [ ] LSD
- [ ] alcohol
solution: Nicotine increases arousal and heart rate.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=text answers="tolerance"
Someone needs more and more of a drug to get the same effect. What is this called?
solution: Tolerance: the body adapts to the drug.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Shaking, sweating and anxiety after stopping a drug that the body has adapted to are signs of:
- [ ] tolerance
- [x] withdrawal
- [ ] hallucination
solution: Withdrawal symptoms are often the opposite of the drug's effects.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
LSD and psilocybin are:
- [ ] depressants
- [ ] stimulants
- [x] hallucinogens
solution: They distort perception and can cause hallucinations.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Which is a reliable finding about hypnosis?
- [ ] It can make people do things against their values.
- [ ] It recovers accurate hidden memories.
- [x] It can help reduce the experience of pain.
solution: Hypnosis does help with pain for many people, but "recovered" memories under hypnosis are often false.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice creative=true
Why is it especially dangerous to mix alcohol with sleeping pills?
- [ ] They cancel each other out.
- [x] Both are depressants, so together they can slow breathing and heart rate to a deadly degree.
- [ ] Sleeping pills turn alcohol into a stimulant.
solution: Drugs from the same class add up. Many overdose deaths involve combining depressants, such as alcohol with opioids or sedatives.
:::
