title: Stress and health
---
Stress helped our ancestors escape predators. Today's stressors (deadlines, money, traffic) trigger the same body response, sometimes for months, and that takes a toll on health. (OpenStax Psychology 2e ch. 14)

::: rule
- A **stressor** is anything we see as threatening or demanding. Big life changes (even happy ones) and everyday hassles both add up.
- **Selye's general adaptation syndrome:** **alarm** (fight or flight), **resistance** (the body stays mobilised) and, if stress goes on, **exhaustion** (resources run down, illness more likely).
- Long-term stress raises **cortisol** and weakens the **immune system**. Hostility (part of the "Type A" pattern) is linked with heart disease.
- **Coping:** **problem-focused** coping tackles the cause (making a study plan); **emotion-focused** coping manages the feelings (talking to a friend, relaxing). **Social support**, exercise and a sense of control all protect health.
:::

::: example
The **Social Readjustment Rating Scale** (Holmes and Rahe) gives life events points: death of a spouse 100, marriage 50, moving house 20. A student who gets married (50) and moves (20) in one year has 70 points of change: more change, more risk of stress-related illness.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="82"
On the Social Readjustment Rating Scale, marriage scores 50, moving house 20 and the holiday season 12. What is the total for someone who experiences all three in a year?
solution: $50 + 20 + 12 = 82$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
In Selye's general adaptation syndrome, which stage comes last if stress continues?
- [ ] alarm
- [ ] resistance
- [x] exhaustion
solution: After a long resistance stage, the body's resources run down.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Making a schedule to handle a heavy workload is:
- [x] problem-focused coping
- [ ] emotion-focused coping
- [ ] the alarm stage
solution: It tackles the source of the stress.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Going for a run to calm down after a breakup is:
- [ ] problem-focused coping
- [x] emotion-focused coping
- [ ] the exhaustion stage
solution: It manages the emotional reaction rather than changing the situation.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Students tend to catch more colds during exam weeks. The best explanation is:
- [x] long-lasting stress weakens the immune system
- [ ] exams spread viruses
- [ ] stress has no effect on the body
solution: Prolonged stress hormones like cortisol suppress immune function.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Which part of the "Type A" personality pattern is most strongly linked with heart disease?
- [ ] being organised
- [x] hostility and anger
- [ ] working long hours
solution: Research points to hostility as the "toxic" core.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=text answers="cortisol"
Which stress hormone, released by the adrenal glands, rises with long-term stress?
solution: Cortisol.
:::
