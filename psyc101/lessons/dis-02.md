title: Mood disorders, schizophrenia and others
---
Some disorders affect mood, others thinking and perception, others how a person relates to people throughout life. All are illnesses, not personal failings, and most can be treated. (OpenStax Psychology 2e ch. 15)

::: rule
- **Major depressive disorder:** at least **two weeks** of low mood or loss of interest, with changes in sleep, appetite and energy, feelings of worthlessness, trouble concentrating, and sometimes thoughts of death.
- **Bipolar disorder:** episodes of depression alternating with **mania** (high energy, little need for sleep, racing thoughts, risky behaviour).
- **Schizophrenia:** **positive symptoms** (added experiences: **hallucinations**, **delusions**, disorganised speech) and **negative symptoms** (things lost: flat emotion, little motivation or speech). Overactive **dopamine** activity is involved. It is **not** "split personality".
- **Dissociative identity disorder** (two or more identities) is rare and debated.
- **Personality disorders** are long-lasting, rigid patterns, e.g. **antisocial** (disregard for others' rights) and **borderline** (unstable mood, relationships and self-image).
- **Neurodevelopmental disorders** begin in childhood: **ADHD**, **autism spectrum disorder**.
:::

::: example
A university student has felt empty for a month: she sleeps 12 hours yet is exhausted, has stopped seeing friends, can't focus, and feels worthless. This matches **major depressive disorder**. Therapy, medication or both help most people. If you or someone you know is thinking about suicide, contact a local crisis line or emergency services right away: help works.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="2"
For a diagnosis of major depressive disorder, symptoms must last at least how many weeks?
solution: At least 2 weeks.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
A man hasn't slept for three days, feels he could run the country, talks very fast and spends all his savings on a whim. He is probably in:
- [x] a manic episode
- [ ] a depressive episode
- [ ] a panic attack
solution: Mania is the "high" phase of bipolar disorder.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Hearing voices that aren't there is:
- [x] a hallucination, a positive symptom of schizophrenia
- [ ] a delusion, a negative symptom
- [ ] a sign of dissociative identity disorder
solution: Hallucinations are false perceptions; delusions are false beliefs.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Firmly believing, against all evidence, that you're being followed by spies is a:
- [ ] hallucination
- [x] delusion
- [ ] compulsion
solution: A delusion is a fixed false belief.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which is a **negative** symptom of schizophrenia?
- [ ] hearing voices
- [ ] disorganised speech
- [x] flat, expressionless emotion
solution: Negative symptoms are normal functions that are reduced or lost.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
"Schizophrenia means having a split personality." This is:
- [ ] true
- [x] false: schizophrenia involves a split from reality, not multiple personalities
solution: Multiple identities describe dissociative identity disorder, which is a different and rare condition.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
A person who repeatedly lies, breaks laws, and shows no remorse for hurting others, since adolescence, fits:
- [x] antisocial personality disorder
- [ ] borderline personality disorder
- [ ] ADHD
solution: A lasting disregard for others' rights.
:::
