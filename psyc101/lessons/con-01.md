title: Sleep and dreams
---
You spend about a third of your life asleep. Far from switching off, the sleeping brain cycles through distinct stages, each with its own job. (OpenStax Psychology 2e ch. 4)

::: rule
- **Circadian rhythm:** the body's roughly 24-hour clock, set mainly by light. When it gets dark, the brain releases **melatonin**, making you sleepy.
- Sleep runs in **cycles of about 90 minutes**: light sleep (**N1**, **N2**), deep slow-wave sleep (**N3**), then **REM** (rapid eye movement) sleep.
- **REM sleep:** the eyes dart, the brain is very active, most vivid **dreaming** happens, and the body's muscles are paralysed. REM periods get **longer** later in the night; deep N3 sleep comes mostly early.
- Sleep disorders: **insomnia** (trouble falling or staying asleep), **sleep apnea** (breathing stops repeatedly), **narcolepsy** (sudden sleep attacks).
- Dream theories: Freud saw dreams as hidden wishes; the **activation-synthesis** theory says the brain weaves random REM activity into a story.
:::

::: example
Someone sleeps 7.5 hours. That's $7.5 \times 60 = 450$ minutes, or $450 / 90 = 5$ cycles. Waking at the end of a cycle, in light sleep, feels much easier than being woken from deep N3 sleep.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="4"
A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes. How many full cycles fit in 6 hours of sleep?
solution: $6 \times 60 = 360$ minutes; $360 / 90 = 4$ cycles.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
During which stage do most vivid dreams happen?
- [ ] N1
- [ ] N3
- [x] REM
solution: REM sleep brings the most vivid, story-like dreams.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
During REM sleep, the body's muscles are:
- [ ] tense and twitching
- [x] mostly paralysed
- [ ] the same as when awake
solution: This paralysis stops us acting out our dreams.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Looking at the night of sleep in the picture, when are REM periods longest?
![](assets/con-sleep.3x.png)
- [ ] early in the night
- [x] late in the night, toward morning
- [ ] they are all the same length
solution: REM periods lengthen through the night, while deep N3 sleep mostly happens in the first cycles.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=text answers="melatonin"
Which hormone, released as it gets dark, makes you feel sleepy?
solution: Melatonin. Bright screens at night delay its release.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
A man snores loudly and stops breathing many times each night, waking briefly each time. He probably has:
- [ ] insomnia
- [ ] narcolepsy
- [x] sleep apnea
solution: In sleep apnea the airway closes; the person wakes slightly to breathe, often without remembering.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
The activation-synthesis theory says dreams are:
- [ ] disguised wishes from the unconscious
- [x] the brain's attempt to make sense of random neural activity during REM sleep
- [ ] messages about the future
solution: The brain "synthesises" a story from activity it's generating anyway.
:::
