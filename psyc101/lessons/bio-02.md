title: The nervous and endocrine systems
---
Neurons are organised into systems: one that thinks and decides, others that carry messages to and from the body, and a slower chemical system of hormones. (OpenStax Psychology 2e ch. 3)

::: rule
- The **central nervous system** (CNS) is the brain and spinal cord. The **peripheral nervous system** (PNS) is all the other nerves.
- The PNS has a **somatic** part (voluntary movement and senses) and an **autonomic** part (automatic functions like heartbeat and digestion).
- The autonomic system has two branches: the **sympathetic** nervous system arouses the body for **"fight or flight"**, while the **parasympathetic** system calms it ("rest and digest").
- The **endocrine system** sends **hormones** through the blood. It's slower than nerves but lasts longer. The **pituitary** is the "master gland"; the **adrenal glands** release adrenaline and cortisol under stress.
- A **reflex** (like pulling your hand off a hot stove) runs through the spinal cord before the brain even knows.
:::

::: example
A dog lunges at you. Your **sympathetic** system kicks in: heart pounding, pupils wide, breathing fast, digestion paused, adrenaline pumping. Once the owner pulls the dog away, your **parasympathetic** system slowly brings everything back to normal.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
The brain and spinal cord make up the:
- [x] central nervous system
- [ ] peripheral nervous system
- [ ] autonomic nervous system
- [ ] endocrine system
solution: Everything else is peripheral.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
You're about to give a speech and your heart races and your mouth goes dry. Which branch is active?
- [x] sympathetic
- [ ] parasympathetic
- [ ] somatic
solution: The sympathetic nervous system prepares the body for action.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
After a big meal, you feel relaxed while your body digests. Which branch is in charge?
- [ ] sympathetic
- [x] parasympathetic
- [ ] somatic
solution: "Rest and digest" is the parasympathetic branch.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Choosing to wave your hand uses the:
- [x] somatic nervous system
- [ ] autonomic nervous system
- [ ] endocrine system
solution: Voluntary movement of skeletal muscles is somatic.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=text answers="pituitary;pituitary gland;the pituitary;the pituitary gland"
Which gland is called the "master gland" because it controls many other glands?
solution: The pituitary, which is itself directed by the hypothalamus.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Compared with the nervous system, the endocrine system's messages are:
- [ ] faster and shorter-lasting
- [x] slower but longer-lasting
- [ ] exactly the same
solution: Hormones travel through the blood, so they take longer to arrive but their effects last.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
You touch a hot pan and jerk your hand away before you even feel pain. Why so fast?
- [ ] The brain makes the decision instantly.
- [x] It's a reflex: the spinal cord sends the "move" signal without waiting for the brain.
- [ ] Hormones move the hand.
solution: The pain message reaches the brain a moment later, which is why you feel it after you've already moved.
:::
