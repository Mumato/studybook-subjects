## Welcome to PSYC101

This book covers a first university course in **Introduction to Psychology**: how the brain and mind shape what we sense, learn, remember, feel and do, and how psychologists find out. You don't need any background. Each lesson notes the matching chapter of the free OpenStax textbook *Psychology 2e* if you want to read more.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule** (the key ideas), **a worked example** and then **practice problems**, with pictures wherever they help.

- Click an option, or type a word and press **Enter**. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Typed answers ignore capital letters: `hippocampus` and `Hippocampus` both count.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking.
- Got something wrong? It goes on your **Review mistakes** list on the contents page, to practise until it sticks.

### Tests

Each chapter ends with a **test**. Score 80% to complete the chapter. Before a test, read the chapter's **Summary**.

### The course

- Part 1: Psychology as a science (history, research methods, statistics, ethics)
- Part 2: Biopsychology (neurons, nervous system, brain)
- Part 3: Sensation and perception
- Part 4: Consciousness (sleep, dreams, drugs)
- Part 5: Learning (classical and operant conditioning)
- Midterm practice: two practice exams on Parts 1-5
- Part 6: Memory
- Part 7: Thinking, language and intelligence
- Part 8: Lifespan development
- Part 9: Motivation, emotion and stress
- Part 10: Personality
- Part 11: Social psychology
- Part 12: Psychological disorders and therapy
- Final practice: two cumulative practice exams
