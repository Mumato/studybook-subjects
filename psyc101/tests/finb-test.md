::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="9"
What is the mean of 5, 7, 9, 11, 13?
solution: $(5+7+9+11+13)/5 = 45/5 = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
A person who can't form new long-term memories after hippocampus damage has:
- [x] anterograde amnesia
- [ ] retrograde amnesia
- [ ] proactive interference
solution: New memories can't be formed.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
A rat presses a lever and gets food after an unpredictable number of presses. This schedule is:
- [ ] fixed-ratio
- [x] variable-ratio
- [ ] fixed-interval
solution: Unpredictable count: variable-ratio.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="130"
IQ has a mean of 100 and an SD of 15. What IQ is 2 standard deviations above the mean?
solution: $100 + 2 \times 15 = 130$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
In Maslow's hierarchy, which need is most basic?
- [x] physiological
- [ ] esteem
- [ ] belonging
solution: Food, water and sleep come first.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
Blaming your poor test result on a noisy room, but crediting your good results to your talent, is the:
- [ ] fundamental attribution error
- [x] self-serving bias
- [ ] bystander effect
solution: Success to yourself, failure to the situation.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Hallucinations and delusions are symptoms most associated with:
- [ ] generalised anxiety disorder
- [x] schizophrenia
- [ ] OCD
solution: They are positive symptoms of schizophrenia.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Which brain area is most involved in fear?
- [ ] cerebellum
- [x] amygdala
- [ ] occipital lobe
solution: The amygdala detects threats and triggers fear.
:::
