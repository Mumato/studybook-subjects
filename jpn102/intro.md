## Welcome to JPN102

This book is the second semester of university **Elementary Japanese**. It continues straight on from **JPN101**: you should be able to read hiragana and katakana, use the polite ます and です forms, and know about 60 kanji. If any of that feels shaky, review JPN101 first: the two books share the same style.

This semester is where Japanese opens up. The **て-form** lets you make requests, ask permission and link actions; **plain forms** let you talk casually with friends and build long sentences; and you'll learn to compare, give and receive, talk about plans and obligations, set conditions, and say what you **can** do. You'll also learn about **80 more kanji**, for roughly 140 in total: the usual first-year set.

### How it works (same as JPN101)

- Type Japanese answers in **romaji**: `tabete` → たべて. Particles can be typed as they sound (`wa`, `e`, `o`).
- Click **▶** to hear any Japanese; listening questions show **▶ Listen** with the words hidden.
- Speaking items: **● Record**, **▶ Me** to hear yourself, and **✔ Check** to have the book listen for the right words.
- Writing drills: draw each kanji with the mouse; **▶ Strokes** shows the order.
- Kanji you haven't learned yet appear with **furigana** (small kana above them).

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **an example** and **practice problems**. Each part ends with a **test**, and there's a **midterm review** after Part 5 and a **final review** at the end, mixing everything, like a real course.

### The course

- Part 1: The て-form: requests, permission, linking actions
- Part 2: ～ている: actions in progress and states
- Part 3: Plain forms and casual speech
- Part 4: Describing and connecting: joining adjectives, describing nouns with verbs, ～たり, ～ことがある
- Part 5: Comparisons and advice
- Midterm review
- Part 6: Plans, obligations and guesses
- Part 7: Giving and receiving
- Part 8: Time, reasons and conditions: ～とき, ～たら, ～ので, ～んです
- Part 9: Ability and change: the potential form and ～なる
- Part 10: Kanji: about 80 more
- Final review
