id: kan-03
title: Kanji: time and weather
---
Kanji for routines and weather: you'll see these on every calendar and forecast.

::: rule
- Time: **毎** every (!say[まいにち] 毎日), **朝** !say[あさ] morning, **夜** !say[よる] night, **昔** !say[むかし] long ago, **早** !say[はやい] early, **間** between (!say[じかん] 時間 time), **次** !say[つぎ] next, **度** degrees, times (!say[いちど] 一度 once).
- Weather: **天** heaven, sky (!say[てんき] 天気 weather), **気** spirit (!say[げんき] 元気 healthy, well), **元** origin, **雨** !say[あめ] rain, **雪** !say[ゆき] snow.
- Special readings: !say[けさ] 今朝 this morning, !say[こんや] 今夜 tonight, !say[まいあさ] 毎朝 every morning.
- 気 is in many words about feelings and conditions: 天気 (weather), 元気 (energy), 気分 (mood).
:::

::: example
A weather forecast:

{明日|あした}の{天気|てんき}：{朝|あさ}は{雨|あめ}、{夜|よる}は{雪|ゆき}でしょう。{気温|きおん}は{二度|にど}です。

!say[あしたの てんき、あさは あめ、よるは ゆきでしょう。きおんは にどです。]

*Tomorrow's weather: rain in the morning, probably snow at night. The temperature will be 2 degrees.*
:::
