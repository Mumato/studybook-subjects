---
id: kan-01
title: Kanji: directions, places and travel
---
JPN101 gave you about 60 kanji. This part adds about 80 more, starting with the ones on every map, station sign and train ticket. Remember: most kanji have a Japanese (kun) reading used alone and a Chinese-style (on) reading used in compounds.

::: rule
- Directions: **東** !say[ひがし] east (とう in 東京), **西** !say[にし] west, **南** !say[みなみ] south, **北** !say[きた] north.
- Places: **国** !say[くに] country (こく in 外国), **京** capital (!say[きょう] in 東京), **町** !say[まち] town, **市** city (!say[し]: !say[しやくしょ] 市役所 city hall), **道** !say[みち] road, **所** !say[ところ] place.
- Travel: **車** !say[くるま] car (しゃ in 電車), **電** electricity (!say[でんき] 電気), **旅** journey (!say[りょこう] 旅行 trip), **会** !say[あう] meet (!say[かいしゃ] 会社 company), **社** company, shrine.
- Compounds you can now read: **東京** !say[とうきょう], **外国** !say[がいこく] foreign country, **電車** !say[でんしゃ] train, **会社** !say[かいしゃ], **南口** !say[みなみぐち] south exit.
:::

::: example
Station signs:

{東口|ひがしぐち}　{西口|にしぐち}　{南口|みなみぐち}　{北口|きたぐち}

!say[ひがしぐち、にしぐち、みなみぐち、きたぐち]

*East exit, west exit, south exit, north exit.* Big stations like {新宿|しんじゅく} have all four: meeting a friend at the wrong one is a classic Tokyo mistake.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
What does 北 mean?
- [ ] south
- [x] north
- [ ] west
solution: 北 (きた): north.
Hear it: !say[きた]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=text answers="でんしゃ"
Type the reading of 電車.
hint: densha
solution: でんしゃ, train.
Hear it: !say[でんしゃ]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=text answers="かいしゃ"
Type the reading of 会社.
hint: kaisha
solution: かいしゃ, company.
Hear it: !say[かいしゃ]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
How is 東京 read?
- [x] とうきょう
- [ ] ひがしきょう
- [ ] とうけい
solution: とうきょう: 東 (とう, east) + 京 (きょう, capital): "eastern capital".
Hear it: !say[とうきょう]
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=text answers="がいこく"
Type the reading of 外国.
hint: gaikoku
solution: がいこく, foreign country (外国人 is a foreigner).
Hear it: !say[がいこく]
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
旅行 means:
- [x] trip, travel
- [ ] road
- [ ] town
solution: 旅行 (りょこう): trip.
Hear it: !say[りょこう]
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|みなみぐちで まって います。]
Which exit is the speaker waiting at?
- [ ] 北口
- [x] 南口
- [ ] 東口
solution: みなみぐち: 南口, the south exit.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice creative=true
東京 means "eastern capital", and 京都 (きょうと) was the old capital. Why might the new capital have been named relative to the old one?
- [x] When the emperor moved east in 1868, the city was renamed to show it was now the capital, "the capital in the east", as opposed to Kyoto in the west.
- [ ] Tokyo is the most eastern city in Japan.
- [ ] It's a random name.
solution: Edo became 東京 in 1868. Place names often record history: kanji make it visible, as with 北海道 (the "northern sea road").
:::

::: problem id=p9 type=draw answer="北"
Write 北 **north**.
solution: 北 has 5 strokes. Left half first: the vertical, the short horizontal, the rising stroke; then the right half.
:::

::: problem id=p10 type=draw answer="西"
Write 西 **west**.
solution: 西 has 6 strokes. The top bar, the left side of the box, the top and right side in one stroke, the two inner strokes, then the bottom.
:::

::: problem id=p11 type=draw answer="町"
Write 町 **town**.
solution: 町 has 7 strokes. 田 on the left (five strokes), then 丁 on the right.
:::

::: problem id=p12 type=draw answer="車"
Write 車 **car**.
solution: 車 has 7 strokes. Top bar, then the box with its middle line, the bar below it, and the long vertical through everything last.
:::

::: problem id=p13 type=draw answer="会"
Write 会 **meet**.
solution: 会 has 6 strokes. The roof (two strokes), the two horizontals, then ム underneath.
:::
