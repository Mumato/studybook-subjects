id: giv-01
title: Giving and receiving: あげる, くれる, もらう
---
English has "give" and "receive". Japanese has **three** verbs, and which one you use depends on **direction**: is the thing moving towards you (or your group), or away from you?

::: rule
- **あげる**: I (or anyone) give to someone **else**, moving away from me: !say[わたしは ともだちに はなを あげました。] *I gave flowers to my friend.* !say[たなかさんは やまださんに ほんを あげました。] *Tanaka gave Yamada a book.*
- **くれる**: someone gives **to me** (or to my family or group): !say[ともだちが わたしに ほんを くれました。] *My friend gave me a book.* わたしに is often dropped: !say[ははが セーターを くれました。]
- **もらう**: receive, from the receiver's point of view; the giver takes **に** or **から**: !say[わたしは ともだちに ほんを もらいました。] *I got a book from my friend.*
- So "My friend gave me a book" can be ともだちが くれました or ともだちに もらいました, but **never** ともだちが わたしに あげました.
- Giving to someone senior: さしあげる (very polite); to animals and plants, やる. You'll mostly use the three above.
:::

::: example
After a birthday:

- A: !say[たんじょうびに なにを もらいましたか。] *What did you get for your birthday?*
- B: !say[m|ちちが とけいを くれました。あねには シャツを もらいました。] *My dad gave me a watch. I got a shirt from my sister.*
- A: !say[わたしは かれに ケーキを あげました。] *I gave him a cake.*
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
Fill in: わたしは いもうとに ペンを ＿。 (I gave my little sister a pen.)
- [x] あげました
- [ ] くれました
- [ ] もらいました
solution: From me to someone else: あげる.
Hear it: !say[わたしは いもうとに ペンを あげました。]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Fill in: ともだちが わたしに チョコレートを ＿。 (My friend gave me chocolate.)
- [ ] あげました
- [x] くれました
- [ ] もらいました
solution: Towards me: くれる.
Hear it: !say[ともだちが わたしに チョコレートを くれました。]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Fill in: わたしは せんせいに じしょを ＿。 (I got a dictionary from my teacher.)
- [ ] あげました
- [ ] くれました
- [x] もらいました
solution: I received: もらう, with the giver marked by に.
Hear it: !say[わたしは せんせいに じしょを もらいました。]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Which is **wrong**?
- [ ] ははが わたしに てがみを くれました。
- [x] ははが わたしに てがみを あげました。
- [ ] わたしは ははに てがみを もらいました。
solution: When something comes to me, use くれる (or もらう), never あげる.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|クリスマスに かのじょに マフラーを もらいました。]
What happened?
- [x] The speaker got a scarf from his girlfriend.
- [ ] The speaker gave his girlfriend a scarf.
- [ ] His girlfriend bought herself a scarf.
solution: かのじょに もらいました: received from his girlfriend.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice creative=true
Why might Japanese need a special verb (くれる) for things that come **to** you?
- [x] It builds the speaker's point of view and gratitude into the grammar: with くれる, the listener always knows you were on the receiving end.
- [ ] Because あげる is rude.
- [ ] To make sentences longer.
solution: Japanese marks direction relative to the speaker's "in-group" (me, my family, my team). That's why くれる is also used when someone gives to your family.
:::
