---
id: mod-02
title: Describing nouns with verbs
---
In English, "the person **who is wearing glasses**" puts the description after the noun. Japanese puts it **before** the noun, using plain forms, with no word for "who" or "that".

::: rule
- Plain-form clause + noun: !say[めがねを かけて いる ひと] *the person who is wearing glasses*, !say[きのう かった ほん] *the book (I) bought yesterday*, !say[にほんで たべた すし] *the sushi (I) ate in Japan*.
- Negative and past work too: !say[にくを たべない ひと] *people who don't eat meat*, !say[いかなかった ひと] *the people who didn't go*.
- The subject inside the clause takes **が** (or の): !say[ははが つくった ケーキ] *the cake my mother made*.
- Use it to identify people: !say[あの あかい セーターを きて いる ひとは だれですか。] *Who's that person wearing the red sweater?*
- Plain だ becomes **の** before a noun (nouns) or **な** (な-adjectives): !say[いしゃの あに] *my brother, who is a doctor*, !say[しずかな へや] *a quiet room*.
:::

::: example
At a party:

- A: !say[あそこで ワインを のんで いる ひとは だれですか。] *Who's the person drinking wine over there?*
- B: !say[m|やまださんです。きょねん わたしが しょうかいした ひとですよ。] *That's Yamada. She's the person I introduced you to last year.*
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
きのう みた えいが means:
- [x] the movie (I) saw yesterday
- [ ] (I) saw a movie yesterday
- [ ] yesterday's movie theater
solution: きのう みた describes えいが: "the movie seen yesterday".
Hear it: !say[きのう みた えいが]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=text answers="ははがつくったケーキ"
Type "the cake my mother made."
hint: haha ga tsukutta keeki
solution: ははが つくった ケーキ. The clause subject takes が.
Hear it: !say[ははが つくった ケーキ]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
"The person who is reading a book" is:
- [x] ほんを よんで いる ひと
- [ ] ひとは ほんを よんで いる
- [ ] ほんを よんで いますの ひと
solution: The description goes before ひと, in plain form.
Hear it: !say[ほんを よんで いる ひと]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Which is correct for "people who don't drink alcohol" (alcohol = おさけ)?
- [ ] おさけを のみません ひと
- [x] おさけを のまない ひと
- [ ] おさけを のまないの ひと
solution: Plain negative (のまない) directly before the noun.
Hear it: !say[おさけを のまない ひと]
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=text answers="にほんでかったかばん"
Type "the bag I bought in Japan."
hint: nihon de katta kaban
solution: にほんで かった かばん.
Hear it: !say[にほんで かった かばん]
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
"My friend who lives in Osaka" is:
- [x] おおさかに すんで いる ともだち
- [ ] ともだちは おおさかに すんで いる
- [ ] おおさかに すんで います ともだち
solution: Plain clause + ともだち.
Hear it: !say[おおさかに すんで いる ともだち]
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|あの ぼうしを かぶって いる ひとが わたしの ちちです。]
Which person is the speaker's father?
- [ ] the one wearing glasses
- [x] the one wearing a hat
- [ ] the one in a red shirt
solution: ぼうしを かぶって いる ひと: the person wearing a hat.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
Listen: !listen[これは せんしゅう ともだちに もらった ほんです。]
What is the book?
- [x] one the speaker got from a friend last week
- [ ] one the speaker gave a friend last week
- [ ] one the speaker will buy next week
solution: ともだちに もらった ほん: a book received from a friend.
:::
