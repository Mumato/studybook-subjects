::: problem id=t1 type=choice
A ball rolls off the edge of a frictionless table in deep space, far from any gravity. After it leaves your hand, it:
- [ ] gradually slows down
- [x] keeps moving in a straight line at constant speed
- [ ] stops immediately
solution: No force acts, so nothing changes its motion (first law).
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="35"
Forces of 50 N to the right and 15 N to the left act on a crate. What is the size of the net force, in newtons?
solution: $50 - 15 = 35$ N, to the right.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="5"
A net force of 40 N acts on an 8 kg cart. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: $40 / 8 = 5$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="200"
What is the weight of a 20 kg suitcase on Earth, in newtons? (Use $g = 10$ m/s².)
solution: $20 \times 10 = 200$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
An elevator moves upward at a constant speed. Compared with the elevator's weight, the cable's pull is:
- [ ] larger
- [x] equal
- [ ] smaller
solution: Constant velocity means zero net force, whichever way it moves.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="1.5" tolerance=0.01
You push a 40 kg box with 100 N, and friction pulls back with 40 N. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: Net force $100 - 40 = 60$ N; $a = 60/40 = 1.5$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A car and a mosquito collide. During the collision:
- [x] the forces on each are equal, but the mosquito's acceleration is far larger
- [ ] the car pushes harder on the mosquito
- [ ] the forces and the accelerations are both equal
solution: Equal forces (third law), but $a = F/m$ and the mosquito's mass is tiny.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A falling skydiver reaches terminal speed. Which is true?
- [ ] There's no force on her.
- [ ] Gravity has switched off.
- [x] Air resistance balances her weight, so her speed stays constant.
- [ ] She's still speeding up, just slowly.
solution: The forces balance, so the net force and the acceleration are zero.
:::
