::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="8"
A metal piece has a mass of 400 g and a volume of 50 cm³. What is its density, in g/cm³?
solution: $400 / 50 = 8$ g/cm³.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="50"
A 5 L block is held completely under water. What buoyant force acts on it, in newtons? (1 L of water weighs 10 N.)
solution: It displaces 5 L of water: 50 N.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="84000"
How much heat warms 1 kg of water by 20 °C, in joules? (Use $c = 4200$ J per kg per °C.)
solution: $1 \times 4200 \times 20 = 84000$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="0.5"
A 680 Hz sound travels at 340 m/s. What is its wavelength, in metres?
solution: $340 / 680 = 0.5$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="3"
A 9 V battery drives current through a 3 Ω resistor. What current flows, in amperes?
solution: $9 / 3 = 3$ A.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="12"
A transformer has 1200 input turns and 60 output turns. With 240 V in, what voltage comes out, in volts?
solution: $240 \times 60/1200 = 12$ V.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="50"
A 400 g radioactive sample has a half-life of 10 minutes. How much is left after 30 minutes, in grams?
solution: 3 half-lives: $400 \to 200 \to 100 \to 50$ g.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Dim ultraviolet light frees electrons from a metal, but very bright red light doesn't. This shows that:
- [ ] red light has no energy
- [x] light comes in photons whose energy depends on frequency
- [ ] brightness and energy are the same thing
solution: One photon frees one electron, and a red photon is too weak on its own.
:::
