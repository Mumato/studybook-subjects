::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="3"
A bike speeds up from rest to 24 m/s in 8 s. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: $24 / 8 = 3$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
To keep a hockey puck sliding at a steady speed on frictionless ice, you need:
- [x] no force
- [ ] a small steady push
- [ ] a push equal to its weight
solution: Newton's first law: without friction, nothing changes its motion.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="4"
A 10 kg crate is pulled with 70 N to the right while friction pulls 30 N to the left. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: Net force $70 - 30 = 40$ N; $a = 40 / 10 = 4$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="500"
What is the weight of a 50 kg student on Earth, in newtons?
solution: $50 \times 10 = 500$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="300"
A 0.1 kg ball moving at 30 m/s is caught and stopped in 0.01 s. What is the average force on it, in newtons?
solution: $\Delta p = 0.1 \times 30 = 3$ kg·m/s; $F = 3 / 0.01 = 300$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="100"
A worker lifts a 20 kg box 3 m in 6 s at a steady speed. What power does the worker develop, in watts?
solution: $W = 200 \times 3 = 600$ J; $P = 600 / 6 = 100$ W.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="4"
A 500 N parent sits 2 m from a seesaw's pivot. How far from the pivot must a 250 N child sit to balance, in metres?
solution: $500 \times 2 = 1000 = 250 \times d$, so $d = 4$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A diver spins faster after tucking her knees to her chest because:
- [ ] she pushes off the air
- [x] her rotational inertia shrinks while her angular momentum stays the same
- [ ] gravity pulls harder on a tucked body
solution: Conservation of angular momentum: less rotational inertia means a faster spin.
:::
