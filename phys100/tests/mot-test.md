::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="15"
A cyclist rides 45 km in 3 hours. What is the average speed, in km/h?
solution: $45 / 3 = 15$ km/h.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="24"
You walk 2 km in half an hour, then ride a bus 22 km in another half hour. What was your average speed for the whole trip, in km/h?
solution: Total distance $2 + 22 = 24$ km, total time $1$ h: $24$ km/h.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
Which statement is true?
- [ ] An object moving at constant speed cannot be accelerating.
- [x] An object moving at constant speed can be accelerating.
- [ ] An object with zero velocity must have zero acceleration.
solution: Turning at constant speed is acceleration. And a ball at the top of its flight has zero velocity but is still accelerating.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="4"
A car speeds up from 8 m/s to 24 m/s in 4 s. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: $(24 - 8)/4 = 16/4 = 4$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="40"
A stone is dropped from rest. How fast is it falling after 4 s, in m/s? (Use $g = 10$ m/s², no air.)
solution: $10 \times 4 = 40$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="125"
A stone dropped from a bridge takes 5 s to reach the river. How high is the bridge, in metres?
solution: $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 5^{2} = 125$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A ball is thrown straight up. While it is on its way **up**, its acceleration is:
- [ ] upward and getting smaller
- [ ] zero
- [x] downward, 10 m/s²
- [ ] upward, 10 m/s²
solution: Gravity pulls down the whole time, so the ball slows on the way up at 10 m/s² downward.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A bowling ball and a tennis ball are dropped together from a tall window. Air resistance is tiny for both. Which lands first?
- [ ] the bowling ball, because it is heavier
- [ ] the tennis ball, because it is lighter
- [x] both land at about the same time
solution: Free fall acceleration doesn't depend on mass.
:::
