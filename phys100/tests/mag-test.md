---
::: problem id=t1 type=choice
The south pole of one magnet is brought near the north pole of another. They:
- [x] attract
- [ ] repel
- [ ] do nothing
solution: Unlike poles attract.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
A current flows through a straight wire. The magnetic field it makes:
- [ ] points along the wire
- [x] circles around the wire
- [ ] doesn't exist
solution: Field lines form circles around a current.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
Which does **not** produce a current in a coil?
- [ ] pushing a magnet into it
- [ ] pulling a magnet out of it
- [x] a magnet resting inside it
solution: Only a changing field induces a voltage.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="6"
A transformer has 400 input turns and 20 output turns. With 120 V in, what voltage comes out, in volts?
solution: $120 \times 20/400 = 6$ V.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="1200"
A transformer has 50 input turns and 500 output turns. With 120 V in, what voltage comes out, in volts?
solution: $120 \times 500/50 = 1200$ V.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="0.5"
An ideal transformer takes in 10 A at 12 V and steps the voltage up to 240 V. What current comes out, in amperes?
solution: Power $= 120$ W. $120 / 240 = 0.5$ A.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
An electric motor and a generator are:
- [x] the same kind of device used in reverse
- [ ] completely unrelated
- [ ] both kinds of battery
solution: A motor turns current into motion; a generator turns motion into current.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Pushing a magnet into a coil faster makes the induced voltage:
- [x] larger
- [ ] smaller
- [ ] the same
solution: A faster change in the field gives a larger voltage.
:::
