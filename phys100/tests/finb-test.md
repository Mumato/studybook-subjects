::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="30"
A ball is dropped from 45 m. Ignoring air, how fast is it moving just before it lands, in m/s?
solution: $10 \times 45 = \tfrac{1}{2} v^{2}$, so $v^{2} = 900$ and $v = 30$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="300"
A gas at 150 kPa is squeezed from 2 L to 1 L at the same temperature. What is its new pressure, in kPa?
solution: Half the volume, twice the pressure: 300 kPa.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="50"
An engine runs between 500 K and 250 K. What is its ideal efficiency, in percent?
solution: $1 - 250/500 = 0.5 = 50\%$.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="510"
You shout toward a cliff and hear the echo 3 s later. How far away is the cliff, in metres? (Sound: 340 m/s.)
solution: The sound travels $340 \times 3 = 1020$ m there and back, so the cliff is 510 m away.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="4"
Two 10 Ω heaters are connected in parallel to a 20 V supply. What total current is drawn, in amperes?
solution: Each branch: $20/10 = 2$ A. Total: 4 A.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="5"
A lens has a focal length of 0.2 m. What is its power, in diopters?
solution: $1 / 0.2 = 5$ diopters.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="10.2" tolerance=0.01
A hydrogen electron drops from −3.4 eV to −13.6 eV. What energy photon is emitted, in eV?
solution: $-3.4 - (-13.6) = 10.2$ eV.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="5"
A spaceship travels at $0.6c$ ($\gamma = 1.25$). Its crew's clocks record 4 years. How many years pass on Earth?
solution: $4 \times 1.25 = 5$ years.
:::
