::: problem id=t1 type=choice
Two negatively charged balls are brought close together. They:
- [ ] attract
- [x] repel
- [ ] are unaffected
solution: Like charges repel.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="1/9"
The distance between two charges triples. By what factor does the force between them change? (Type a fraction.)
solution: $\frac{1}{3^{2}} = \frac{1}{9}$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="4"
A 24 V battery drives current through a 6 Ω resistor. What current flows, in amperes?
solution: $24 / 6 = 4$ A.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="1200"
A hair dryer draws 10 A from a 120 V outlet. What is its power, in watts?
solution: $10 \times 120 = 1200$ W.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="10"
A 5 Ω and a 15 Ω resistor are in series with a 200 V supply. What current flows, in amperes?
solution: $R = 20$ Ω; $I = 200/20 = 10$ A.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="6"
Three identical 4 Ω heaters are connected in parallel to a 8 V supply. What total current is drawn, in amperes?
solution: Each: $8/4 = 2$ A. Three branches: $6$ A.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Houses are wired in parallel so that:
- [x] each appliance gets the full voltage and can be switched on or off on its own
- [ ] the current is the same everywhere
- [ ] less electricity is used
solution: Each branch connects straight across the supply.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="2"
A 500 W TV runs for 4 hours. How many kilowatt-hours does it use?
solution: $0.5 \text{ kW} \times 4 \text{ h} = 2$ kWh.
:::
