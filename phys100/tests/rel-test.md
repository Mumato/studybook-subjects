title: Relativity test
---
::: problem id=t1 type=choice
According to special relativity, every observer measures the same:
- [ ] time between two events
- [ ] length of a moving rod
- [x] speed of light
solution: That's Einstein's second postulate; time and length are what adjust.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="10"
A ship moves at $0.6c$ ($\gamma = 1.25$). Its clock records 8 hours. How many hours pass on Earth?
solution: $8 \times 1.25 = 10$ hours.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="6"
A traveller moves at $0.8c$ ($\gamma = 5/3$) for a trip lasting 10 years in Earth time. How many years does the traveller age?
solution: $10 / (5/3) = 6$ years.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="40"
A 50 m ship flies past at $0.6c$ ($\gamma = 1.25$). What length does Earth measure, in metres?
solution: $50 / 1.25 = 40$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
A moving clock, seen by someone it passes, runs:
- [x] slow
- [ ] fast
- [ ] at the normal rate
solution: Time dilation.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="9"
How much energy is 0.1 kg of mass equivalent to, in units of $10^{15}$ J?
solution: $0.1 \times 9 \times 10^{16} = 9 \times 10^{15}$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
An electron in a particle accelerator is pushed harder and harder. Its speed:
- [ ] eventually passes $c$
- [x] gets ever closer to $c$ but never reaches it
- [ ] stops increasing at $c/2$
solution: Nothing with mass reaches the speed of light.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Two events happen at the same time for one observer. For another observer moving past:
- [ ] they must also happen at the same time
- [x] they may happen at different times
- [ ] they happen in reverse
solution: Simultaneity is relative.
:::
