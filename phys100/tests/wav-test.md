---
::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="0.1"
A wave has a frequency of 10 Hz. What is its period, in seconds?
solution: $1/10 = 0.1$ s.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="15"
A wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and a wavelength of 3 m. What is its speed, in m/s?
solution: $5 \times 3 = 15$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="4"
A sound at 85 Hz travels at 340 m/s. What is its wavelength, in metres?
solution: $340 / 85 = 4$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="680"
Thunder is heard 2 s after the flash. How far away was the lightning, in metres?
solution: $340 \times 2 = 680$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
Sound waves in air are:
- [ ] transverse
- [x] longitudinal
- [ ] not waves at all
solution: Air is squeezed and stretched along the direction the sound travels.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=choice
Turning up the volume of a note changes its:
- [ ] frequency
- [ ] wavelength
- [x] amplitude
solution: Louder means bigger pressure swings; the pitch stays the same.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A train blowing its horn moves away from you. You hear the horn's pitch as:
- [ ] higher than normal
- [x] lower than normal
- [ ] normal
solution: Moving away stretches the waves, lowering the frequency you hear.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
Soldiers break step when marching over a bridge to avoid:
- [x] resonance
- [ ] interference
- [ ] the Doppler effect
solution: Steps in rhythm with the bridge's natural frequency could build up a dangerous vibration.
:::
