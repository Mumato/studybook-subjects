::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="1/16"
The distance between two planets becomes 4 times larger. By what factor does the gravitational force between them change? (Type a fraction.)
solution: $\frac{1}{4^{2}} = \frac{1}{16}$.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="250"
A probe weighs 1000 N on Earth's surface. What does it weigh 2 Earth radii from Earth's centre, in newtons?
solution: Twice as far from the centre: $1000 / 4 = 250$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="840"
A 70 kg person stands on a scale in an elevator that is slowing down while going down, at 2 m/s² (so the acceleration points up). What does the scale read, in newtons?
solution: Acceleration is upward: $70 \times (10 + 2) = 840$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
A person in a freely falling elevator would:
- [ ] feel heavier
- [x] feel weightless
- [ ] feel normal
solution: Nothing supports them, so the scale reads zero.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
Earth gets two high tides a day mainly because:
- [x] the Moon's pull is stronger on Earth's near side than its far side, stretching the oceans into two bulges
- [ ] the Sun heats the oceans twice a day
- [ ] the wind piles up water
solution: The difference in pull creates one bulge facing the Moon and one opposite it.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="2"
A ball rolls off the edge of a 20 m high cliff. How many seconds does it take to land?
solution: $20 = 5 t^{2}$, $t = 2$ s.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="12"
The ball in the last question left the cliff at 6 m/s horizontally. How far from the base does it land, in metres?
solution: $6 \times 2 = 12$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A cannonball is fired horizontally from a high mountain at about 8 km/s, above the air. It will:
- [ ] fall straight down
- [ ] fly off into space
- [x] fall around Earth in a circular orbit
solution: At 8 km/s near Earth, the ground curves away as fast as the ball falls.
:::
