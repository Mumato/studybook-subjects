---
::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="6000"
What is the momentum of a 300 kg motorbike and rider moving at 20 m/s, in kg·m/s?
solution: $300 \times 20 = 6000$ kg·m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="15"
A 1.5 kg ball goes from rest to 10 m/s. What impulse was given to it, in N·s?
solution: $\Delta p = 1.5 \times 10 = 15$ N·s.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="300"
A 0.6 kg basketball is stopped from 10 m/s in 0.02 s. What is the average stopping force, in newtons?
solution: $\Delta p = 6$ kg·m/s; $F = 6 / 0.02 = 300$ N.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=choice
Gymnasts land on thick mats instead of a hard floor because the mat:
- [ ] reduces the gymnast's momentum before landing
- [ ] reduces the impulse needed to stop
- [x] increases the stopping time, which lowers the force
solution: The change in momentum is the same; a longer time means a smaller force.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="3"
A 2 kg cart at 6 m/s hits a 2 kg cart at rest and they stick together. How fast do they move off, in m/s?
solution: $2 \times 6 = 12 = 4 v$, $v = 3$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="2"
A 60 kg boy and a 40 kg girl stand at rest on ice and push apart. The girl moves off at 3 m/s. How fast does the boy move, in m/s?
solution: Total momentum stays 0: $40 \times 3 = 120 = 60 v$, $v = 2$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Which needs a bigger impulse: catching a ball, or letting the same ball bounce off your hand at the same speed?
- [ ] catching it
- [x] letting it bounce
- [ ] they need the same impulse
solution: Bouncing reverses the momentum, which is twice the change of just stopping it.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A cannon fires a cannonball. Compared with the cannonball, the cannon gets:
- [x] the same size momentum, but a much smaller speed
- [ ] a smaller momentum and a smaller speed
- [ ] the same speed
solution: Total momentum stays zero, so the momenta are equal and opposite. The cannon's larger mass means a smaller speed.
:::
