::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="8"
A metal cube has a mass of 800 g and a volume of 100 cm³. What is its density, in g/cm³?
solution: $800 / 100 = 8$ g/cm³.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
A log floats in a river. Compared with water, the log's density is:
- [x] less
- [ ] the same
- [ ] greater
solution: Objects less dense than the fluid float.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="40"
A 4 L sealed jar is held completely under water. What buoyant force acts on it, in newtons? (1 L of water weighs 10 N.)
solution: It displaces 4 L of water, weighing 40 N.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="5000"
A 50 kg crate rests on a 0.1 m² base. What pressure does it put on the floor, in pascals?
solution: Weight $500$ N; $500 / 0.1 = 5000$ Pa.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="3"
Water pressure grows by about 1 atmosphere for every 10 m of depth. About how many atmospheres of *extra* pressure does a diver feel at 30 m?
solution: $30 / 10 = 3$ atmospheres extra (4 in total, counting the air above).
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="4"
A gas at 100 kPa is squeezed from 8 L to 2 L at the same temperature. By what factor does its pressure grow?
solution: A quarter of the volume means 4 times the pressure (400 kPa).
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A floating boat displaces water whose weight equals:
- [ ] the boat's volume
- [x] the boat's weight
- [ ] half the boat's weight
solution: Floating objects sink until the displaced fluid weighs as much as they do.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A shower curtain blows inward when the shower runs because:
- [x] the moving air and water inside lower the pressure there
- [ ] the hot water pulls it
- [ ] the curtain gets heavier
solution: Bernoulli: faster-moving air inside the shower has lower pressure than the still air outside.
:::
