::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="2"
A microwave has a frequency of $1.5 \times 10^{8}$ Hz. What is its wavelength, in metres? (Light speed $3 \times 10^{8}$ m/s.)
solution: $3 \times 10^{8} / 1.5 \times 10^{8} = 2$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=choice
A green leaf looks green because it:
- [x] reflects green light and absorbs most other colours
- [ ] absorbs green light
- [ ] emits green light
solution: We see the light it reflects.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="50"
A ray hits a mirror at 50° from the normal. At what angle from the normal does it reflect, in degrees?
solution: Angle of reflection = angle of incidence = 50°.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="2"
Light travels at $1.5 \times 10^{8}$ m/s in a material. What is its index of refraction?
solution: $n = 3 / 1.5 = 2$.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
A rainbow forms because water drops:
- [x] bend different colours by different amounts
- [ ] are coloured
- [ ] reflect only blue light
solution: Dispersion in each drop spreads white sunlight into colours.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="4"
A lens has a focal length of 0.25 m. What is its power, in diopters?
solution: $1/0.25 = 4$ diopters.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A camera lens forms an image of a distant tree on the sensor. The image is:
- [ ] upright and virtual
- [x] upside down and real
- [ ] upright and real
solution: Objects beyond the focal length give real, inverted images.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
A farsighted person needs glasses with:
- [ ] diverging lenses
- [x] converging lenses
- [ ] no lenses
solution: Their eye bends light too weakly; a converging lens adds bending.
:::
