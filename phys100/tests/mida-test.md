::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="90"
A car travels 180 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed, in km/h?
solution: $180 / 2 = 90$ km/h.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="20"
A stone is dropped from rest. Ignoring air, how far does it fall in 2 s, in metres?
solution: $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 2^{2} = 20$ m.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
A ball is thrown straight up. At the very top of its flight:
- [ ] its velocity and acceleration are both zero
- [x] its velocity is zero and its acceleration is 10 m/s² downward
- [ ] its velocity is zero and its acceleration is upward
solution: Gravity keeps pulling at the top. The ball only stops for an instant.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="4"
A net force of 20 N acts on a 5 kg box. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: $20 / 5 = 4$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=choice
A heavy truck and a small car crash head on. During the crash, the force of the truck on the car is:
- [ ] larger than the force of the car on the truck
- [x] equal to the force of the car on the truck
- [ ] smaller than the force of the car on the truck
solution: Newton's third law: the pair of forces is always equal. The car's smaller mass makes its acceleration, and the damage, bigger.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="3"
A 3 kg cart moving at 4 m/s hits a 1 kg cart at rest, and they stick together. How fast do they move, in m/s?
solution: $3 \times 4 = 12 = 4 v$, so $v = 3$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=numeric answer="200"
A 4 kg ball is dropped from 5 m. Ignoring air, what is its kinetic energy just before it lands, in joules?
solution: All the PE becomes KE: $4 \times 10 \times 5 = 200$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="500"
A satellite weighs 2000 N on Earth's surface. What is the force of gravity on it 2 Earth radii from Earth's centre, in newtons?
solution: Twice the distance from the centre: $2000 / 4 = 500$ N.
:::
