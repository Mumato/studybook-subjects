::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="25"
A 200 g sample has a half-life of 2 hours. How much is left after 6 hours, in grams?
solution: 3 half-lives: $200 \to 100 \to 50 \to 25$ g.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="1/16"
What fraction of a sample remains after 4 half-lives? (Type a fraction.)
solution: $(1/2)^{4} = 1/16$.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=choice
Which radiation is stopped by a sheet of paper?
- [x] alpha
- [ ] beta
- [ ] gamma
solution: Alpha particles are big and highly charged, so they stop quickly.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="222"
Radium-226 emits an alpha particle. What is the mass number of the new nucleus?
solution: $226 - 4 = 222$ (radon).
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="7"
Carbon-14 (atomic number 6) undergoes beta decay. What is the atomic number of the new nucleus?
solution: A neutron becomes a proton: $6 + 1 = 7$ (nitrogen).
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="27"
How much energy is 3 g of mass equivalent to, in units of $10^{13}$ J?
solution: $0.003 \times 9 \times 10^{16} = 27 \times 10^{13}$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
Nuclear power plants today get their energy from:
- [x] fission of uranium
- [ ] fusion of hydrogen
- [ ] burning uranium
solution: Controlled fission chain reactions heat water to steam.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=choice
In both fission and fusion, the energy released comes from:
- [ ] electrons changing levels
- [x] a small loss of mass, $E = mc^{2}$
- [ ] chemical bonds
solution: The products weigh slightly less than the starting nuclei.
:::
