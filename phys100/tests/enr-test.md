title: Energy test
---
::: problem id=t1 type=numeric answer="150"
You push a cart 10 m with a steady 15 N force along the floor. How much work do you do, in joules?
solution: $15 \times 10 = 150$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t2 type=numeric answer="50"
A crane does 5000 J of work in 100 s. What is its power, in watts?
solution: $5000 / 100 = 50$ W.
:::

::: problem id=t3 type=numeric answer="500"
What is the potential energy of a 5 kg rock on a 10 m high cliff, relative to the ground below, in joules?
solution: $5 \times 10 \times 10 = 500$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t4 type=numeric answer="100"
What is the kinetic energy of a 2 kg ball moving at 10 m/s, in joules?
solution: $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 10^{2} = 100$ J.
:::

::: problem id=t5 type=numeric answer="9"
A truck triples its speed. By what factor does its kinetic energy grow?
solution: $3^{2} = 9$.
:::

::: problem id=t6 type=numeric answer="10"
A ball is dropped from 5 m. Ignoring air, how fast is it moving just before it hits the ground, in m/s?
solution: $10 \times 5 = \tfrac{1}{2} v^{2}$, $v^{2} = 100$, $v = 10$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=t7 type=choice
A pendulum swings back and forth. Where is its kinetic energy greatest?
- [ ] at the highest points of the swing
- [x] at the lowest point
- [ ] it is the same everywhere
solution: At the bottom, the most PE has turned into KE, so it's fastest there.
:::

::: problem id=t8 type=numeric answer="40"
An electric motor uses 500 J and lifts a 20 kg load 1 m. What is its efficiency, in percent?
solution: Useful work $= 200 \times 1 = 200$ J. $200 / 500 = 40\%$.
:::
