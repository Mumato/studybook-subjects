title: How heat moves
---
Heat always flows from hot to cold, by three routes. Knowing them explains thermos flasks, winter coats and why the Sun can warm us across empty space. (OpenStax College Physics 2e ch. 14)

::: rule
- **Conduction:** heat passes through a material, atom bumping atom. Metals are good **conductors**. Air, wool, wood and plastic foam are poor conductors: **insulators**.
- **Convection:** a heated fluid (liquid or gas) expands, rises, and carries heat with it, while cooler fluid sinks to take its place.
- **Radiation:** everything gives off electromagnetic waves, more the hotter it is. Radiation needs no material, so it crosses space. Dark, dull surfaces absorb and emit it well; shiny surfaces reflect it.
:::

![](assets/hea-transfer.3x.png)

::: example
A thermos flask fights all three: a **vacuum** between two walls stops conduction and convection, and **silvered** walls reflect radiation back.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
The metal handle of a pot on the stove gets hot. How did the heat get there?
![](assets/hea-transfer.3x.png)
- [x] conduction
- [ ] convection
- [ ] radiation
solution: Heat travels through the solid metal, atom to atom.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
A metal bench and a wooden bench sit in the same cold park, at the same temperature. The metal feels colder. Why?
- [ ] The metal really is colder.
- [x] Metal conducts heat away from your hand much faster.
- [ ] Wood makes its own heat.
solution: Your hand loses heat faster to the metal, and your skin senses the heat flow, not the temperature.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
The air near the ceiling of a heated room is warmer than near the floor because of:
- [ ] conduction
- [x] convection
- [ ] radiation
solution: Warm air expands, becomes less dense and rises.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
How does the Sun's heat reach Earth?
- [ ] conduction through space
- [ ] convection currents in space
- [x] radiation
solution: Space is nearly empty, so only radiation can cross it.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
On a sunny day, which shirt warms up the most?
- [x] a black one
- [ ] a white one
- [ ] a shiny silver one
solution: Dark surfaces absorb the most radiation; white and shiny ones reflect much of it.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Double-glazed windows have a thin layer of air sealed between two panes. This helps because:
- [ ] air is a good conductor
- [x] still air is a poor conductor, so less heat escapes
- [ ] the air reflects heat
solution: Trapped still air insulates. It's the same reason a fluffy coat or a bird's puffed-up feathers keep warm.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Why is the heating element at the bottom of an electric kettle?
- [ ] so the bottom boils first
- [x] so heated water rises and cooler water sinks to it: convection stirs the whole kettle
- [ ] because the bottom is closest to the plug
solution: Heating from below sets up convection currents that spread the heat through the water.
:::
