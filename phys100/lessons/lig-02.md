title: Reflection and refraction
---
Light bounces off mirrors and bends when it passes into glass or water. These two simple rules explain mirrors, rainbows, mirages and the fibre-optic cables that carry the internet. (OpenStax College Physics 2e ch. 25)

::: rule
- **Reflection:** the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence (both measured from the **normal**, the line at right angles to the surface). A flat mirror's image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front.
- **Refraction:** light slows down in water or glass, and bends **toward the normal** going in, away from it coming out. The **index of refraction** is $n = c / v$.
- Going from glass or water into air at a steep enough angle, light can't get out: **total internal reflection**. Fibre optics trap light this way.
- Different colours bend by different amounts (violet most, red least): **dispersion**. That's how prisms and rainbows split white light.
:::

::: example
Light travels at $2 \times 10^{8}$ m/s in a glass block, so
$$n = \frac{3 \times 10^{8}}{2 \times 10^{8}} = 1.5$$
Water's index is about 1.33, diamond's 2.4. The higher the index, the more the light bends.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="35"
A light ray hits a mirror at 35° from the normal. At what angle from the normal does it reflect, in degrees?
solution: Angle in = angle out: 35°.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="4"
You stand 2 m in front of a flat mirror. How far are you from your image, in metres?
solution: The image is 2 m behind the mirror: $2 + 2 = 4$ m from you.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="1.25"
Light travels at $2.4 \times 10^{8}$ m/s in a plastic. What is the plastic's index of refraction?
solution: $n = 3 / 2.4 = 1.25$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
A straw in a glass of water looks bent at the surface. Why?
![](assets/lig-refract.3x.png)
- [ ] The water really bends the straw.
- [x] Light from the underwater part bends as it leaves the water, so that part appears in the wrong place.
- [ ] Reflection off the glass.
solution: Refraction at the surface shifts where the underwater part seems to be, making it look raised.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Fibre-optic cables carry light around bends without it escaping because of:
- [ ] dispersion
- [x] total internal reflection
- [ ] absorption
solution: The light hits the fibre's wall at such a shallow angle that it is completely reflected back inside.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
White light passes through a prism. Which colour bends the most?
![](assets/lig-prism.3x.png)
- [ ] red
- [ ] green
- [x] violet
solution: Glass slows violet light slightly more than red, so violet bends most.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Light passes from air into water at an angle. It:
- [x] slows down and bends toward the normal
- [ ] speeds up and bends toward the normal
- [ ] slows down and bends away from the normal
solution: Light is slower in water, which turns it toward the normal.
:::
