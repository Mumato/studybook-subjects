title: Light and colour
---
Light is an electromagnetic wave: a ripple of electric and magnetic fields that needs no medium and crosses empty space at the fastest speed there is. Visible light is a thin slice of a much bigger family. (OpenStax College Physics 2e ch. 24-25)

::: rule
- All electromagnetic waves travel at $c = 3 \times 10^{8}$ m/s in a vacuum, and $c = f \lambda$.
- From long to short wavelength: radio, microwave, infrared, **visible** (red to violet), ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays.
- An object's colour is the light it **reflects**; it absorbs the rest. A red shirt reflects red.
- Mixing **light**: red + green + blue make white; red + green make yellow.
- The sky is blue because air molecules **scatter** blue light much more than red.
:::

::: example
An FM radio station broadcasts at 100 MHz ($10^{8}$ Hz). Its wavelength is
$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{3 \times 10^{8}}{10^{8}} = 3 \text{ m}$$
Visible light has wavelengths around half a millionth of a metre.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="1.28" tolerance=0.01
The Moon is $3.84 \times 10^{8}$ m away. How many seconds does light take to get from the Moon to us?
solution: $t = \frac{3.84 \times 10^{8}}{3 \times 10^{8}} = 1.28$ s.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="6"
A radio wave has a frequency of $5 \times 10^{7}$ Hz. What is its wavelength, in metres?
solution: $\lambda = \frac{3 \times 10^{8}}{5 \times 10^{7}} = 6$ m.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Which has the highest frequency?
- [ ] radio waves
- [ ] visible light
- [x] X-rays
- [ ] microwaves
solution: X-rays have much shorter wavelengths, so much higher frequencies (and more energy per photon).
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
A red shirt is lit only by blue light. It looks:
- [ ] red
- [ ] blue
- [x] black (very dark)
solution: The shirt reflects only red. With no red light to reflect, it reflects almost nothing.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Red and green spotlights overlap on a white wall. The overlap looks:
![](assets/lig-rgb.3x.png)
- [ ] brown
- [x] yellow
- [ ] white
solution: Adding light: red + green = yellow. All three (red, green, blue) give white. That's how screens make every colour.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Why is the daytime sky blue?
- [ ] It reflects the oceans.
- [x] Air molecules scatter blue light far more than red, so blue reaches our eyes from all over the sky.
- [ ] The Sun is blue.
solution: At sunset the light crosses so much air that most blue is scattered away, leaving reds and oranges.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Light can cross the vacuum of space, but sound can't. Why?
- [ ] Light is faster.
- [x] Light is an electromagnetic wave and needs no medium; sound needs matter to vibrate.
- [ ] Space blocks sound.
solution: Electric and magnetic fields keep regenerating each other, with no material needed.
:::
