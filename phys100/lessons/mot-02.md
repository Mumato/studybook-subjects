title: Acceleration
---
Pressing the gas, braking and turning the wheel all do the same thing in physics: they change the velocity. The rate of that change is **acceleration**. (OpenStax College Physics 2e ch. 2)

::: rule
$$\text{acceleration} = \frac{\text{change in velocity}}{\text{time}}$$
- Units: (m/s) per second, written **m/s²**. "3 m/s²" means the velocity changes by 3 m/s *every second*.
- Speeding up, slowing down and **changing direction** are all acceleration.
- Zero acceleration means constant velocity: steady speed in a straight line (or standing still).
:::

![](assets/mot-speedup.3x.png)

::: example
A car goes from rest to 20 m/s in 5 s.
$$a = \frac{20 - 0}{5} = 4 \text{ m/s}^2$$
Each second it gains 4 m/s: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 m/s. Because it goes faster each second, it also covers more distance each second, as the dots above show.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="5"
A car goes from 0 to 30 m/s in 6 s. What is its acceleration, in m/s²?
solution: $30 / 6 = 5$ m/s².
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="3"
A bike slows from 25 m/s to 10 m/s in 5 s. How big is its acceleration, in m/s²? (Give the size, without a sign.)
solution: The velocity changes by $10 - 25 = -15$ m/s in 5 s: $-15/5 = -3$ m/s². Its size is 3 m/s².
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="14"
A cart starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s². How fast is it going after 7 s, in m/s?
solution: It gains 2 m/s each second: $2 \times 7 = 14$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="19"
A runner is going 4 m/s and speeds up by 3 m/s every second. How fast is the runner going after 5 s, in m/s?
solution: $4 + 3 \times 5 = 19$ m/s.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which one is **not** accelerating?
- [ ] A car rounding a curve at a steady 40 km/h
- [ ] A car braking at a red light
- [x] A car driving straight at a steady 100 km/h
- [ ] A car pulling away from a stop sign
solution: Only the straight, steady car keeps both its speed and its direction. Turning counts as acceleration.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
The dots show a cart once every second, moving to the right.
![](assets/mot-speedup.3x.png)
The cart is:
- [ ] moving at a steady speed
- [x] speeding up
- [ ] slowing down
solution: The gap between dots grows, so it covers more distance each second: it's speeding up.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=numeric answer="3"
The graph shows a cart's velocity over time.
![](assets/mot-vt.3x.png)
What is its acceleration during the first 4 seconds, in m/s²?
hint: How much does the velocity rise each second?
solution: It goes from 0 to 12 m/s in 4 s: $12 / 4 = 3$ m/s². (After 4 s the line is flat: steady velocity, zero acceleration.)
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
On the same graph, what is the cart's acceleration between 4 s and 5 s?
![](assets/mot-vt.3x.png)
- [x] zero
- [ ] 3 m/s²
- [ ] 12 m/s²
- [ ] it is slowing down
solution: The velocity stays at 12 m/s, so it doesn't change: zero acceleration, even though the cart is moving fast.
:::
