title: Atoms and density
---
Everything around you is made of atoms, so small that a glass of water holds more of them than there are glasses of water in all the oceans. How tightly they're packed, and how heavy they are, decides an object's **density**. (OpenStax College Physics 2e ch. 11)

::: rule
$$\text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$$
- Units: g/cm³ or kg/m³. **Water** is 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³; 1 litre of water has a mass of 1 kg.
- Density belongs to the **material**, not the amount: half a brick has half the mass and half the volume, so the same density.
- An atom is mostly empty space: a tiny heavy nucleus surrounded by light electrons.
- Matter comes as **solid** (fixed shape), **liquid** (flows, fixed volume) or **gas** (fills its container).
:::

::: example
A metal block has a mass of 540 g and a volume of 200 cm³.
$$\text{density} = \frac{540}{200} = 2.7 \text{ g/cm}^3$$
That's aluminium. Iron would be 7.9 g/cm³, so the same block in iron would have a mass of $7.9 \times 200 = 1580$ g.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=numeric answer="3"
A stone has a mass of 600 g and a volume of 200 cm³. What is its density, in g/cm³?
solution: $600 / 200 = 3$ g/cm³.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="193"
Gold has a density of 19.3 g/cm³. What is the mass of a 10 cm³ gold bar, in grams?
solution: $19.3 \times 10 = 193$ g.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=numeric answer="20"
An aluminium block (2.7 g/cm³) has a mass of 54 g. What is its volume, in cm³?
solution: volume = mass ÷ density $= 54 / 2.7 = 20$ cm³.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="2000"
What is the mass of 2 m³ of water, in kg?
solution: $1000 \times 2 = 2000$ kg.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
You cut a block of wood into two unequal pieces. Compared with the whole block, the density of the smaller piece is:
- [ ] smaller
- [x] the same
- [ ] larger
solution: Density depends on the material, not on how much of it you have.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
An object with a density of 1.5 g/cm³ is put in water. It:
- [x] sinks
- [ ] floats
- [ ] floats exactly halfway under
solution: It's denser than water (1 g/cm³), so it sinks.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Most of the volume of an atom is:
- [ ] the nucleus
- [ ] protons
- [x] empty space
solution: If an atom were a football stadium, the nucleus would be a pea in the centre. Nearly all its mass is in that pea.
:::
