# Credits

The topics and their order follow a standard first-year university conceptual physics course (physics for
non-majors, little algebra). All explanations, examples, problems and diagrams were written and drawn for StudyBook.

Reference for topics, terms and values: *College Physics 2e* by OpenStax (Rice University), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): https://openstax.org/details/books/college-physics-2e

Diagram sources are in `.src/` (SVG drawn by script, rendered to `assets/*@3x.png`).
