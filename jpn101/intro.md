## Welcome to JPN101

This book covers a first university course in **Elementary Japanese**, starting from zero: the hiragana and katakana scripts, everyday conversation, basic grammar and your first kanji.

### Typing Japanese

You don't need a Japanese keyboard. When an answer is in Japanese, type **romaji** (Japanese in English letters) and it turns into kana as you type: `taberu` → たべる.

- Both common spellings work: `shi`/`si`, `chi`/`ti`, `tsu`/`tu`, `fu`/`hu`.
- Double a consonant for a small っ: `kitte` → きって. Use `n'` or `nn` for ん when the next letter is a vowel: `kin'en` → きんえん.
- Particles can be typed as they sound: `wa` for は, `e` for へ, `o` for を.
- Katakana answers accept the same romaji, with `-` for the long mark ー: `ko-hi-` → コーヒー.
- Answers asking for a **reading** ("type the reading of か") want romaji: `ka`.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**. Wrong answers go on your **Review mistakes** list on the contents page. Each chapter ends with a **test**: score 80% to complete it.
