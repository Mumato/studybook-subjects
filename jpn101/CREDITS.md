# Credits

The topics and their order follow a standard first-semester university Elementary Japanese course. All
explanations, examples, problems and diagrams were written and drawn for StudyBook.

Japanese text in the book and diagrams uses the **M PLUS 1p** font by the M+ Project Authors, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (bundled with the StudyBook mod).

Diagram sources are in `.src/` (SVG drawn by script, rendered to `assets/*.3x.png`).

Audio was generated with VOICEVOX (https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/), offline text-to-speech:
**VOICEVOX:No.7** (female voice, announcer style) and **VOICEVOX:玄野武宏** (male voice).

Stroke-order data for the writing practice comes from **KanjiVG** (https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), Copyright (C) Ulrich Apel, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0. The files in assets/strokes are adapted from it (paths flattened to points) and are shared under the same licence.
