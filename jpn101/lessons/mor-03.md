id: mor-03
title: Family and describing people
---
Family words come in two sets: plain words for **your own** family, and polite ones for **someone else's**. You'll also learn to give ages and describe what people look like.

::: rule
- **My family** (humble) / **your family** (polite): !say[ちち] / !say[おとうさん] (father), !say[はは] / !say[おかあさん] (mother), !say[あに] / !say[おにいさん] (older brother), !say[あね] / !say[おねえさん] (older sister), !say[おとうと] / !say[おとうとさん] (younger brother), !say[いもうと] / !say[いもうとさん] (younger sister), !say[かぞく] / !say[ごかぞく] (family).
- Inside the family, you call your own parents おとうさん, おかあさん, but talking **about** them to others, use ちち, はは.
- **Age**: number + さい: !say[じゅうはっさい] (18), !say[にじゅういっさい] (21). 20 is special: !say[はたち]. Ask: !say[なんさい ですか。] (or politely !say[おいくつ ですか。]).
- **Describing**: person **は** feature **が** adjective: !say[あには せが たかいです。] *My older brother is tall.* (せ = height) !say[いもうとは かみが ながいです。] *My younger sister has long hair.*
- Useful words: !say[やさしい] (kind), !say[おもしろい] (funny), !say[あたまが いい] (smart), !say[わかい] (young).
:::

::: example
Showing a family photo:

- A: !say[ごかぞくは なんにん ですか。] *How many people are in your family?*
- B: !say[m|よにん です。ちちと ははと あねが います。] *Four: my father, my mother and my older sister.*
- A: !say[おねえさんは なんさい ですか。] *How old is your sister?*
- B: !say[m|はたち です。あねは せが たかくて、やさしいです。] *She's twenty. She's tall and kind.*

(たかくて joins two adjectives: you'll learn it in JPN102.)
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
Talking to a classmate about **your own** mother, you say:
- [x] はは
- [ ] おかあさん
- [ ] おかあさま
solution: Use the humble word, はは, for your own family when talking to others.
Hear it: !say[はは]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Asking about **your classmate's** older brother, you say:
- [ ] あに
- [x] おにいさん
- [ ] おとうと
solution: Someone else's older brother is おにいさん.
Hear it: !say[おにいさん]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=text answers="はたち"
Type "twenty years old".
hint: The special word: hatachi.
solution: はたち: 20 is special for ages.
Hear it: !say[はたち]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=text answers="じゅうきゅうさい"
Type "nineteen years old".
hint: juukyuu-sai
solution: じゅうきゅうさい.
Hear it: !say[じゅうきゅうさい]
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
"My younger sister has long hair" is:
- [x] いもうとは かみが ながいです。
- [ ] いもうとの かみを ながいです。
- [ ] かみは いもうとが ながいです。
solution: person は feature が adjective.
Hear it: !say[いもうとは かみが ながいです。]
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
あねは あたまが いいです means:
- [ ] My older sister has a good head of hair.
- [x] My older sister is smart.
- [ ] My older sister has a headache.
solution: あたまが いい ("the head is good") means smart.
Hear it: !say[あねは あたまが いいです。]
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|わたしの かぞくは ごにん です。ちちと ははと あにと おとうとが います。]
Who isn't in the speaker's family?
- [ ] an older brother
- [x] a sister
- [ ] a younger brother
solution: ちち, はは, あに (older brother), おとうと (younger brother): no sister.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=open
Introduce your family: how many people, who they are, and one thing about one of them.
!speak[my family]
solution: For example: わたしの かぞくは よにん です。ちちと ははと いもうとが います。いもうとは じゅうごさい です。とても おもしろいです。
:::
