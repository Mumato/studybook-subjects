---
id: vrb-02
title: Particles を, で, に and へ
---
Particles are small words that come **after** a noun and show its job in the sentence: object, place, time or destination. Because of them, Japanese word order is flexible.

::: rule
- **を** (read **o**) marks the **object**, the thing the action is done to: !say[コーヒーを のみます] *I drink coffee.*
- **で** marks **where** an action happens: !say[としょかんで べんきょうします] *I study at the library.*
- **に** marks a **destination**: !say[がっこうに いきます] *I go to school.* **へ** (read **e**) can replace it for direction: がっこうへ いきます.
- **に** also marks a **specific time**: !say[しちじに おきます] *I get up at 7.* Relative time words take no particle: !say[あした], !say[まいにち], !say[こんばん].
- A typical order: (time) (place で) (object を) verb: !say[あした うちで えいがを みます] *Tomorrow I'll watch a movie at home.*
:::

::: example
A weekend plan:

!say[m|どようびに ともだちと きょうとに いきます。レストランで ばんごはんを たべます。]

*On Saturday I'll go to Kyoto with a friend. We'll eat dinner at a restaurant.* (と means "with" a person.)

Spot the jobs: どようび**に** (time), きょうと**に** (destination), レストラン**で** (place of action), ばんごはん**を** (object).
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
Fill in: パン ＿ たべます。 (I eat bread.)
- [x] を
- [ ] で
- [ ] に
solution: パン is the thing eaten, the object: を.
Hear it: !say[パンを たべます]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Fill in: カフェ ＿ コーヒーを のみます。 (I drink coffee at a café.)
- [ ] を
- [x] で
- [ ] に
solution: The café is where the action happens: で.
Hear it: !say[カフェで コーヒーを のみます]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=choice
Fill in: とうきょう ＿ いきます。 (I go to Tokyo.)
- [ ] を
- [ ] で
- [x] に
solution: Tokyo is the destination: に (or へ).
Hear it: !say[とうきょうに いきます]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
Fill in: じゅうじ ＿ ねます。 (I go to bed at 10.)
- [x] に
- [ ] で
- [ ] (no particle)
solution: A clock time takes に.
Hear it: !say[じゅうじに ねます]
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which sentence is correct?
- [ ] あしたに いきます。
- [x] あした いきます。
- [ ] あしたで いきます。
solution: Relative time words like あした (tomorrow), きょう (today) and まいにち (every day) take no particle.
Hear it: !say[あした いきます。]
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=text answers="ほんをよみます"
Type "I read a book." (ほん = book)
hint: hon o yomimasu
solution: ほんを よみます。 Type を as *o* or *wo*.
Hear it: !say[ほんを よみます]
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|にちようびに ともだちの うちで ゲームを します。]
Where will the speaker play games?
- [ ] at school
- [x] at a friend's house
- [ ] at a game centre
solution: ともだちの うち**で** (at a friend's house): "On Sunday I'll play games at my friend's house."
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice
How is the particle を pronounced?
- [x] o
- [ ] wo, always
- [ ] wa
solution: を is pronounced *o*, like お. On a keyboard you type *wo*, or *o* here.
:::
