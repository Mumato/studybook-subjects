---
id: mor-01
title: Particles も, と, で, から and まで
---
A handful of small particles make your sentences much richer: "too", "and", "with", "by bus", "from 9 to 5".

::: rule
- **も** means "too, also". It **replaces** は, が or を: !say[わたしも がくせいです。] *I'm a student too.* !say[コーヒーも のみます。] *I drink coffee too.* With a negative it means "either": !say[わたしも いきません。] *I'm not going either.*
- **と** joins nouns, "A and B" (only nouns, not sentences): !say[パンと たまご] *bread and eggs.* After a person it means "with": !say[ともだちと いきます。] *I'll go with a friend.*
- **で** marks the **means**: !say[バスで いきます。] *I go by bus.* !say[はしで たべます。] *I eat with chopsticks.* !say[にほんごで はなします。] *I speak in Japanese.* On foot is !say[あるいて].
- **から** (from) and **まで** (until, as far as): !say[くじから ごじまで はたらきます。] *I work from 9 to 5.* !say[うちから えきまで あるいて じゅっぷん です。] *It's a 10-minute walk from home to the station.*
:::

::: example
- A: !say[まいにち なんで がっこうに きますか。] *How do you come to school every day?*
- B: !say[m|でんしゃで きます。うちから がっこうまで さんじゅっぷん です。] *By train. It's 30 minutes from home to school.*
- A: !say[わたしも でんしゃです。] *Me too: by train.*

なんで ("by what") asks for the means.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=choice
Fill in: たなかさんは がくせいです。やまださん ＿ がくせいです。 (Yamada is a student too.)
- [ ] は
- [x] も
- [ ] と
solution: も (too) replaces は.
Hear it: !say[やまださんも がくせいです。]
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=choice
Fill in: ちかてつ ＿ いきます。 (I go by subway.)
- [ ] に
- [ ] を
- [x] で
solution: で marks the means of transport.
Hear it: !say[ちかてつで いきます。]
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=text answers="ともだちとえいがをみます"
Type "I'll watch a movie with a friend."
hint: tomodachi to eiga o mimasu
solution: ともだちと えいがを みます。
Hear it: !say[ともだちと えいがを みます。]
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=choice
"From Monday to Friday" is:
- [x] げつようびから きんようびまで
- [ ] げつようびまで きんようびから
- [ ] げつようびと きんようび
solution: から means from, まで means until.
Hear it: !say[げつようびから きんようびまで]
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=choice
Which is wrong?
- [ ] すしと てんぷらを たべます。
- [x] すしを たべますと てんぷらを たべます。
- [ ] すしも たべます。
solution: と only joins nouns, not sentences.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=choice
Your friend says ぼくは いきません. You aren't going either. What do you say?
- [ ] わたしは いきます。
- [x] わたしも いきません。
- [ ] わたしと いきません。
solution: も with a negative verb means "not either".
Hear it: !say[わたしも いきません。]
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
Listen: !listen[m|わたしは まいにち じてんしゃで だいがくに いきます。]
How does the speaker get to university?
- [ ] by bus
- [x] by bicycle
- [ ] on foot
solution: じてんしゃで: by bicycle.
:::

::: problem id=p8 type=choice creative=true
English "and" joins anything: "I ate and slept", "bread and eggs". Japanese と only joins nouns. What might a Japanese learner of English find hard because of this?
- [x] Using one word, "and", for joining both nouns and whole actions, where Japanese uses different patterns.
- [ ] Pronouncing "and".
- [ ] Nothing: the two languages work the same.
solution: Japanese joins actions with other forms (the て-form, coming in JPN102). Seeing how your own language bundles meanings into one word helps you notice where Japanese splits them.
:::
