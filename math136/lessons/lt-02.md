title: Geometric transformations
---
Rotations, reflections, projections, stretches and shears of the plane are all linear. To find a matrix, just ask where $\vec{e}_1 = (1, 0)$ and $\vec{e}_2 = (0, 1)$ go. (Nicholson ch. 2)

::: rule
- **Rotation** by angle $\theta$ (counterclockwise): $\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}$.
- **Reflection** in the $x$-axis: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$; in the line $y = x$: $\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
- **Projection** onto the $x$-axis: $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
- A transformation with matrix $A$ multiplies every area by $|\det A|$. A negative determinant also flips orientation.
:::

::: example
Rotation by 90°: $\cos 90° = 0$, $\sin 90° = 1$, so the matrix has rows $(0, -1)$ and $(1, 0)$.
It sends $(3, 1)$ to $(-1, 3)$: a quarter turn counterclockwise.
:::

::: problem id=p1 type=point answer="-1,3"
Plot the image of $(3, 1)$ under rotation by 90° counterclockwise.
graph:
x -4..4
y -4..4
point 3,1 P blue
solution: $(x, y) \mapsto (-y, x)$: $(-1, 3)$.
:::

::: problem id=p2 type=numeric answer="0.866" tolerance=0.001
What is entry (2, 1) of the rotation matrix for 60°? (3 decimals)
solution: $\sin 60° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.866$.
:::

::: problem id=p3 type=point answer="2,4"
Plot the reflection of $(4, 2)$ in the line $y = x$.
graph:
x -1..5
y -1..5
point 4,2 P blue
solution: Swap the coordinates: $(2, 4)$.
:::

::: problem id=p4 type=numeric answer="0"
Projection onto the $x$-axis sends $(5, -3)$ to $(a, b)$. What is $b$?
solution: The $y$-part is dropped: $(5, 0)$.
:::

::: problem id=p5 type=numeric answer="10"
The matrix with rows $(3, 1)$ and $(1, 2)$ is applied to a triangle of area 2. What is the area of the image?
solution: $|\det| = |6 - 1| = 5$; the new area is $5 \times 2 = 10$.
:::

::: problem id=p6 type=numeric answer="1"
What is the determinant of any rotation matrix?
solution: $\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$: rotations keep areas and orientation.
:::

::: problem id=p7 type=choice
The projection matrix onto the $x$-axis has determinant 0. This means:
- [x] it squashes areas to 0 and has no inverse
- [ ] it keeps areas the same
- [ ] it's a rotation
solution: Many points land on the same image, so it can't be undone.
:::
