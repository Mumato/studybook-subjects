## Welcome to MATH136

This book covers a first university course in **Linear Algebra**: solving systems of equations, working with vectors and matrices, determinants, eigenvalues and orthogonality. You need algebra from the Math book (Parts 1-5). Calculus isn't needed. Each chapter notes the matching chapter of the free textbook *Linear Algebra with Applications* by W. Keith Nicholson (Lyryx), if you want to read more.

### How a lesson works

Every lesson gives you **a rule**, **a worked example** and then **practice problems**.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**, or click an option. The box turns **green** when you're right and **red** when you're not.
- Stuck? Click **Hint**. Missed it twice? The worked **Solution** opens for you.
- Problems marked with a star are **creative**: they need more thinking.

### Typing answers

- Most questions ask for **one number**: an entry of a matrix, one component of a vector, a determinant. Type it as `-3`, `0.5` or `2/3`.
- Roots are fine: `sqrt(14)` or `√14`.
- "Entry (2, 1)" means **row 2, column 1**.
- For several numbers in any order (like all the eigenvalues), separate them with commas: `2, -3`.
